## Summary of the two flagged-as-likely epics

**EPIC-11 (Agentic Operations Layer):** Real, substantial code exists for 5 of the 10 stories (S-316 partial, S-317, S-318 partial, S-319 partial, S-320) — considerably more than the sheet's "1/10" suggests. The gate/router (S-316/HRMS-1101) is real but architecturally different from the "master loop" the summary describes (no scheduler, no full-system-state scan). Critically, the router's own module docstring is now **stale**: it claims agents HRMS-1102–1108 "do not exist in this codebase yet," but HRMS-1102 (S-317), HRMS-1103 (S-318), HRMS-1104 (S-319), and HRMS-1105 (S-320) were evidently built in a later pass and do exist. Conversely, HRMS-1106/1107/1108/1109/1110 (S-321–S-325) are genuinely NOT built, confirmed independently by two other files' own contemporaneous docstrings (`org_health_service.py`, `error_log.py`) admitting the gap.

**EPIC-12 (Analytics & Executive Dashboards):** The sheet's "0/10" undersells real adjacent work but the specific EPIC-12 stories are still mostly not satisfied. Real executive/BU-level dashboards exist in this codebase (Executive Revenue Dashboard S-241/HRMS-0212, BU P&L Engine, Candidate Risk Dashboard S-063, Employee Utilization/Bench Cost S-254/S-255) — but on inspection, every one of them turned out to be a **different, already-numbered story from an earlier epic** that happens to closely resemble an EPIC-12 story by name (e.g., "Executive Revenue Dashboard" vs "CXO Revenue Dashboard"; "Candidate Risk Dashboard" vs "CXO Risk Heatmap"). None of them fully satisfies the EPIC-12 story's specific asks (multi-currency, continental breakdown, BU/role/continent grouping, combined cross-domain risk). Net: 3 PARTIAL (S-326, S-331, S-332), 7 NOT-DONE. Zero CONFIRMED-DONE in EPIC-12.
