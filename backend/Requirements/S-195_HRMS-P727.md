## Why — Business Rationale

- A client's own finance or compliance team occasionally needs a record of what was said and when — a rejection reason from six months ago, a timesheet dispute's resolution. Without a searchable archive, that history exists only scattered across whichever individual thread it originated in.

## What — Scope

- A searchable archive of all historical client communications — messages, submission comments, timesheet rejection notes, RFP updates — searchable by keyword, date, and topic, with PDF export for audit purposes, visible in full to the Client Admin role.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-193, S-184, and S-190 must be live and producing the communication records this story indexes.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the unified communication index

- Aggregates messages (S-193), timesheet rejection notes (S-184), and RFP status updates (S-190) into one searchable index, scoped to the client's own company.

## Step 2: Build the search UI

- Keyword, date range, and topic filters.

## Step 3: Build PDF export

- For a filtered result set, generate a PDF suitable for the client's own audit needs.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0727-01 — Full archive visibility is Client Admin only

- A regular client user sees their own communications in context; the cross-communication-type searchable archive with export is scoped to the Client Admin role, matching Section 12.

- Field: Archive search endpoint checks Client Admin role before returning results beyond the requesting user's own threads

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: A keyword search returns matching results across all three communication source types.

- AC-2: A non-Client-Admin user cannot access the cross-thread archive search, only their own individual threads.

- AC-3: PDF export accurately reflects the currently applied filter set.
