## Why — Business Rationale

- A Sent invoice that sits unpaid for weeks is a cash-flow risk nobody notices until someone happens to check — and since payment confirmation here is manual (per S-389), there's a real risk of a paid invoice just not getting marked promptly, or a genuinely unpaid one going unchased. This agent keeps gentle, escalating pressure on outstanding receivables the same way S-387 keeps pressure on timesheets.

## What — Scope

- A scheduled agent that tracks days-since-Sent on every invoice not yet marked Paid, and sends escalating follow-up reminders — to the client at moderate delay, to the internal account manager and Finance at longer delay — without ever auto-sending anything a human hasn't already approved the tone of.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0907 and HRMS-1603 must be live — this agent tracks invoices through exactly those two stories' status lifecycle.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the days-since-Sent tracker

- Daily scan of all Sent-status invoices, computing days elapsed since the Sent transition.

## Step 2: Build the escalation thresholds

- 15 days: LLM-composed, gentle client reminder, queued for account manager review before send.

- 30 days: firmer client reminder, same review-before-send gate, plus an internal notification to the account manager and Finance.

- 45+ days: internal escalation to the BU Head, in addition to continued client follow-up — matches the same principle as S-387's cascade, making a stalled situation visible to increasingly senior people rather than staying invisible.

## Step 3: Build the review-before-send queue

- Every LLM-composed client message lands in the account manager's queue for approval or edit before it actually sends — the agent drafts, a human approves, matching the platform's established pattern of LLM output being advisory for anything client-facing.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-1604-01 — No client-facing message sends without account manager review and approval

- Matches the platform-wide principle already established for other client-facing agent actions — an LLM-composed AR follow-up is a draft for a human to approve, not an autonomous send.

- Field: ar_follow_up_messages.status=PENDING_REVIEW until an explicit account-manager approval action

BR: BR-1604-02 — 45+ days escalates internally to the BU Head, in addition to continued client follow-up

- A receivable this overdue is a business risk senior leadership should see, not just something the account manager quietly keeps chasing.

- Field: days_outstanding >= 45 triggers an HRMS-0113 notification to bu_head_id, separate from and in addition to the client-facing follow-up

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: An invoice reaching 15 days outstanding produces a draft client reminder queued for account-manager review, not an auto-sent message.

- AC-2: An invoice reaching 45 days outstanding notifies the BU Head, in addition to continued client-facing follow-up.

- AC-3: An LLM composition failure falls back to a simple templated reminder rather than silently skipping that cycle's follow-up.

## Not In Scope

- No automatic client-facing send without human review, at any escalation threshold.

- No automatic late-payment penalty or interest calculation in this story — that would be a separate, explicitly-scoped story if the business ever wants it.
