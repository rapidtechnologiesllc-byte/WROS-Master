## Why — Business Rationale

- Every downstream financial calculation in this platform — margin indicators, min bill rate, hiring affordability — ultimately depends on knowing what an employee actually costs to keep on payroll, fully loaded. Your locked formula for this (Section 4 of the Operating Model reference) is genuinely different for India versus US employees, with real components — ESI/PF, GW certification amortization, laptop CapEx, admin charges — that a generic 'salary plus overhead percentage' approximation would get wrong in ways that compound into every rate decision built on top of it.

## What — Scope

- A calculation engine implementing your two locked formulas exactly — the India (BXIN) fully-loaded cost formula and the US (BXUS) fully-loaded cost formula — as configurable-constant engines, not hardcoded numbers, since four of the input constants (India hourly basis, US hourly basis, 401k match %, and the ESI eligibility cap) are still open questions pending Avinash's confirmation.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-1308 Payroll System Sync must be live — this engine reads actual compensation figures from the same source that story already syncs to payroll, not a separately-maintained copy.

- **Placeholder constants required before this engine produces real numbers** — see Business Rules below. The engine itself can and should be fully built now; only the four numeric constants are pending.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the India (BXIN) fully-loaded cost formula

- Annual Pay Rate + (Annual Pay ÷ 12, the PTO component) + Joining Bonus (amortized if multi-year) + ESI (conditional on the ₹21,000/month gross cap — see ESI rule below) + PF (manual entry per your direction) + Health Insurance (₹27,270 fixed) + Life Insurance (₹15,000 fixed) + Admin Charges (25% of annual pay) + Payroll Cost (₹7,200) + GW Certification (₹187,000 standard allocation, always applied regardless of actual certification status per your explicit rule) + Laptop CapEx (₹170,000, USD $2,000 × FX) + Bonus (10% of annual pay).

- Divide by 12 for monthly, then by the configured India billable-hours-per-month constant (placeholder — pending 1,920 vs 1,680 basis confirmation) for hourly, then by the configured FX rate for USD.

## Step 2: Build the ESI eligibility check

- ESI only applies if gross monthly salary ≤ ₹21,000 — Employee ESI = 0.75% of gross if eligible (else 0), Employer ESI = 3.25% of gross if eligible (else 0).

- Given your note that most BlitzenX India roles exceed this cap, expect ESI = ₹0 for the large majority of employees — the check still runs for every employee, it simply resolves to zero for most.

## Step 3: Build the US (BXUS) fully-loaded cost formula

- For FTE-with-Benefits: Annual Base Pay + Payroll Burden (9.7% of base) + Health Insurance (51% of $459/month × 12) + Gusto ($50/month × 12) + 401k Match (percentage — placeholder, pending confirmation).

- For 1099 FTE and 1099 Contractor: no benefits, no payroll burden — cost is simply the annual pay figure plus Gusto.

- Divide by the configured US billable-hours-per-year constant (placeholder — pending 1,760 vs 1,848 basis confirmation) for hourly.

## Step 4: Build the recalculation trigger

- Recalculates whenever the underlying compensation, benefits enrollment, or FX rate changes — never a stale, one-time-computed figure, matching the recalculate-on-change discipline already established elsewhere in this platform (e.g., S-240's revenue potential recalculation).

## Business Rules

BR: BR-1608-01 — Four input constants are configurable placeholders, not hardcoded, pending Avinash's confirmation

- India billable-hours-per-month/year basis, US billable-hours-per-year basis, US 401k match percentage, and — noting this affects HRMS-1610, not this story directly — the Min Bill Rate multiplier are all stored in system_config (HRMS-0115) rather than hardcoded, exactly so the real numbers can be plugged in without a code change once confirmed.

- Field: india_billable_hours_basis, us_billable_hours_basis, us_401k_match_pct stored in system_config with clearly labeled placeholder default values, and every calculation using them visibly flags itself as placeholder-based per the Calculation Basis Indicator field

BR: BR-1608-02 — GW Certification is always ₹187,000 in this calculation, regardless of actual certification status

- Per your explicit rule: the Expense Ledger tracks actual certification usage separately for analytics purposes, but the Roster/cost calculation always loads the full standard allocation — these are deliberately not the same number, and this engine must never conflate them.

- Field: gw_certification_component is a fixed constant in this formula, never conditionally reduced based on actual certification completion

BR: BR-1608-03 — ESI applies only below the ₹21,000 gross-monthly cap, computed per employee, not assumed

- Even though most employees are expected to exceed this cap and resolve to ₹0, the eligibility check runs for every employee rather than assuming a blanket ₹0 — a future lower-salaried role should correctly receive ESI without needing a manual exception.

- Field: esi_employee_pct and esi_employer_pct computed via the IF(gross<=21000, ...) conditional for every employee, every calculation
