## Why — Business Rationale

- Reactive hiring — waiting for a partner to raise a demand before starting sourcing — always results in gap periods where Core-ready talent is not available when needed. The Predictive Demand ML Engine reads partner historical cadence, pipeline opportunities, seasonal patterns, and contract end dates to predict demand before it is formally raised. This gives the BU Head a 30-60 day head start on sourcing.

## What — Scope

- Build the Predictive Demand ML Engine: learns from historical demand patterns per partner, seasonal cycles, opportunity pipeline conversion rates, and contract end dates. Outputs 30/60/90 day demand predictions by role with confidence scores. Feeds BU Dashboard and demand planning.

## Before — Prerequisites

- Minimum 6 months of: demand history, partner_intent_profiles (HRMS-0527), opportunity pipeline data (HRMS-0305), analytics_fact_hiring_funnel. Contract end dates from employee_allocations.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build demand pattern learner

- DemandPatternLearner runs weekly. Learns: (1) Per-partner demand cadence — Curtis averages 1.8 demands per month, peaks in Q1 and Q3. (2) Role mix patterns — 80% of Curtis's demands are Senior GW Consultant. (3) Seasonal patterns — industry-wide Guidewire demand peaks in January-February and August. (4) Opportunity-to-demand conversion lag — average 6 weeks from opportunity CREATED to demand raised for this BU.

- Stores learned patterns in demand_ml_patterns table.

## Step 2: Build demand forecaster

- DemandForecaster.forecast(bu_id, horizon_days=90): combines: (a) Partner cadence model: expected demands per partner in next 30/60/90 days based on historical frequency. (b) Pipeline conversion: opportunities with close_probability >= 60% weighted by expected close date + average conversion lag. (c) Contract end supply: employees whose allocations end in the horizon — these free up supply but also may reveal new demand if clients want replacements.

- Output: { forecast_30d: [{ role_type, demand_count, confidence_pct, supply_count, gap }], ... }

## Step 3: Build ML accuracy tracking

- Monthly: compare last month's forecasts against actual demands raised. Compute prediction accuracy per confidence band. Store in forecast_accuracy_metrics.

- BU Dashboard shows: 'Predictions with confidence ≥ 75%: 84% accurate in last 6 months.' This builds BU Head trust in the model.

## Business Rules

## BR: Minimum 6 Months of Data Before Predictions Activate

- DemandForecaster only outputs predictions if demand_ml_patterns has at least 6 months of historical data for this BU. Before that threshold: BU Dashboard shows 'Predictive demand available after 6 months of data. Currently: [N] months.' No predictions from insufficient data.

- Field: 6-month minimum — no predictions before threshold

## BR: Confidence Scores Are Calibrated — Labeled Appropriately

- Confidence scores are calibrated against historical accuracy. A 75% confidence prediction means the model has been correct approximately 75% of the time for similar predictions. BU Dashboard always shows the ML accuracy alongside predictions so BU Head can contextualise confidence scores.

- Field: Confidence calibrated + accuracy history always shown together
