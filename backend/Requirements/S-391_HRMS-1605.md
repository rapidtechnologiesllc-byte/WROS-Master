## Why — Business Rationale

- Every number in the P&L — revenue, expense, the Reserve Fund balance — is only as trustworthy as its agreement with what actually happened in the bank. Without a monthly reconciliation step, WROS's internal ledger and reality can quietly drift apart, and nobody notices until year-end audit finds the gap. This is explicitly a manual-upload process, matching how BlitzenX does it today — WROS doesn't have a live bank feed, and isn't trying to fake one.

## What — Scope

- A monthly reconciliation tool: Finance uploads a bank statement (PDF or Excel), the system attempts to match each bank transaction against WROS's own invoice-paid and expense records, and surfaces exactly what matched automatically versus what needs manual review — never silently assuming a match that wasn't actually confirmed.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0907 and HRMS-1603 must be live and producing the invoice-paid records this process reconciles against.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the statement upload and extraction

- Accept PDF or Excel upload.

- LLM extraction produces a structured transaction list: date, amount, description.

- Reject and retry once on malformed extraction; on second failure, allow Finance to manually key in transactions as a fallback rather than blocking reconciliation entirely.

## Step 2: Build the automatic matching pass

- For each extracted bank transaction, search WROS's invoice-paid records (per S-389's payment_date and amount) and expense records for an amount-and-approximate-date match.

- A confident match (exact amount, date within a small tolerance window) is proposed, not auto-confirmed.

## Step 3: Build the Finance confirmation step

- Finance reviews every proposed match — confirm, or reject and manually search/link instead.

- Unmatched bank transactions and unmatched WROS records are both surfaced explicitly as open items requiring investigation, not hidden until someone thinks to look.

## Step 4: Build the reconciliation completion record

- Once every transaction for the period is either matched-and-confirmed or explicitly logged as an open item with a note, the period is marked Reconciled — this becomes the input the P&L Engine and Reserve Fund calculations trust as verified.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-1605-01 — No match is ever auto-confirmed, regardless of how confident the automatic matching pass is

- This is real money reconciliation — even an exact amount-and-date match must be explicitly confirmed by Finance before being treated as reconciled, since a coincidental amount match (two different transactions of the same value on nearby dates) is a real risk an automated system shouldn't silently resolve.

- Field: invoice_reconciliation_matches.confirmed only set true by explicit Finance action, never by the matching algorithm itself

## BR: BR-1605-02 — A period cannot be marked Reconciled while any item remains unaddressed

- Every bank transaction and every WROS-side paid/expense record for the period must be either matched-and-confirmed or explicitly logged as an open item with a note — silently ignoring a stray unmatched transaction defeats the entire purpose of reconciliation.

- Field: Period-level Reconciled status requires zero unaddressed items, checked before the status can be set

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: No proposed match is treated as reconciled until Finance explicitly confirms it, verified by checking that an unconfirmed match has no effect on any downstream P&L or Reserve figure.

- AC-2: A period cannot be marked Reconciled while any bank transaction or WROS record remains unmatched and unnoted.

- AC-3: A failed statement extraction offers manual transaction entry rather than blocking the reconciliation process entirely.

## Not In Scope

- No live bank feed or API integration — this is explicitly, permanently a manual upload process, per direct instruction.

- No automatic booking of unmatched transactions as new expense records — an unmatched item is flagged for human investigation, never auto-created as a new ledger entry.
