## Why — Business Rationale

- An HTD candidate is being evaluated on a fundamentally different question than a standard hire — not 'can they do Guidewire work today' but 'can they learn it, and will a client trust them once they have.' That needs its own interview structure with its own panel roles, not a repurposed standard interview process that's really asking the wrong question.

## What — Scope

- A two-level internal interview process for HTD candidates — L1 Java technical (Tech Lead), L2 learning aptitude (Technical Manager) — with the final go/no-go decision resting with the Director, and explicitly no client involvement until after training completes.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-258 must be producing scored HTD candidates.

- HRMS-P606 base interview panel assignment infrastructure must be live.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the L1 Java technical interview

- Assigned to a Tech Lead, standard technical assessment scoped to Java depth (not Guidewire) per HRMS-0248's existing panel assignment pattern.

## Step 2: Enforce L1-before-L2 per R-05

- L2 cannot be scheduled until L1 outcome=PASS is logged — no exception for HTD, same absolute rule as every other interview pipeline.

## Step 3: Build the L2 learning aptitude interview

- Assigned to a Technical Manager, scoped to learning aptitude and adaptability assessment rather than domain knowledge.

## Step 4: Build the Director decision step

- Final go/no-go rests with the Director, informed by both interview outcomes and S-258's HTD_readiness score.

## Step 5: Enforce no client involvement

- No client contact, submission, or visibility exists anywhere in this process — the candidate is purely internal until training completes and they reach deployment readiness (S-264).

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-P505-01 — L1 must pass before L2 can be scheduled, no HTD exception

- R-05 is an absolute platform rule with no override authority — HTD candidates get exactly the same L1-before-L2 gating as any other interview pipeline.

- Field: L2 interview creation blocked at the API level unless L1 outcome=PASS exists for that candidate

## BR: BR-P505-02 — No client involvement of any kind before training completion

- An HTD candidate hasn't demonstrated Guidewire capability yet — presenting them to a client before training completes would misrepresent their readiness.

- Field: No submission, client-visible profile, or client-facing record exists for a candidate until htd_phase reaches deployment readiness

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: An attempt to schedule L2 before L1=PASS is logged is rejected.

- AC-2: No client-facing record of any kind exists for an HTD candidate before they reach deployment readiness.

- AC-3: The Director's final decision is logged and gates entry into the HTD training program.
