## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

BlitzenX spends LinkedIn Premium credits and recruiter time on LinkedIn sourcing. Do these investments produce placed consultants? Which search queries produce the most conversions? What is the LinkedIn cost-per-qualified-candidate? These answers determine whether to expand or reduce LinkedIn sourcing.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build LinkedIn Sourcing Analytics at /recruiter/sourcing/analytics. Metrics: profiles sourced per week, InMail send/reply rates, conversion rates at each stage, top performing search queries, estimated cost per qualified candidate.

- Route: /recruiter/sourcing/analytics

## BR-01: Placed Rate Uses Actual Placement Data

placed_rate_pct = COUNT(LinkedIn-sourced candidates who completed onboarding) / COUNT(all LinkedIn-sourced candidates). Requires cross-referencing candidates.source=LINKEDIN with COMPLETED conversation status.

↳ Triggered by field: placed_rate_pct definition
