## Why — Business Rationale

- Revenue doesn't only fail to materialize because a deal is lost — it leaks quietly through stalled opportunities nobody's chasing, demand that stays unfilled past when the client needed it, time that was worked but never billed, and sub-vendor costs that ran over budget. None of those show up as a 'lost deal' anywhere, which is exactly why they need their own detection layer.

## What — Scope

- A detection engine flagging four leakage patterns — stalled opportunities (no stage movement past a threshold), unfilled demand past the client's need date, unbilled tracked time, and sub-vendor cost overruns — surfaced as an actionable list, not just a dollar figure.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-238 must be live for the stalled-opportunity check.

- HRMS-0605 Timesheet data must be queryable for the unbilled-time check.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the stalled opportunity check

- Opportunity with no stage change in 30+ days (configurable via HRMS-0115) flagged.

## Step 2: Build the unfilled demand check

- Demand past its need_date with status still OPEN flagged, cross-referenced against sourcing agent activity.

## Step 3: Build the unbilled time check

- Approved timesheet hours with no corresponding invoice line item after a configurable grace period flagged.

## Step 4: Build the sub-vendor cost overrun check

- Actual sub-vendor placement cost exceeding the originally quoted/budgeted amount flagged.

## Step 5: Build the unified leakage list

- All four types surfaced in one prioritized list, sortable by estimated dollar impact, each with a direct link to the underlying record.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0214-01 — Every leakage flag links directly to its underlying record

- A leakage list that just says 'there's a problem somewhere' isn't actionable — every row must click through to the specific opportunity, demand, timesheet, or sub-vendor placement causing it.

- Field: Each leakage_events row carries a foreign key to its source entity, not just a description

## BR: BR-0214-02 — Thresholds are configurable, not hardcoded

- 30-day stall, grace period for unbilled time, and similar thresholds live in HRMS-0115 system config so they can be tuned without a code deploy.

- Field: All threshold values referenced from system_config, none hardcoded in the detection logic

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: An opportunity with no stage change in 30+ days appears in the leakage list within one daily scan cycle.

- AC-2: Every leakage list entry links directly to its underlying source record.

- AC-3: Adjusting the stall threshold in system config changes detection behavior without a code deploy.
