## Why — Business Rationale

- Every downstream metric about sub-vendor performance — contribution to placements, cost comparison against direct sourcing, per-vendor scorecards — depends on knowing which candidates came through which vendor. Without a reliable, consistently-applied tag at the moment of creation, that analytics layer would be reconstructing sourcing history after the fact, unreliably.

## What — Scope

- A source_channel=SUBVENDOR tag plus vendor_id applied to every candidate created via createCandidateSafe() from the sub-vendor pipeline — visible internally for analytics, never visible to the candidate or the client.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-141 HRMS-P805 must be feeding accepted submissions into createCandidateSafe() (via S-144) for this tag to have a moment to attach.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Add source_channel and vendor_id fields

- Extend the candidate creation payload from S-144's Accept action to include source_channel=SUBVENDOR and vendor_id.

## Step 2: Enforce tag immutability

- Once set at creation, source_channel and vendor_id are never edited — sourcing history is a fact about how the candidate entered the system, not something that changes later.

## Step 3: Restrict visibility

- Internal BlitzenX roles (Recruiter, RM, Admin) can see the tag on the candidate record.

- Never rendered on any client-facing submission or candidate profile view.

- Never referenced in any Thunder message to the candidate.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0816-01 — source_channel and vendor_id are immutable once set

- Sourcing history is a factual record, not an editable field — no role can change it after candidate creation.

- Field: No UPDATE path exists for source_channel or vendor_id on the candidates table after INSERT

## BR: BR-0816-02 — Tag is never visible to the candidate or any client-facing view

- Matches the design principle that the candidate's experience with a vendor-sourced placement is identical to a direct one — the tag exists purely for internal analytics.

- Field: Client portal and candidate-facing API responses explicitly exclude source_channel and vendor_id fields

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: Every candidate created via S-144's Accept action has source_channel=SUBVENDOR and a populated vendor_id.

- AC-2: No API path allows editing source_channel or vendor_id after candidate creation.

- AC-3: The tag never appears in any client-facing or candidate-facing view or message.
