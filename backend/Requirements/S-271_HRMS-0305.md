## Why — Business Rationale

- Every opportunity in the pipeline represents potential future staffing need. If a $300k deal closes, BlitzenX needs people ready to deliver. Without converting pipeline opportunities to workforce demand signals, the recruiting function is always reactive — scrambling to hire after the deal closes rather than having people ready.

## What — Scope

- Build automatic conversion of opportunity pipeline into workforce demand signals. When opportunity reaches a configured probability threshold or stage, create demand items in the workforce planning system so recruiting can start preparing.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0207 opportunities table. HRMS-0210 role_demands per opportunity. HRMS-0303 demand plan infrastructure.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build OpportunityDemandConverter service

- OpportunityDemandConverter runs on: (1) opportunity stage change, (2) close_probability change crossing threshold, (3) nightly job for any missed events.

- For opportunities where close_probability >= system_config.opportunity_demand_trigger_pct (default 60%): for each role_demand on the opportunity: create or update a demand record in demands table with source=OPPORTUNITY, opportunity_id, status=PIPELINE, close_probability.

- On probability drop below threshold: set demand status=PIPELINE_HOLD. On opportunity LOST: set demand status=CANCELLED. On opportunity WON: set demand status=OPEN (active hiring need).

## Step 2: Build opportunity demand visibility in workforce planning

- Demand forecast (HRMS-0304) and gap analysis (HRMS-0306) read PIPELINE demands weighted by close_probability.

- RM Dashboard (HRMS-P203) shows a 'Pipeline Demand' section: opportunities expected to close, their role demands, probability, expected start date.

- Alert: when opportunity win probability changes >20% in either direction: notify RM via portal notification.

## Business Rules

## BR: Opportunity Demand Is a Signal — Not a Hard Commitment

- PIPELINE demands from opportunity conversion do not trigger hiring or job reqs automatically. They appear in forecasts and gap analysis as planning signals only. Hard demand (requires active hiring) only created when opportunity status changes to WON.

- Field: PIPELINE demand = planning signal only. WON = active demand.

## BR: Trigger Threshold Configurable per Tenant

- Default 60% close_probability threshold. Admin can adjust per tenant in system_config. Some BUs may want earlier signals (40%) for long-lead roles; others prefer to wait until 80%. Configurable.

- Field: system_config.opportunity_demand_trigger_pct (default 60%)

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: Opportunity crossing 60% threshold creates PIPELINE demand items

- AC-2: Probability drop below threshold sets demand to PIPELINE_HOLD

- AC-3: Opportunity WON converts PIPELINE demand to OPEN (active)

- AC-4: Opportunity LOST cancels demand

- AC-5: RM Dashboard shows pipeline demand section
