## Why — Business Rationale

- An account manager overseeing a client relationship needs to see, at a glance, whether the revenue planned for that engagement is actually being realized as billable work — earned vs. planned, how much of the team's time is billable at all, and how fast revenue is being consumed against a fixed-bid budget. This is the internal, BlitzenX-side counterpart to the leakage detection in S-225.

## What — Scope

- An internal dashboard per client showing earned vs. planned revenue, billable-to-non-billable ratio, and revenue burn rate — for account managers and BU Heads, not the client-facing S-197 analytics.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-225 and S-226 must be live for realized revenue and leakage data.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the earned-vs-planned view

- Per client, sum of invoiced revenue against the originally planned project revenue (from S-238/S-240's opportunity-to-project figures).

## Step 2: Build the billable ratio

- billable_hours / total_hours across all employees allocated to that client.

## Step 3: Build the burn rate indicator

- For fixed-bid engagements: rate at which planned revenue/budget is being consumed against elapsed project time.
