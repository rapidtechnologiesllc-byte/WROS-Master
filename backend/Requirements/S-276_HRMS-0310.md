## Why — Business Rationale

- The demand forecast and gap analysis APIs produce data. This story builds the operational dashboard that RM, Director, and BU Head use in weekly staffing reviews. It consolidates 30/60/90d forecast, gap analysis, sourcing status, and plan vs actuals into one view.

## What — Scope

- Build the Demand Forecast Dashboard at /admin/workforce-planning. Combines: forecast heat grid, gap analysis table, sourcing pipeline status, plan vs actuals, and open demands list.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0304 forecast API. HRMS-0306 gap analysis API. HRMS-0307 sourcing workflow log.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build Demand Forecast Dashboard UI

- Route: /admin/workforce-planning/dashboard. Auth: Admin, Director, RM, BU Head.

- Section 1 — Forecast Heat Grid: 30/60/90d view per role per engine. Color-coded gaps.

- Section 2 — Gap Analysis Table: supply vs demand breakdown per role. Export PDF.

- Section 3 — Sourcing Pipeline: per open demand, sourcing channel status (LinkedIn: N profiles, Sub-vendor: N responses, HTD: N in training, Bench: N candidates notified).

- Section 4 — Plan vs Actuals: approved plan headcount vs actual hires YTD. Variance per role.

- BU filter: default to user's BU. Admin/Director can select any BU.
