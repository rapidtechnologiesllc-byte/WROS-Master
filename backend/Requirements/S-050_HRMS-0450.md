## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

No-show rates for interviews drop significantly when candidates receive timely reminders. A reminder 24 hours before and another 1 hour before gives the candidate two chances to remember, reschedule if needed, or confirm they are prepared. Thunder handles all of this automatically — the recruiter never needs to manually chase candidates before interviews.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build: (1) interview_reminders table tracking scheduled reminders. (2) ReminderSchedulingService that creates two reminders (24h and 1h before) when interview.confirmed fires. (3) ReminderExecutionJob that sends the reminders at the right time in the candidate's local timezone.

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Create interview_reminders table

- id UUID PK, tenant_id UUID, interview_id UUID FK→interviews.id, candidate_id UUID, reminder_type ENUM('24H_BEFORE','1H_BEFORE'), scheduled_at TIMESTAMP NOT NULL, status ENUM('PENDING','SENT','CANCELLED'), sent_at TIMESTAMP nullable

## Step 2: Build ReminderSchedulingService

- Listens for interview.confirmed event

- Schedule 24H reminder: INSERT reminder_type='24H_BEFORE', scheduled_at = interviews.scheduled_at_utc - 24 hours

- Schedule 1H reminder: INSERT reminder_type='1H_BEFORE', scheduled_at = interviews.scheduled_at_utc - 1 hour

- If interview is less than 25 hours away: skip 24H reminder. Only send 1H reminder.

- If interview is less than 70 minutes away: skip both reminders (too late to be useful).

## Step 3: Build ReminderExecutionJob (runs every 10 minutes)

- SELECT * FROM interview_reminders WHERE status='PENDING' AND scheduled_at <= NOW()

- For each pending reminder: load interview record. Check interview.status still CONFIRMED (not rescheduled/cancelled).

- If interview cancelled/rescheduled: UPDATE reminder status=CANCELLED, skip.

- Generate reminder message via INTERVIEW_REMINDER prompt:

- 24H: 'Hi [name]! Just a friendly reminder — your interview with [Interviewer Name] at BlitzenX is tomorrow at [time in candidate timezone]. Please let me know if you need to reschedule.'

- 1H: 'Hi [name]! Your BlitzenX interview is in 1 hour — at [time in candidate timezone]. [Meeting link]. Good luck! 🤞'

- Send via BOTH WhatsApp AND Email (same logic as confirmation)

- UPDATE interview_reminders status=SENT, sent_at=NOW()

## Step 4: Cancel reminders when interview is rescheduled or cancelled

- When interview.status changes to RESCHEDULED or CANCELLED: UPDATE interview_reminders SET status=CANCELLED WHERE interview_id=X AND status='PENDING'

- Wire this to the reschedule workflow (HRMS-0451) and any cancellation flows

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: All Reminder Times Shown in Candidate's Timezone

The time in the reminder message is always in the candidate_timezone stored on the interview record. Never show UTC or interviewer timezone to the candidate.

↳ Triggered by field: candidate_timezone

## BR-02: Send Via Both Channels — Same as Confirmation

Reminders sent via both WhatsApp and Email. Missing an interview because a reminder went to only one channel is unacceptable. Double-channel for all interview-related messages.

↳ Triggered by field: Send via both channels

## BR-03: Do Not Send Reminder If Interview Status is Not CONFIRMED

Before sending: verify interview.status=CONFIRMED. If RESCHEDULED or CANCELLED: mark reminder CANCELLED and skip. Never remind candidates about interviews that have changed.

↳ Triggered by field: interviews.status check before send
