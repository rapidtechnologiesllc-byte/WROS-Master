## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

Raw desire signals (50+ records per candidate) are not usable by Thunder or HR directly. The Desire Profile aggregates all signals into a clear, ranked picture: 'This candidate's top desire is Career Growth (0.91), followed by Remote Flexibility (0.78). They fear Instability (0.65). Their speed of decision is HIGH — they have a competing offer.'

This profile is the single most important context item for Thunder's motivation strategy and for the HR team's personal outreach. It tells them exactly what to lead with and what to avoid.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build: (1) candidate_desire_profiles table — aggregated, ranked desire model per candidate. (2) buildDesireProfile() — aggregates all signals into the desire model. (3) Narrative summary generator — LLM writes a 3-paragraph human-readable profile for HR. (4) Continuous update as new signals arrive.

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Create candidate_desire_profiles table

- CREATE TABLE candidate_desire_profiles: id UUID PK, tenant_id UUID, candidate_id UUID UNIQUE FK→candidates.id, top_desire_category VARCHAR(100), top_desire_score DECIMAL(3,2), desire_ranking JSONB (array of {category, score, signal_count, direction} sorted by score DESC), primary_fear VARCHAR(100), primary_fear_score DECIMAL(3,2), engagement_level ENUM('HOT','WARM','COOL','COLD') computed from response_speed signals, has_competing_offer BOOLEAN default false, decision_urgency ENUM('URGENT','NORMAL','SLOW'), narrative_summary TEXT (LLM-generated 3-paragraph profile for HR), narrative_updated_at TIMESTAMP, profile_updated_at TIMESTAMP

## Step 2: Build buildDesireProfile() aggregation function

- buildDesireProfile(candidate_id, tenant_id)

- Load all processed candidate_desire_signals (processed=true) for this candidate

- For each desire_category: aggregate score = WEIGHTED_AVG(desire_strength) weighted by recency (recent signals count more: signals from last 7 days * 1.5, last 30 days * 1.0, older * 0.5) and by direction (TOWARDS signals are positive scores, AWAY_FROM signals contribute to 'fears')

- Sort categories by aggregated score DESC → desire_ranking array

- top_desire_category = first in ranking. primary_fear = highest AWAY_FROM category.

- engagement_level: compute from response_speed signals. Avg response < 2h = HOT. 2-12h = WARM. 12-48h = COOL. >48h = COLD.

- has_competing_offer: true if any signal contains keywords: 'other offer', 'another company', 'counter offer', 'considering other options'.

- decision_urgency: based on chat/message frequency and response speed trend. Increasing speed = URGENT. Decreasing = SLOW.

- UPSERT candidate_desire_profiles. Set profile_updated_at=NOW().

## Step 3: Build LLM narrative summary generator

- generateDesireNarrative(candidate_id, tenant_id) — called after buildDesireProfile()

- Build narrative prompt: 'You are a senior talent psychologist. Based on the desire profile below, write a 3-paragraph candidate motivation briefing for the HR team. Paragraph 1: What this candidate wants most and WHY they would say yes to BlitzenX. Paragraph 2: What this candidate fears or is uncertain about — and how to address it. Paragraph 3: Your recommended approach for the HR team to close this candidate — specific actions, specific talking points, specific timing. Desire Profile: {desire_ranking}. Engagement Level: {engagement_level}. Primary Fear: {primary_fear}. Has competing offer: {has_competing_offer}. Candidate memory: {memory_summary}. Be specific, actionable, and write as if briefing a senior recruiter.'

- UPDATE candidate_desire_profiles.narrative_summary = LLM output, narrative_updated_at=NOW()

## Step 4: Build DesireProfileUpdateJob

- Runs every 4 hours. For all candidates with unprocessed signals since last profile update: call buildDesireProfile() + generateDesireNarrative().

- Publish event: candidate.desire_profile_updated { candidate_id, top_desire_category, engagement_level } → consumed by HRMS-P119 Proactive Motivation Engine.

- If top_desire_category changed from previous: publish candidate.desire_shift_detected — important signal for Thunder to adapt its approach.

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Recency Weighting Is Critical

Signals from the last 7 days are 1.5x weighted vs older signals. A candidate's desires change. If they initially wanted salary but after 3 conversations they are asking about growth: the profile must reflect the current state, not the historical average.

↳ Triggered by field: Recency weighting in aggregation

## BR-02: Competing Offer Detection Triggers Urgency Flag

If has_competing_offer=true: decision_urgency is automatically set to URGENT regardless of response speed signals. A candidate with a competing offer must be prioritized immediately. Publish candidate.competing_offer_detected event.

↳ Triggered by field: has_competing_offer=true → URGENT always

## BR-03: Profile Updates Within 4 Hours of New Signals

The DesireProfileUpdateJob must run every 4 hours and process all new signals. A desire profile that is 24 hours stale while the candidate is actively chatting in the portal is useless for real-time motivation.

↳ Triggered by field: 4-hour max staleness
