## Why — Business Rationale

- An opportunity is a sales-side concept — a deal being pursued. The moment BlitzenX starts thinking about who would actually staff it, that's a different kind of record: a role demand with skills, quantity, and a bill rate. Without an explicit link from opportunity to role demand, the sales pipeline and the staffing pipeline stay disconnected, and RMs find out about upcoming demand too late.

## What — Scope

- The ability to create one or more role demands directly from an opportunity — role, required skills, quantity, duration, and bill rate in the client's currency — feeding into the standard Workforce Planning demand pipeline.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-236 must be live — role demands originate from an existing opportunity.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the role demand creation form

- Linked to a parent opportunity_id, role title, required skills (multi-select), quantity, duration, bill rate in client currency.

## Step 2: Wire into the standard demand pipeline

- Created role demands appear in the same demands table and downstream sourcing/allocation flows as any other demand — no parallel opportunity-originated demand pipeline.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-0210-01 — Opportunity-originated demands use the same demands table as any other demand

- No separate, lighter-weight demand type exists for opportunity-sourced roles — they get the same sourcing, bench-first, and allocation treatment as any demand in the platform.

- Field: demands.source_type=OPPORTUNITY is a tag, not a schema branch

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: A role demand created from an opportunity appears in the standard demands pipeline identically to any other demand.

- AC-2: Multiple role demands can be created from a single opportunity (e.g., a deal requiring 3 PC developers and 1 QA).
