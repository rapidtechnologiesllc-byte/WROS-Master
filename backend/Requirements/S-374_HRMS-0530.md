## Why — Business Rationale

- The BU Head is accountable for everything: talent quality, delivery health, Core pool size, HTD conversion, revenue trajectory, and client relationships. Today this accountability has no single dashboard — the BU Head pieces together information from emails, spreadsheets, and ad-hoc RM updates. The Workforce Command Centre is the single screen that shows the BU Head everything they need to run their BU intelligently — real-time workforce status, individual employee scores, predictive demand, and alerts requiring action today.

## What — Scope

- Build the BU Head ML Dashboard: real-time workforce status section, individual employee scorecard grid, predictive demand ML section with confidence-weighted forecasts, and an actions-required panel showing everything that needs BU Head decision today.

## Before — Prerequisites

- All HRMS-0515 through HRMS-0529 complete. HRMS-0532 Predictive Demand ML running. HRMS-1201 Analytics Warehouse.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build workforce status section

- Real-time count cards (refresh every 5 minutes): Total Headcount | In Buddy Program | Specialty Deployed | Core-Certified in Specialty | Bench (available now) | Core Deployed | HTD Track.

- Sub-indicators: Buddy Program graduates this week, contracts ending < 30 days, Specialty pool vs 40 minimum.

## Step 2: Build individual employee scorecard grid

- Table: one row per employee. Columns: Name | Status | Core Certified | Buddy Score | Specialty Days | 35-KPI Avg | Peer Trust | Escalations | Timesheet % | Next Action.

- Sortable by any column. Filter by status, delivery engine, certification status.

- Color coding: green=strong performer, amber=watch, red=concern. Auto-coloured by composite KPI score.

- Row click: expands to full employee performance profile in slide-out panel.

## Step 3: Build actions-required panel

- Pulls all items requiring BU Head decision today: pending Core eligibility approvals, Buddy Program graduation gates overdue, Core-Pull replacement plans needed, HTD quarterly checkpoints overdue, Specialty pool below 40, manager weekly input gaps (3+ consecutive misses).

- Each item: description, days overdue, [Action] button linking to relevant workflow.

## Step 4: Build predictive demand section

- Reads HRMS-0532 Predictive Demand ML output. Shows: demand predictions for next 30/60/90 days by role with confidence %. Current supply (bench + near-available). Predicted gap. ML recommendation: 'Start sourcing 2 Senior GW Consultants now — expected demand in 45 days at 78% confidence.'

- Historical ML accuracy shown: '82% of predictions within this confidence range were accurate in last 6 months.'

## Business Rules

## BR: Actions Required Panel Is Sorted by Business Impact — Not Date

- Items in the actions panel are sorted by business impact score (Core eligibility delays block Curtis from fulfilling client demand; Buddy graduation delays leave an employee in limbo; pool guard breaches risk new demand ability) — not simply by how long they have been pending. BU Head always sees the highest-impact item first.

- Field: Business impact sort — not date sort

## BR: Dashboard Accessible to BU Head and Director Only — No RM Self-Service

- The BU Head dashboard shows individual performance scores, predictive ML output, and decision queues. RM can see their own demand queue but not the full employee scorecard grid with KPI scores. Director can see all BUs.

- Field: BU Head + Director only. RM excluded from scorecard grid.
