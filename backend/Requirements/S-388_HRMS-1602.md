## Why — Business Rationale

- S-226 already built the invoice document itself — line items, PDF export, status lifecycle. What's missing is the actual monthly clock: invoices for a month's approved work need to generate on a fixed schedule (the 2nd of the following month, per your accrual-basis fiscal timing) and route to Accounting for review before anything reaches a client, every month, without someone remembering to kick it off manually.

## What — Scope

- A scheduled job that, on the 2nd of every month, generates draft invoices per client for the prior month's approved billable hours (accrual basis — April work invoiced in May, exactly as your fiscal model specifies), routes each to the Accounting team for review and approval, and only then makes it available to actually send to the client via S-226's existing Sent-status flow.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0907 (S-226) must be live — this story triggers it, it doesn't duplicate its line-item or PDF logic.

- HRMS-0605 must be enforcing R-10 (unapproved timesheets block generation) exactly as already specified.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the 2nd-of-month scheduled trigger

- On the 2nd of each calendar month, identify every client with approved billable hours for the prior calendar month (accrual basis, per your fiscal timing rules) and call S-226's existing invoice generation for each.

## Step 2: Route generated invoices to Accounting review

- Every invoice generated by this cycle lands in Draft status per S-226's existing lifecycle, with an explicit notification to the Accounting team — this story doesn't change S-226's Draft-requires-Finance-approval rule (BR-0907-02), it's simply the mechanism that makes sure that review queue actually fills up on schedule every month rather than someone needing to remember.

## Step 3: Handle partial-month/mid-cycle employee changes

- An employee who started or ended mid-month bills only for their actual approved hours within that month — no special-casing needed since this reads from the same approved-hours source S-226 always has, but worth confirming explicitly since a monthly-cycle trigger is new here.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-1602-01 — This story triggers S-226, it does not reimplement invoice generation

- Avoids a second invoice-generation code path drifting from the original — the monthly cycle is purely a scheduling and routing layer on top of already-built, already-audited logic.

- Field: The 2nd-of-month job calls S-226's existing generation endpoint per client, no independent line-item logic exists in this story

BR: BR-1602-02 — A client with any unresolved timesheet in the period is skipped, not partially invoiced

- Matches R-10 exactly as already enforced by HRMS-0605 — this story surfaces which clients were skipped and why, it does not attempt a workaround or a partial invoice that ignores the unresolved portion.

- Field: Skipped clients appear in the skipped-clients list with the specific blocking timesheet identified, not just a generic failure

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: Invoices generate automatically on the 2nd of every month for all clients with fully-approved prior-month hours.

- AC-2: A client with an unresolved timesheet in the period is skipped and listed with the specific blocking reason, not silently omitted or partially invoiced.

- AC-3: Every generated invoice lands in Draft status and triggers an Accounting notification, exactly matching S-226's existing pre-send review gate.
