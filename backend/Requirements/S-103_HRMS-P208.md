## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

The BU Head needs visibility across ALL hiring decisions within their business unit — not just the ones they are personally involved in. They need to catch quality inconsistencies (HMs who pass everyone vs those who fail everyone), see pipeline health, and have the authority to reverse an HM rejection (override with documented reason).

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build BU Head Oversight at /bu/hiring-oversight. Shows: (1) All hiring decisions in their BU last 30 days. (2) Interview-to-offer conversion by HM. (3) Override capability for HM rejections. (4) Technical score distribution across interviewed candidates.

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Build GET /api/bu/hiring-overview endpoint

- Auth: BU Head role. Filter by tenant_id AND bu_id (BU Head sees only their BU).

- { decisions_summary: { total: N, approved: N, rejected: N, hold: N, conversion_rate_pct }, hm_breakdown: [{ hm_name, approved: N, rejected: N, pass_rate_pct }], recent_decisions: [{ candidate_name, job_title, hm_name, decision, decided_at }] (last 30 days), score_distribution: [{ score_range, count }] }

## Step 2: Build BU Head Oversight page

- Route: /bu/hiring-oversight

- Summary row: 4 metrics — Total Decisions / Approved / Rejected / Conversion Rate %.

- HM Breakdown table: per HM in BU — decisions made, pass rate. Flag HM with pass rate > 90% (possible low standards) or < 30% (possible too strict).

- Recent Decisions table: candidate, job, HM, decision, date. [Override] button on REJECTED rows.

- Score Distribution bar chart: shows how many candidates fell in each score range (0-20, 21-40, 41-60, 61-80, 81-100).

## Step 3: Build HM rejection override capability

- [Override] button on any REJECTED hiring decision: opens modal 'Override Rejection — This will advance the candidate to offer stage bypassing the hiring manager's rejection. This action is logged and requires documented justification. Justification (required, min 150 chars): [textarea]'

- On confirm: INSERT hiring_decision_overrides: { id, original_decision_id, override_by, override_justification, override_at }. UPDATE hiring_decisions to indicate overridden. Publish event hiring.override_applied.

- Log in conversation_audit_log: actor_type=BU_HEAD, event_type=HIRING_DECISION_OVERRIDDEN.

- Notify HM: 'Your rejection of [Candidate Name] for [Job Title] has been overridden by [BU Head Name]. Reason: [justification].'

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Override Requires 150-Character Justification and Is Permanently Audited

BU Head overrides are a significant governance action. Minimum 150 chars justification. Every override logged immutably in conversation_audit_log. HM notified immediately. This is the audit trail for the override exception that HRMS-0453 allows.

↳ Triggered by field: hiring_decision_overrides + audit_log

## BR-02: BU Head Sees Only Their BU

BU Head is scoped to their own business unit. They cannot see other BUs' hiring decisions even within the same tenant.

↳ Triggered by field: bu_id scoping on all queries
