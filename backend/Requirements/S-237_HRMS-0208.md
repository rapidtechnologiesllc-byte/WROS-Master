## Why — Business Rationale

- A BU Head managing 40 open opportunities across 6 stages needs to see the shape of the pipeline at a glance, not scroll a flat list guessing which deals are stuck. A Kanban view with per-column revenue totals turns 'how's the pipeline looking' from a question into something visible the moment the screen loads.

## What — Scope

- A drag-and-drop Kanban board with configurable stage columns, revenue total per column, and filtering by BU and owner.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-236 must be live and producing opportunities with a stage field.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the Kanban board

- Columns from a configurable stage list (stored per HRMS-0115 system config, not hardcoded), cards show client name, revenue value, owner, close date.

## Step 2: Build drag-and-drop stage transitions

- Dragging a card to a new column updates opportunities.stage, logged to Activity Timeline per HRMS-0118.

## Step 3: Build per-column revenue totals

- Sum of revenue_value_usd_cents for all cards in each column, displayed in the column header.

## Step 4: Build BU and owner filters

- Standard filter bar, respects BU context scoping from HRMS-0107.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0208-01 — Stage columns are configurable, not hardcoded

- The same stage list source used by S-236's dropdown drives the Kanban columns, so the two never drift out of sync.

- Field: Both S-236's stage dropdown and this Kanban's columns read from the same system_config key

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: Dragging a card to a new column updates the underlying opportunity's stage and logs the transition.

- AC-2: Column revenue totals accurately sum all cards currently in that column.

- AC-3: Stage columns match whatever is currently configured, not a hardcoded list.
