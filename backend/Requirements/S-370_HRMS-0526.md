## Why — Business Rationale

- HTD employees start with zero Guidewire knowledge. A Java engineer converting to Guidewire consultant needs 365 billable days in Specialty — not 90 — because they are building both technical depth and client delivery confidence from scratch. The 365-day track applies the same certification clock mechanics but with a longer runway and additional milestones checkpoints at 90, 180, 270, and 365 days to track conversion progress.

## What — Scope

- Extend HRMS-0522 certification clock for HTD track employees: clock_type=HTD_365, certified at 365 billable days. Add quarterly milestone checkpoints at 90/180/270 days reviewed by Hemant. AI Assessor weights technical progression KPIs more heavily for HTD candidates.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0522 certification clock. HRMS-0515 performance store. employees.htd_track=TRUE.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build HTD clock configuration

- On employee creation with htd_track=TRUE: create specialty_certification_clocks record with clock_type=HTD_365. Certification threshold = 365 billable days.

- All standard clock mechanics apply (bench pause, RESOURCE_ERROR reset, billable days only) — only the threshold changes.

## Step 2: Build quarterly checkpoint reviews

- At Day 90, 180, 270 billable days: auto-generate quarterly_htd_review record. Notify Hemant and BU Head.

- Checkpoint review covers: Guidewire technical progression (has their GW skill depth improved since last checkpoint?), delivery velocity trend, manager feedback trajectory, client feedback.

- Hemant logs checkpoint decision: ON_TRACK / NEEDS_SUPPORT / EXTEND_VALIDATION. NEEDS_SUPPORT: assigns additional training resources. EXTEND_VALIDATION: can add up to 90 additional days to certification threshold (max 455 total days).

## Step 3: Build HTD-specific AI Assessor weighting

- For employees with htd_track=TRUE: modify HRMS-0518 AI Assessor weights: technical_progression_score weight increases to 30% (vs 15% standard). GW certification attempt/pass added as additional KPI with 10% weight. Manager weekly 'development signal' carries 20% (vs 10% standard). Peer trust stays at 15%.

## Business Rules

## BR: HTD Employees Cannot Be Core Certified Before 365 Billable Days

- HRMS-0513 Core Eligibility Gate validates clock_type and billable_days_count before allowing initiation. An HTD employee at Day 200 cannot initiate Core eligibility review even if their performance scores are perfect. The 365-day threshold is non-negotiable.

- Field: 365 billable days minimum — hard gate, no exceptions for HTD

## BR: Hemant Signs Off on Each Quarterly Checkpoint

- Quarterly HTD checkpoints are not system-automated. Hemant personally reviews and logs the checkpoint decision. This maintains human accountability for the conversion quality of every HTD cohort and ensures the conversion rate metrics (HRMS-0320) reflect genuine quality gates, not automated pass-throughs.

- Field: Hemant personal sign-off required on all HTD quarterly checkpoints
