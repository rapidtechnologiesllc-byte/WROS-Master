## Why — Business Rationale

- Raw pipeline value overstates what BlitzenX will actually close — a $2M pipeline at 20% average probability isn't a $2M forecast. Weighted forecasting is the one calculation every dashboard, coverage ratio, and forecast-vs-actual comparison in this epic depends on, so it needs to be correct and consistent everywhere it's used, not recalculated slightly differently on each screen.

## What — Scope

- The weighted revenue forecast engine — revenue_value × probability, aggregated by client, BU, and month, with all figures normalized to USD.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-236 must be live and producing opportunities with revenue_value and probability_pct.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the weighted forecast calculation

- weighted_value = revenue_value_usd_cents × (probability_pct / 100), per opportunity.

## Step 2: Build the aggregation layers

- Sum by client, by BU, by month (based on expected_close_date) — three distinct aggregation views over the same underlying calculation, not three separate calculations.

## Step 3: Expose the calculation as a shared function

- S-241, S-242, and S-244 all call this same forecast function rather than each reimplementing the weighting logic independently.
