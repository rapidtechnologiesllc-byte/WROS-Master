## Why — Business Rationale

- When demand plan is approved and external sourcing is authorized (after bench-first check), job requisitions should be created automatically from the demand items. Manual job creation from approved demand is a process gap that delays sourcing by 1-3 days per role. Auto-creation eliminates this gap.

## What — Scope

- Build JobReqAutoCreator: reads approved demand plan items with sourcing_strategy=EXTERNAL_HIRE or HTD, creates demand records in demands table with all required fields pre-populated from the plan item. Assigns to recruiting queue.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0307 sourcing trigger. HRMS-0308 bench-first check passed. HRMS-0103 demands table.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build JobReqAutoCreator service

- JobReqAutoCreator.create(demand_plan_item_id): reads workforce_demand_plan_items record. Maps to demands table: job_title from role_type, required_skills from bu_revenue_role_mapping skill tags, delivery_engine from plan item, source=DEMAND_PLAN, demand_plan_item_id (FK), urgency from plan item urgency, status=OPEN, bu_id from plan, min_experience_years=5 (hard rule), work_authorization from system config.

- Creates one demand record per head required. (If heads_external=3: creates 3 demand records for that role type.)

- Notifies RM via portal notification: 'N job requisitions auto-created from approved demand plan. Review and assign recruiters.'

## Step 2: Build demand assignment to recruiter

- After auto-creation: RM assigns each demand to a recruiter via PATCH /api/demands/{id}/assign { recruiter_user_id }.

- Unassigned demands older than 24h: alert RM.

## Business Rules

## BR: Auto-Created Demands Inherit 5-Year Experience Gate

- All demands auto-created from workforce planning have min_experience_years=5 set automatically. This cannot be overridden at creation time. Override requires BU Head approval per HRMS-P604 experience override workflow.

- Field: min_experience_years=5 on all auto-created demands

## BR: One Demand Record Per Head Required

- If plan item says heads_external=3 for Senior GW Consultant: three separate demand records are created, not one. This enables independent tracking of each hire: each gets its own pipeline, its own recruiter assignment, its own candidate shortlist.

- Field: One demand per head — not one demand per role type
