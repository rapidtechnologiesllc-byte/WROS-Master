## Why — Business Rationale

- BlitzenX must quote a rate immediately for adhoc requests. AI reads historical billing rates for similar roles + client, candidate salary cost, and target margin to suggest a billing rate range.

## What — Scope

- PricingSuggester: min/target/max billing rate with confidence score and rationale. RM accepts or overrides.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0601 demand. analytics_fact_revenue historical rates. Employee salary data.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build PricingSuggester

- cost_basis = salary/160h. min_rate = cost_basis/(1-0.20). target_rate = cost_basis/(1-0.30). max_rate = MAX(historical rates for role+client)*1.05. Store in adhoc_pricing_suggestions.

## Step 2: Pricing suggestion UI

- Card on demand detail: Min/Target/Max with confidence %. [Use Target Rate] or enter custom.

## Business Rules

## BR: Min 3 Historical Comparables

- < 3 comparables → confidence capped 50%, labeled LOW CONFIDENCE.

- Field: 3-comparable minimum

## BR: 20% Margin Floor

- Rate never suggested below cost_basis/(1-0.20). Floor labeled if applied.

- Field: 20% margin floor — never breached
