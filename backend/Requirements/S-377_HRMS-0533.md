## Why — Business Rationale

- When a BlitzenX employee conducts an interview and the candidate they pass gets placed at a client, does the client agree with that assessment? If the client then says 'this person is not meeting our expectations' within 90 days of placement, it suggests the interviewer's screening was insufficient. Interviewer quality scoring tracks this: not how many interviews someone conducted, but how many of the candidates they cleared actually succeeded at the client.

## What — Scope

- Build interviewer quality scoring: tracks candidates cleared by each employee interviewer, matches against client outcome within 90 days of placement. Computes client_pass_rate per interviewer. Writes to HRMS-0515 performance store as one of the 35 Buddy/Specialty KPIs.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0248 interview records with interviewer_user_id. HRMS-0507 allocations (to know who was placed). Client feedback signals from HRMS-P703.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build InterviewerQualityScorer service

- Monthly job: for each employee who conducted interviews in the last 90 days: (1) Count interviews they conducted where outcome=PASS (they cleared the candidate). (2) Count how many of those candidates were subsequently placed at a client. (3) Count how many placed candidates received no negative client feedback in their first 90 days at client (success). (4) client_pass_rate = successes / placements. (5) Write to HRMS-0515: event_type=INTERVIEW_QUALITY, score=client_pass_rate*100.

## Step 2: Build interviewer capacity management

- RM can see per-employee interview capacity: how many interviews they have taken, their client_pass_rate, and whether they are available for more interviews.

- Employees with < 50% client_pass_rate flagged for RM review: 'Interviewer quality concern: [name] — 40% client pass rate in last quarter. Review interview approach before assigning more interviews.'

- IMPORTANT: employees are never penalised if candidates they placed sit on bench — bench duration is driven by partner sales timelines, not interview quality. Only client performance failures count against the score.

## Business Rules

## BR: Bench Duration Does Not Count Against Interviewer Score

- If a candidate cleared by an interviewer is placed but then sits on bench waiting for a project, this does NOT negatively impact the interviewer's quality score. Bench duration is a sales/demand timing issue, not an interview quality issue. Only active client engagements that result in negative client feedback count against the score.

- Field: Bench duration excluded from interviewer quality calculation — client feedback only

## BR: Minimum 3 Placements Before Score Is Shown

- Interviewer quality score is only displayed when the employee has had at least 3 candidates placed who have reached 90 days at a client. Before this threshold: 'Insufficient data for quality score.' This prevents unfair scores from single-interview samples.

- Field: Minimum 3 placements before score displayed
