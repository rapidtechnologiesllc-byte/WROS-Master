## Why — Business Rationale

- Once a BU sets a $2.4M revenue target, the system needs to know: how many people does $2.4M require? What roles? What billing rates? The revenue-to-role mapping table is the conversion engine that translates a dollar target into a headcount plan. Without it, workforce planning is manual and inconsistent across BUs.

## What — Scope

- Build: (1) Revenue-to-role conversion ratio configuration per BU. (2) Role mix definition — what percentage of headcount is each role type. (3) Billing rate assumptions per role. (4) Headcount calculation engine.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0301 bu_revenue_targets. Role taxonomy from demands table (job_title, skill categories).

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Create bu_revenue_role_mapping table

- CREATE TABLE bu_revenue_role_mapping: id UUID PK, tenant_id UUID, bu_id UUID, delivery_engine ENUM('SPECIALITY','CORE'), role_type VARCHAR(200) (e.g. 'Senior Guidewire Consultant', 'Java Developer', 'Technical Lead'), avg_billing_rate_usd_cents_per_hour BIGINT, avg_billable_hours_per_month INTEGER default 160, role_mix_pct DECIMAL (% of total headcount this role represents), created_by UUID, effective_from DATE

- One record per role type per BU per delivery engine. BU Head configures these assumptions.

## Step 2: Build headcount calculator

- HeadcountCalculator.calculate(bu_id, target_amount_usd_cents, months): for each role_type in mapping: monthly_revenue_per_head = avg_billing_rate * avg_billable_hours. heads_needed = (target * role_mix_pct) / (monthly_revenue_per_head * months). Round up. Return: [{ role_type, delivery_engine, heads_needed, monthly_revenue_per_head }]

- Example: $2.4M / 12 months = $200k/month. Senior GW Consultant: $110/hr * 160h = $17,600/month. $200k * 40% mix = $80k needed from this role → 80000/17600 = 4.5 → 5 heads.

## Step 3: Build mapping configuration UI

- Route: /admin/workforce-planning/role-mapping

- Table per BU: role type, delivery engine, billing rate, billable hours, role mix %. Add/edit per row.

- Live calculator: enter target amount → instantly shows required headcount by role.

## Business Rules

## BR: Role Mix % Must Sum to 100% Before Saving

- System validates that all role_mix_pct values for a BU sum to 100% before saving. If not: show validation error listing current total. Cannot generate demand plan without valid mapping.

- Field: role_mix_pct SUM = 100% validation

## BR: Separate Mapping for Speciality and Core Delivery Engines

- Billing rates and role mix differ between Speciality (SI partner work, lower bill rates) and Core (direct carrier, higher bill rates). Each delivery engine has its own mapping configuration. HeadcountCalculator applies the correct engine's rates.

- Field: Delivery engine-specific mapping
