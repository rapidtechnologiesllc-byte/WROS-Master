## Why — Business Rationale

- Every other financial calculation in this platform — the Reserve Fund, hiring affordability, partner incentives, the executive dashboard — ultimately reads from one number: each entity's monthly Net Profit, computed correctly and kept genuinely separate between BXIN and BXUS. Your explicit architectural decision that these are two standalone P&Ls with no automatic consolidation is the single most important design constraint in this whole epic — getting it right here means everything downstream inherits it correctly; getting it wrong here means every other number in this epic is subtly wrong too.

## What — Scope

- The monthly P&L calculation engine for both entities, run independently — BXIN's P&L reads only BXIN-scoped revenue, salary cost, incentives, and expenses; BXUS's reads only its own — down through Gross Profit, EBITDA (equal to Gross Profit per your D&A-embedded-in-Min-Bill-Rate decision), and Net Profit after Reserve Contribution. An org-level rollup exists only as a dashboard-display aggregation, never as a third P&L with its own independent logic.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-1608 must be live — this engine's Salary Cost line reads each active employee's fully-loaded cost.

- HRMS-1605 Bank Reconciliation should be the trusted source for confirming revenue/expense figures are accurate, though this engine can run against unreconciled data with a clear staleness/unreconciled indicator, since monthly P&L generation shouldn't be blocked waiting for reconciliation to complete.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the BXIN monthly P&L calculation

- Revenue: Specialty billing only, from the Invoice Ledger (accrual basis — performance month, not invoice date, per your fiscal timing rule), Location=India.

- Salary Cost: sum of (Total Annual Cost ÷ 12) for all active BXIN employees, from HRMS-1608.

- Incentives Paid: from the Incentive Ledger where status=Paid, this month, Location=India.

- Direct Expense: from the Expense Ledger, Location=India, this month.

- Gross Profit = Revenue - Salary Cost - Incentives Paid - Direct Expense.

- EBITDA = Gross Profit + D&A Add-back — per your explicit note, D&A is already embedded in Min Bill Rate via the Laptop CapEx component, so this add-back is effectively $0 in practice and EBITDA equals Gross Profit for BlitzenX's purposes. Build the add-back as a real, separate line (even if currently always zero) rather than hardcoding EBITDA=Gross Profit, in case this assumption changes later.

- Net Profit = EBITDA - Reserve Contribution (computed per HRMS-1612, called from here, not reimplemented).

## Step 2: Build the BXUS monthly P&L calculation

- Revenue: US-billable employees only (Alfa Insurance, Builders Insurance Group, CastleBay, Subcontracting), explicitly excluding revenue from India employees billed through BXUS (Core employees physically in India) — this exclusion rule matters and should be a named, tested condition, not an incidental byproduct of how the query happens to be written.

- Partner and staff salaries: Troy, Curtis, Avinash (including Avinash's 5% org revenue share, which itself depends on the org-level rollup — see the circular-dependency note below), plus other US staff, from HRMS-1608/payroll sync.

- Same Gross Profit / EBITDA / Net Profit structure as BXIN.

Step 3: Build the org-level rollup as a pure dashboard aggregation, not a third calculation

- Total Revenue = BXIN Revenue (USD) + BXUS Revenue. Total Expense = same pattern. EBITDA and Net Profit follow the same additive pattern.

- This rollup exists solely for HRMS-1614's dashboard display — it is never itself the input to another calculation (per your explicit 'no auto-consolidation' rule), with one necessary exception noted below.

## Step 4: Resolve the Avinash revenue-share circular dependency explicitly

- Avinash's 5% org-revenue compensation is a BXUS expense, but it's computed from the org-level rollup, which depends on both entities' revenue figures — meaning BXUS's own Net Profit depends on a figure that depends on BXIN's revenue too. Build this as an explicit two-pass calculation (compute both entities' revenue first, then the org rollup, then Avinash's share, then finalize BXUS's expense line) rather than trying to force it into a single linear pass — and document this as the one place org-level data legitimately feeds back into an entity P&L, distinct from general consolidation.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-1607-01 — BXIN and BXUS P&Ls never auto-consolidate into a shared calculation, with one explicit exception

- Per your architectural decision, each entity's P&L is standalone — the sole exception is Avinash's 5% org-revenue compensation, which by its own definition requires the org-level rollup, and that dependency is built as an explicit two-pass calculation, not a general-purpose consolidation mechanism other stories could accidentally start relying on.

- Field: No shared calculation function computes both entities' Net Profit together except the specifically-scoped Avinash revenue-share resolution

BR: BR-1607-02 — BXUS revenue explicitly excludes India-based Core employees billed through BXUS

- This exclusion is easy to get wrong silently (a query that joins on 'billed through BXUS' without also filtering employee location would overstate BXUS revenue) — it must be a named, independently tested condition.

- Field: BXUS revenue query filters explicitly on employee_location != India even when delivery_engine=CORE and billing_entity=BXUS

BR: BR-1607-03 — EBITDA = Gross Profit + D&A Add-back is a real calculation, not a hardcoded equivalence

- Currently the D&A add-back resolves to $0 because D&A is embedded in Min Bill Rate — but this is a fact about current cost-structure design, not a permanent mathematical identity, and should remain a genuine (if currently zero) line item.

- Field: da_addback_usd_cents is a computed field, defaulting to 0 under current cost-structure assumptions, not a skipped calculation step

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: BXIN's P&L reads exclusively BXIN-scoped data (Location=India) at every line, verified by seeding cross-entity data and confirming no leakage.

- AC-2: BXUS's revenue correctly excludes India-based Core employees billed through BXUS, verified with a specific test case seeding that exact scenario.

- AC-3: The org-level rollup is provably a pure sum of the two independently-computed entity P&Ls, except for the explicitly-scoped Avinash revenue-share resolution.

- AC-4: Avinash's revenue share correctly reflects the two-pass calculation, verified by changing BXIN revenue and confirming it flows through to BXUS's expense line via the org rollup, not directly.
