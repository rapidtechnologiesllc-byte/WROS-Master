## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

HR's focus is different from recruiting. They don't care about AI activity — they care about: who accepted an offer, who needs documents, who is joining this week, and who has outstanding compliance items. The HR Dashboard surfaces exactly these metrics without the recruiting noise.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build HR Dashboard at /hr/dashboard. Four sections: (1) Upcoming Joiners (next 14 days). (2) Document Collection Status. (3) Offer Pipeline (released, accepted, declined). (4) Joining Readiness Alerts (candidates with readiness score < 50).

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Build GET /api/hr/dashboard endpoint

- Auth: HR role only. Returns: { upcoming_joiners: [{ name, role, start_date, readiness_score }] (next 14 days), document_status: { complete: N, pending_reminder: N, overdue: N, candidates_list: [{name, docs_received, total_docs, days_to_joining}] }, offer_pipeline: { released: N, accepted: N, declined: N, countered: N, expired: N }, readiness_alerts: [{ name, readiness_score, missing_items: string[] }] (score < 50) }

## Step 2: Build HR Dashboard page

- Route: /hr/dashboard. Auth: HR role.

- Card 1 — Upcoming Joiners: table of candidates joining in next 14 days. Sorted by start_date. Readiness score badge (green/amber/red).

- Card 2 — Preboarding Status: 3 donut segments: Complete (all docs) / In Progress (partial) / Overdue (missing, 3+ reminders sent). Click each to see candidate list.

- Card 3 — Offer Pipeline: 5 horizontal bars for Released/Accepted/Declined/Countered/Expired counts with candidate names on hover.

- Card 4 — Readiness Alerts: candidates with joining_readiness_score < 50. Sorted lowest first. Each shows: name, score, what is missing.

## BR-01: HR Dashboard Only Shows Candidates in Offer or Preboarding Stage

Cards 1, 2, 4 only show candidates where conversation.status IN (OFFER_SENT, PREBOARDING). HR does not manage earlier pipeline stages.

↳ Triggered by field: OFFER_SENT and PREBOARDING stages only
