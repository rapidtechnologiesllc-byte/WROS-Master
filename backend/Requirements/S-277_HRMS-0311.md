## Why — Business Rationale

- The manual revenue-to-role mapping (HRMS-0302) uses fixed billing rates and role mix percentages set by BU Heads. Over time, with actual data on win rates, time-to-hire, billing rates achieved, and HTD conversion rates, the system can learn better conversion ratios automatically. This AI model improves demand planning accuracy by learning from historical patterns.

## What — Scope

- Build an ML model that learns from historical data: past revenue targets vs actual revenue, past headcount plans vs actual hires, win rates by opportunity stage, HTD conversion rates. Produces adjusted conversion ratios as suggestions to BU Heads.

## Before — Prerequisites

- Minimum 6 months of: bu_revenue_targets, workforce_demand_plans, actual revenue from analytics_fact_revenue, HTD pipeline data, opportunity win/loss history.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build RevenueWorkforceModel

- Train on: historical target_amount vs actual_revenue (accuracy), historical role mix vs actual hired role mix (accuracy), opportunity stage → close_probability accuracy by stage, HTD conversion rate by cohort.

- Outputs: adjusted_billing_rate_suggestion per role (if actual rates differ from configured), adjusted_role_mix_suggestion per BU (if actual hires differ from plan mix), confidence_score 0-100.

- Runs monthly. Results stored in ai_conversion_model_outputs table.

## Step 2: Build model suggestions UI

- On BU revenue mapping config page (HRMS-0302): show AI suggestion badge next to each configured value. '⚡ AI suggests: $118/hr (based on 6 months actual data, confidence 78%)'. BU Head can accept or dismiss.

- On demand plan generation (HRMS-0303): show model-adjusted headcount alongside manual headcount calculation for comparison.

## Business Rules

## BR: AI Suggestions Are Advisory — BU Head Decides

- The AI model suggests adjustments but never automatically changes configuration values. BU Head explicitly accepts or dismisses each suggestion. Accepted suggestions are applied to the mapping config; dismissed suggestions are logged but not applied. Model learns from acceptance rate.

- Field: Advisory only — no auto-apply of AI suggestions
