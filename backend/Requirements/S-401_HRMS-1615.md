## Why — Business Rationale

- GST filing (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B), TDS deduction and filing, e-invoicing thresholds, and HSN/SAC code handling carry real legal and financial consequences if built incorrectly — unlike every other story in this epic, this is not internal business logic where a reasonable, well-tested implementation is sufficient. Per your explicit instruction, WROS needs to match or exceed Tally's statutory capability, verified through direct comparison, before Tally is decommissioned for this purpose — this story is deliberately scoped as a structural first pass, not a shippable feature, until that verification happens.

## What — Scope

- A structural scaffold for India GST and statutory compliance — GSTR-1/GSTR-3B report generation, TDS calculation and filing support, e-invoicing threshold detection, HSN/SAC code assignment — built to current general understanding of these requirements, explicitly and permanently flagged as requiring BlitzenX's Finance team and CA to validate every calculation and filing output against actual statutory requirements before any of it is used for real filing.

## Before — Prerequisites

- **Do not begin implementation** until BlitzenX's Finance team and CA have reviewed and signed off on the specific statutory requirements this story needs to satisfy — GSTR-1/3B format and filing cadence, current TDS rates and thresholds, e-invoicing applicability given BlitzenX's turnover, and HSN/SAC codes for BlitzenX's actual service lines. Building ahead of this review risks producing code that looks complete but encodes incorrect assumptions about India's statutory requirements.

- A structural first pass may be drafted now, clearly labeled throughout as pending professional review, if useful as a starting point for that Finance/CA conversation — but this is explicitly not a substitute for the requirements review itself.
