## Why — Business Rationale

- Workforce plans represent significant financial commitments — hiring decisions that will cost BlitzenX salary, bench cost, and training investment. Before sourcing starts, the plan must be approved by BU Head and HR Director. This approval workflow ensures both functional (BU Head knows the business need) and financial (Director has budget sign-off) review happens before headcount spend begins.

## What — Scope

- Build the two-step approval workflow for workforce demand plans: RM submits → BU Head approves → Director final approval → sourcing triggers. With SLA, reminders, and escalation.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0303 demand plan with status=DRAFT. HRMS-P611 org authority map.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build two-step approval state machine

- States: DRAFT → SUBMITTED_FOR_APPROVAL → BU_HEAD_APPROVED → APPROVED (Director approved) | REJECTED_BU_HEAD | REJECTED_DIRECTOR

- POST /api/workforce-planning/plans/{id}/submit: RM submits. Status→SUBMITTED_FOR_APPROVAL. Notifies BU Head.

- PATCH /api/workforce-planning/plans/{id}/bu-approve: Auth BU Head. Status→BU_HEAD_APPROVED. Notifies Director.

- PATCH /api/workforce-planning/plans/{id}/final-approve: Auth Director/Admin. Status→APPROVED. Triggers sourcing.

- PATCH /api/workforce-planning/plans/{id}/reject: body { reason, stage }. Returns to DRAFT with rejection notes.

## Step 2: Build SLA reminders

- BU Head SLA: 48 hours to approve/reject. If no action: WhatsApp + Email reminder. If no action after 72 hours: escalate to Director.

- Director SLA: 24 hours to final-approve. If no action: escalate to Admin with note.

## Business Rules

## BR: BU Head and Director Must Both Approve Before Sourcing Starts

- Both approvals are required — not either/or. BU Head approval alone is insufficient to trigger sourcing. Director final approval is the gate. This ensures financial oversight at every plan cycle.

- Field: Sequential two-step approval — both required

## BR: Plan Cannot Be Modified While Under Review

- Once submitted for approval: plan items cannot be edited. If revision needed: reject back to DRAFT first. This prevents plan changes after BU Head has reviewed but before Director approves.

- Field: Lock on submission — edit requires rejection to DRAFT

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: RM can submit plan for approval

- AC-2: BU Head approves/rejects with 48h SLA

- AC-3: Director final-approves with 24h SLA

- AC-4: Sourcing triggered only after Director approval

- AC-5: Plan locked for editing while under review

- AC-6: Rejection returns to DRAFT with notes
