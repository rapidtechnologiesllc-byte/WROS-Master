## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

Drop risk prediction is the unified risk signal that spans the entire candidate journey — from qualification all the way through preboarding. A candidate can drop out at any stage for different reasons: during qualification they ghost, during interview they accept another offer, during preboarding they get a counter-offer. Thunder must detect these risks proactively so recruiters can intervene before the dropout happens, not after.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build calculateDropRisk(candidate_id) — a comprehensive 0-100 risk score that combines stage-appropriate signals: abandonment score (qualifying stage), response rate, sentiment trend, ghosting history, stage time (days stuck in same stage), and joining readiness score (preboarding stage). Flag score >= 70 in intervention queue.

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Create candidate_drop_risk table

- id UUID PK, tenant_id UUID, candidate_id UUID UNIQUE FK→candidates.id, drop_risk_score INTEGER (0-100), risk_level ENUM('LOW','MEDIUM','HIGH','CRITICAL') computed from score, risk_signals JSONB (breakdown of contributing signals), is_flagged BOOLEAN, calculated_at TIMESTAMP

- Risk levels: LOW=0-39, MEDIUM=40-59, HIGH=60-79, CRITICAL=80-100

## Step 2: Build calculateDropRisk() function

- calculateDropRisk(candidate_id, tenant_id) → integer 0-100

- Load: current conversation state, abandonment_score (from HRMS-0446), last 5 sentiment records, ghosting status, days in current stage, readiness_score if in PREBOARDING

- Stage-gated scoring:

- QUALIFYING/QUALIFIED stages: 60% weight from abandonment_score. 25% from sentiment (all negative=25pts, mixed=10pts). 15% from days in stage (>7 days=15pts, >3 days=8pts, <3 days=0pts).

- INTERVIEW_SCHEDULED stage: 50% from response rate since interview booked. 30% from days until interview (> 5 days with no Thunder contact = risk). 20% from reschedule count (2 reschedules=20pts).

- OFFER_SENT stage: 60% from days since offer sent without response (each day=8pts up to 60). 25% from last sentiment. 15% from FAQ questions asked (many questions = engaged = lower risk).

- PREBOARDING stage: 70% weight from INVERSE of readiness_score ((100-readiness_score)*0.70). 30% from days since last communication.

- Ghosting multiplier: if candidate_ghosting_status.is_reactivated=false AND ghosted_at is not null: multiply final score by 1.3 (cap at 100). Prior ghosting increases risk.

- UPSERT into candidate_drop_risk

## Step 3: Build DropRiskScoringJob (runs every 4 hours)

- Score all candidates in QUALIFYING, QUALIFIED, INTERVIEW_SCHEDULED, OFFER_SENT, PREBOARDING states

- After scoring: if drop_risk_score >= 80 (CRITICAL) and not already in intervention queue: add to HRMS-0462 with priority=HIGH. Notify recruiter immediately.

- If drop_risk_score >= 70 (HIGH or CRITICAL): is_flagged=true. Add to intervention queue with normal priority.

- If score drops back below 60 (previously flagged): remove from intervention queue. Log DROP_RISK_RESOLVED.

## Step 4: Build drop risk API and display

- Add drop_risk_score and risk_level to GET /api/candidates/{id} response

- Show on candidate profile Status Card when risk_level=HIGH or CRITICAL: '⚠ Drop Risk: HIGH (score: 82)' in amber/red

- Show CRITICAL candidates with flashing red indicator

- In recruiter candidate list: sort option 'Sort by Drop Risk' (CRITICAL first)

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: CRITICAL Score (80+) Triggers Immediate Recruiter Notification

When drop_risk_score first reaches 80+ (crosses into CRITICAL): notify recruiter immediately via in-app + WhatsApp + email notification. Do not wait for next job run. This is the equivalent of an emergency alert — a candidate is about to walk away.

↳ Triggered by field: drop_risk_score >= 80

## BR-02: Score Is Stage-Appropriate — Not One-Size-Fits-All

The contributing signals and their weights change depending on the candidate's current stage. A candidate in PREBOARDING cares about readiness score; a candidate in QUALIFYING cares about response rate. Using the same formula for all stages produces meaningless scores.

↳ Triggered by field: stage-gated scoring logic

## BR-03: Ghosting History Amplifies Risk — Never Resets

A candidate who previously ghosted (even if reactivated) carries a permanent 1.3x multiplier on their drop_risk_score. Past ghosting is a strong predictor of future dropout. This multiplier can only be removed by admin action.

↳ Triggered by field: ghosting_multiplier = 1.3 for previously ghosted candidates
