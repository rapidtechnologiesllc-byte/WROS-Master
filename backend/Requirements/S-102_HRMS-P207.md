## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

After L1 and L2 interviews are complete and feedback is submitted, the Hiring Manager makes the final technical hiring decision: approve for offer or reject. They need all the information in one place — both interviewers' feedback, the candidate's overall score, their profile, and the recommendation — without navigating between multiple pages.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build HM Decision View at /technical/hiring-decision/{candidate_id}/{job_id}. Shows consolidated interview feedback from L1 and L2, candidate scores, HM Decision form (Approve/Reject/Hold with justification).

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Build HM Decision page

- Route: /technical/hiring-decision/{candidate_id}/{job_id}. Auth: Hiring Manager role.

- Section 1 — Candidate Summary: name, job title, technical score (87/100), overall score (82/100), years experience, top skills.

- Section 2 — L1 Feedback Summary: interviewer name, overall rating, recommendation badge (PASS/FAIL), strengths (condensed), concerns.

- Section 3 — L2 Feedback Summary: same structure.

- Section 4 — HM Decision: 3 radio buttons: APPROVE FOR OFFER / REJECT / HOLD (pending more info). Justification textarea (required, min 100 chars). Submit button.

## Step 2: Build POST /api/technical/hiring-decisions endpoint

- Body: { candidate_id, job_id, decision: 'APPROVE'|'REJECT'|'HOLD', justification: string }

- Auth: Hiring Manager role.

- Validate: justification min 100 chars.

- INSERT hiring_decisions: { id, tenant_id, candidate_id, job_id, decision, justification, decided_by, decided_at }

- APPROVE: publish event hiring.approved. HRMS-0453 offer readiness now picks up HM approval.

- REJECT: UPDATE candidate_job_scores flags. Notify recruiting team. Thunder notifies candidate with empathy message.

- HOLD: flag for recruiter to gather more info. No candidate notification yet.

- Publish event: hiring.decision_made { candidate_id, job_id, decision }

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: HM Decision Requires Both L1 and L2 Feedback

The HM Decision page must verify both L1 and L2 interview_feedback records exist before allowing submission. If either is missing: show 'Please wait for all interview feedback to be submitted before making a hiring decision.' HM cannot override this.

↳ Triggered by field: Both L1 and L2 feedback required

## BR-02: REJECT Triggers Thunder Empathy Notification

When HM decision = REJECT: Thunder sends a warm, professional rejection message to the candidate via their preferred channel: 'Thank you for your time interviewing with BlitzenX. After careful consideration, we have decided to move forward with another candidate for this role. We appreciate your interest and will keep your profile for future opportunities.'

↳ Triggered by field: REJECT → Thunder notification to candidate
