## Why — Business Rationale

- P1 emergency: client needs resource within 24 hours. Standard adhoc flow is too slow. P1 compresses everything: instant sync bench search, RM call trigger, Director awareness, auto-booked intro call, SLA tracking.

## What — Scope

- P1 flow triggered when required_by_date ≤ NOW()+24h. Escalates to Director, WhatsApp/SMS to RM, synchronous bench search, auto-books intro call, tracks 4h submission SLA.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0603/0604. HRMS-1306 calendar. Director contact config.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: P1 detection and escalation

- On adhoc creation: if required_by_date <= NOW()+24h: set demands.p1_emergency=true. P1EmergencyProtocol: synchronous InstantBenchMatcher + AllocationRecommender. WhatsApp+SMS to RM. Director WhatsApp notification. If no RM assigned: notify all RM + Director.

## Step 2: Auto-booked intro call

- If match found: auto-book 30-min intro call within 4 hours via HRMS-1306. Calendar invite sent to all parties.

## Step 3: P1 SLA tracking

- p1_sla_log: demand_id, triggered_at, match_found_at, submission_sent_at, intro_call_booked_at, sla_met. Submission SLA: 4h. Intro call: 2h after match.

## Business Rules

## BR: P1 = Required Within 24 Hours — Auto-Set

- Auto-flagged on creation. RM cannot manually flag P1 for longer timelines.

- Field: 24h threshold — auto

## BR: Director Always Notified on P1

- Every P1 notifies Director immediately — no exception.

- Field: Director always notified
