## Why — Business Rationale

- The 30-day Buddy Program is only meaningful if there is a real gate at the end. Without BU Head sign-off, the program becomes a formality. This story builds the graduation gate: BU Head reviews the composite scorecard from all three evaluators, makes a deliberate decision (Graduate / Extend / Exit), and that decision determines whether the employee can be deployed to a Specialty client. No graduation = no client deployment.

## What — Scope

- Build Day 30 graduation gate: BU Head receives composite scorecard, reviews it, and makes a decision. GRADUATE sets status=SPECIALTY_READY. EXTEND adds 15 days and re-evaluates. EXIT triggers performance management. All decisions logged permanently.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0520 buddy program Day 30 scorecard. HRMS-0101 buddy_program_status field.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build graduation gate notification and review page

- On Day 30 scorecard generation: notify BU Head via WhatsApp + portal notification: 'Buddy Program Graduation Review Required: [Employee Name]. Weighted Score: [N]/5. [View Scorecard]'

- Route: /admin/buddy-program/{record_id}/graduation-gate. Shows: full 35-KPI scorecard, trajectory chart, lowest-scoring KPIs, Buddy Engineer written feedback, HR compliance summary, RM operational summary.

## Step 2: Build BU Head decision flow

- Three buttons: [Graduate] [Extend 15 Days] [Exit Program].

- GRADUATE: buddy_program_records.status=GRADUATED, graduation_date=TODAY. employees.buddy_program_status=GRADUATED. employees.status=SPECIALITY_READY. Notifies RM: 'John Smith graduated Buddy Program. Ready for Specialty deployment.'

- EXTEND: adds 15 days. extension_count increments. Requires BU Head to log which specific KPIs need improvement (min 50 chars). Max 2 extensions — third failure = EXIT.

- EXIT: employees.status=PERFORMANCE_MANAGED. HR notified to initiate performance process. RM notified.

## Step 3: Build graduation gate SLA

- BU Head has 48 hours to review scorecard from notification. If no decision in 48 hours: escalate to Director with note 'BU Head graduation review overdue for [employee].'

## Business Rules

## BR: No Client Deployment Until Buddy Program GRADUATED

- employees.status=SPECIALITY_READY is set only on GRADUATE decision. The allocation system (HRMS-0507) validates buddy_program_status=GRADUATED before creating any client allocation. An employee with status=IN_PROGRESS or EXTENDED cannot be allocated to any client.

- Field: buddy_program_status=GRADUATED required for allocation — validated at allocation creation

## BR: Maximum 2 Extensions — Third Failure Is Exit

- extension_count is tracked. On third BU Head review (after 2 extensions): only [Graduate] or [Exit] buttons are shown. [Extend 15 Days] is hidden. If still not ready after 60 days of buddy program: exit. BlitzenX does not carry underperforming employees indefinitely.

- Field: Max 2 extensions. Third review: Graduate or Exit only.
