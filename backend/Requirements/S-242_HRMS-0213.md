## Why — Business Rationale

- Section 13's platform KPI target is under 10% revenue variance by Q3 — but that number is only meaningful if BlitzenX can see, month by month and BU by BU, exactly where the weighted forecast diverged from what actually closed. This story is what makes that KPI measurable rather than aspirational.

## What — Scope

- A month-by-month, BU-by-BU comparison of S-238's weighted forecast against actual closed revenue from HRMS-0313, with variance highlighted.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-238 must be live for forecast figures.

- HRMS-0313 Plan vs Actuals must expose actual closed revenue per BU per month.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the comparison table

- Rows: month × BU. Columns: weighted forecast, actual closed, variance (absolute and %).

## Step 2: Build variance highlighting

- Variance beyond 10% (matching Section 13's target threshold) visually flagged.

## Step 3: Link to HRMS-1106

- This view is the manual drill-down a Director reaches when investigating an HRMS-1106 Revenue Monitoring Agent alert — same underlying variance calculation, not a second implementation.
