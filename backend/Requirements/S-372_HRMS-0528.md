## Why — Business Rationale

- A partner winning a project and a partner confirming a project are two completely different moments. If BlitzenX pulls an employee from PwC to prepare for an Alfa Insurance engagement that then falls through: PwC is disrupted, the employee is on bench, BlitzenX has created a problem with both clients. The confirmed vs potential workflow protects all parties: for potential demands, the candidate is prepared and submitted but the Specialty client is not told anything until the SOW is signed.

## What — Scope

- Build demand confirmation workflow: Thunder asks partners whether demand is CONFIRMED (SOW signed) or POTENTIAL (interview required). Two distinct downstream workflows with different sequencing. Specialty client release only triggered after SOW confirmed and both employee + management agree.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0527 partner intent engine. HRMS-0103 demands. HRMS-0534 Specialty client release workflow.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Add confirmation_status to demands

- ALTER TABLE demands ADD COLUMN confirmation_status ENUM('POTENTIAL','CONFIRMED','CANCELLED') DEFAULT 'POTENTIAL'.

- ALTER TABLE demands ADD COLUMN sow_reference VARCHAR(200) nullable.

- ALTER TABLE demands ADD COLUMN sow_received_date DATE nullable.

## Step 2: Build POTENTIAL demand workflow

- POTENTIAL workflow: (1) RM Agent scores Core pool. (2) Top 3 candidates identified. (3) Curtis + BU Head + candidate 3-way alignment call scheduled. (4) Curtis submits candidate to client for interview. (5) Client confirms fit. (6) SOW received → demand.confirmation_status=CONFIRMED. (7) ONLY NOW: Specialty client release process (HRMS-0534) triggered. (8) Start date confirmed. Allocation created.

- Specialty client (PwC) is NOT notified until Step 7. No premature disruption.

## Step 3: Build CONFIRMED demand workflow

- CONFIRMED workflow: (1) RM Agent scores Core pool. (2) Top match identified. (3) Curtis + BU Head + candidate alignment call — same day. (4) Employee and management both confirm fit. (5) RM notifies Specialty client with replacement plan and handover timeline. (6) Allocation created with confirmed start date.

- Timeline: CONFIRMED demands target allocation within 5 business days. POTENTIAL demands: no timeline commitment until SOW received.

## Step 4: Build 3-way alignment call scheduler

- On candidate identified: SchedulerService.book3WayAlignment(curtis_user_id, bu_head_user_id, employee_id). Uses HRMS-1306 calendar. Finds first 30-min mutual slot. Sends invites. Records meeting_at in demands.alignment_call_at.

- Both employee and management must confirm fit after call: POST /api/demands/{id}/confirm-fit { participant: EMPLOYEE|BU_HEAD, confirmed: true|false, notes }. Both confirmations required before any Specialty client notification.

## Business Rules

## BR: Specialty Client Not Notified Until SOW Confirmed and Both Parties Agree

- HRMS-0534 Specialty Client Release workflow cannot be triggered until: (1) demand.confirmation_status=CONFIRMED, (2) both employee and BU Head fit confirmations logged. This is a hard sequence that cannot be skipped. Notifying PwC before these conditions are met is a policy violation.

- Field: SOW + both confirmations = gate for Specialty client notification

## BR: Employee Confirmation Is Mandatory — Employee Must Agree to Move

- The employee being considered for a Core move must personally confirm their agreement via the portal before the move proceeds. An employee who says no to a Core opportunity is not penalised. The BU Head may discuss with them but cannot override their decision without a formal HR process.

- Field: Employee fit confirmation required — cannot be overridden by RM or BU Head
