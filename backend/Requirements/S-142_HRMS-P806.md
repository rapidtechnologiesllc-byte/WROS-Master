## Why — Business Rationale

- R-03 is an absolute platform rule with no override authority: every BlitzenX hire is W2, full-time, no exceptions. Sub-vendors are the one channel where a C2C or 1099 candidate is most likely to slip in, because the vendor's own business model is built around exactly that kind of placement. This story is the one gate that makes R-03 actually unbreakable for sub-vendor submissions, rather than a policy a recruiter has to remember to check.

## What — Scope

- A hard validation gate that checks employment_type=FULL_TIME on every sub-vendor candidate submission before it can enter WROS at all — anything else is rejected automatically, logged, and the vendor is notified, with a three-strike escalation to compliance review.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-141 HRMS-P805 Sub-Vendor Submit Candidate Profile must be live — this story is the validation gate that sits immediately after submission.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Add the employment_type validation check

- On every sub-vendor submission, check employment_type field equals FULL_TIME exactly.

- Any other value (C2C, CONTRACT, 1099, or missing) triggers automatic rejection before the submission is persisted as a real record.

## Step 2: Build the rejection response

- Vendor sees an immediate rejection message explaining R-03: BlitzenX hires full-time only.

- Rejected submission is logged in sub_vendor_violations (not silently dropped) for compliance tracking purposes.

## Step 3: Wire the BlitzenX recruiter alert

- The recruiter who sent the original request is notified of the violation via HRMS-0113 — visibility into vendor behavior patterns, not just a vendor-side rejection.

## Step 4: Implement the three-strike compliance trigger

- Count violations per vendor_id over a rolling 90-day window.

- Third violation within the window auto-flags the vendor account for compliance review — feeds into S-147's governance agent, does not itself suspend the account (that threshold is 5, per S-147's compliance tracking).

## Business Rules

BR: BR-0806-01 — employment_type=FULL_TIME is validated server-side regardless of what the UI allows

- Even though the frontend dropdown offers only one option, the backend validates independently — a direct API call bypassing the UI cannot slip a non-compliant submission through.

- Field: Backend validation runs on every submission regardless of client-side dropdown constraints

BR: BR-0806-02 — Three violations in 90 days triggers compliance review, five triggers suspension

- A graduated response: repeated violations escalate rather than each rejection being treated identically.

- Field: sub_vendor_violations count over rolling 90-day window per vendor_id, thresholds at 3 and 5

## BR: BR-0806-03 — Rejections are logged, never silently dropped

- Every rejected submission is a compliance-relevant event and must be auditable, matching R-03's 'no override authority' status.

- Field: sub_vendor_violations row created on every rejection, including vendor_id, timestamp, submitted employment_type

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: A submission with employment_type other than FULL_TIME is rejected before any candidate record is created.

- AC-2: A direct API call bypassing the UI dropdown with a non-compliant employment_type is still rejected server-side.

- AC-3: The third violation for a vendor within 90 days triggers a compliance review flag, verified by seeding 3 violations and checking the flag.

- AC-4: Every rejection produces exactly one sub_vendor_violations row with full context.

## Not In Scope

- No override path for this gate at any role level — R-03 explicitly has no override authority, and this story does not create one.

- No partial acceptance or 'flag for review' path for a non-compliant submission — it's a hard reject, not a soft warning.
