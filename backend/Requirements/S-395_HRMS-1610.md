## Why — Business Rationale

- This is the correction to a mistake in this project's own gap-closure work — an earlier addendum stated Min Bill Rate as simply FLC × 1.25, omitting RM Burden entirely. Your actual locked formula includes it. Getting this specific number wrong isn't a cosmetic error — it's the one figure that directly determines whether a bill rate is allowed to be set at all under R-11, so it has to be exactly right before it gates anything.

## What — Scope

- The correct Min Bill Rate calculation — (Fully Loaded Cost + RM Burden) × the configured multiplier — feeding R-11's enforcement gate at every point in the platform where a bill rate gets entered.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-1608 and HRMS-1609 must both be live — this engine is purely their combination plus the multiplier.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the combined formula

- Min Bill Rate = (Fully Loaded Cost $/hr + RM Burden $/hr) × Multiplier.

- Using your worked example: ($12.88 + $0.541) × 1.25 = $16.78/hr.

- The multiplier itself (1.25 as currently stated, versus 1.19 needed to match your own reference sheet's $14.30 figure) is a configured placeholder per Section 16's open question 4 — not hardcoded, exactly like HRMS-1608's constants.

## Step 2: Wire the R-11 enforcement gate

- Retrofit R-11 (from the earlier gap-closure addendum) to reference this engine's output correctly — a bill rate entered below this employee's (or role's typical) Min Bill Rate triggers the logged-BU-Head-justification requirement, never a silent allow, at every entry point: S-236 Create Opportunity, S-239 Create Role Demand, S-301 Assign Resource.

## Step 3: Fix S-304's margin indicator reference

- S-304 Project Revenue Estimate & Margin Indicator should flag specifically when an assigned resource's bill rate is below their individual Min Bill Rate from this engine, not a generic margin estimate that doesn't reference this specific floor.

- The prior gap-closure addendum's formula (FLC × 1.25 alone) was incomplete — this story is the authoritative correction, and any code, documentation, or Claude Code build brief referencing the old formula should be updated to point here instead.

- Field: min_bill_rate_usd_cents = (fully_loaded_cost_usd_cents + rm_burden_per_hour_usd_cents) × multiplier_config

- Building with a silently-assumed 1.25 risks every downstream R-11 enforcement being wrong if the real answer turns out to be 1.19 to match the existing reference sheet — this must stay visibly flagged until confirmed.

- A below-floor bill rate is never silently allowed at any of the three entry points (S-236, S-239, S-301) — the logged BU Head justification requirement applies uniformly, not just wherever it was originally noticed.
