## Why — Business Rationale

- A sub-vendor candidate who's accepted, tagged, and engaged by Thunder still isn't fully 'in the system' until every standard ATS check that applies to a direct candidate — the 5-year experience gate (R-01), pipeline stage tracking, eligibility rules — has actually run against them too. This story is the explicit confirmation that a vendor-sourced candidate gets zero shortcuts through the platform's standard gates.

## What — Scope

- Confirmation and integration wiring ensuring every accepted sub-vendor candidate runs through the same ATS eligibility checks (R-01's 5-year experience gate included), gets the same pipeline stage tracking, and is otherwise indistinguishable in the ATS from a direct-sourced candidate.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-144, S-145, and S-152 must all be live — this story is the integration checkpoint after those three, not a new independent build.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Confirm R-01 5-year experience gate applies

- Verify createCandidateSafe()'s standard R-01 validation runs against sub-vendor candidates with no bypass — a vendor cannot submit an under-experienced candidate any more than a recruiter could source one directly.

## Step 2: Confirm standard pipeline stage tracking

- Sub-vendor sourced candidates move through the same submissions/interviews/offer pipeline stages, tracked identically to direct candidates — no parallel, lighter-weight pipeline.

## Step 3: Confirm profile completeness

- Full candidate profile (not just the fields captured at S-141 submission) gets built out through the standard Thunder qualification flow triggered by S-145, same as any candidate.

## Step 4: Build the integration verification report

- A one-time (per-release) audit query confirming zero sub-vendor candidates exist with a bypassed R-01 check or a non-standard pipeline stage set — a regression guard, not an ongoing operational screen.
