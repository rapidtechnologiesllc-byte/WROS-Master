## Why — Business Rationale

- A bill rate and a headcount on their own don't tell a BU Head what a role demand is actually worth. Multiplying it out — and rolling that up to the parent opportunity — is what turns individual role demands into a real revenue number the sales pipeline can be measured against.

## What — Scope

- Revenue potential per role demand — bill_rate × duration × quantity — stored in USD, aggregated back up to the parent opportunity's total revenue potential.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-239 must be live and producing role demands with bill_rate, duration, and quantity.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the per-role calculation

- revenue_potential = bill_rate_usd × duration_hours × quantity, converted and stored in USD cents per R-09.

## Step 2: Build the opportunity-level rollup

- Sum of all linked role demands' revenue_potential, displayed on the parent opportunity's detail view alongside its own directly-entered revenue_value from S-236 for comparison.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-0211-01 — Revenue potential is stored in USD cents per R-09, computed at duration/rate entry, recalculated on any change

- Not a one-time snapshot — if bill_rate or quantity is edited later, revenue_potential recalculates rather than staying stale.

- Field: revenue_potential_usd_cents recalculates on any write to bill_rate, duration, or quantity
