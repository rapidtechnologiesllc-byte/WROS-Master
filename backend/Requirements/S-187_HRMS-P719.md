## Why — Business Rationale

- A client's RFP today can arrive as a structured form, a pasted email, or an attached PDF — and every one of those needs to become the same thing on BlitzenX's side: a real Demand record a recruiter or AI sourcing agent can act on. Structured intake, document-upload extraction, and the handoff into an internal Demand were three stories, but they're one continuous flow a client experiences as a single action: submit the requirement, get a reference number, and BlitzenX starts working it.

## What — Scope

- A unified RFP intake flow: a structured form (title, role, skills, experience, headcount, dates, location, budget, priority) that a client can either fill in directly or pre-populate by uploading a PDF/DOCX RFP document (AI-extracted, low-confidence fields highlighted for confirmation), which on submit generates a reference number, alerts CS for a 4-hour review, and — once reviewed — creates the internal Demand record that AI sourcing (S-192) picks up.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-P702 Client Portal base access must be live.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the structured RFP form

- Fields: title, type, role, skills, experience, headcount, start date, duration, location (onsite/remote/hybrid), city, budget range, priority (Critical/High/Normal), notes, attachment.

## Step 2: Build the document upload + extraction path

- Client attaches a PDF/DOCX instead of manually filling every field.

- LLM extraction call populates role, skills, duration, location, start date within 30 seconds.

- Fields below a confidence threshold are visually flagged; client must confirm or correct every flagged field before submit — extraction pre-fills, it never auto-submits.

## Step 3: Generate reference number and alert CS

- On submit, a unique RFP reference number is generated and shown to the client.

- CS is notified with a 4-hour review SLA.

## Step 4: Build the CS-to-Demand handoff

- CS opens the RFP, reviews the (possibly AI-extracted) fields, and creates the corresponding internal Demand record in WROS — this is a deliberate human checkpoint between an external client submission and an internal actionable record, not an automatic pass-through.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0719-01 — Extraction pre-fills but never auto-submits

- The client always sees and can edit every extracted field before the RFP is actually submitted — the AI accelerates data entry, it doesn't remove the client's final say on their own requirement.

- Field: Submit button remains disabled until all LOW-confidence fields have been explicitly reviewed

## BR: BR-0719-02 — CS review is a mandatory human checkpoint before Demand creation

- An RFP submission does not automatically become an internal Demand — CS reviews it first, within a 4-hour SLA, ensuring a human catches anything the client's submission (structured or extracted) got wrong before sourcing effort is spent.

- Field: demand records are only created via the CS review action, never directly from RFP submission

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: A document upload completes extraction within 30 seconds for a typical single-page RFP brief.

- AC-2: Every LOW-confidence extracted field must be explicitly confirmed or corrected before the client can submit.

- AC-3: An RFP submission generates a unique reference number shown to the client immediately.

- AC-4: No Demand record is created until CS completes their review action — verified by checking demand_id is null immediately after client submission.

## Not In Scope

- No automatic Demand creation directly from client RFP submission — CS review is a deliberate, non-bypassable checkpoint.

- No multi-document RFP merging in this story — one RFP submission corresponds to one attached document at most.
