## Why — Business Rationale

- HRMS-1107's Project Health Monitoring Agent already computes a composite health score from four signal sources — this story is what makes two of those signal sources (staffing gaps and milestone delays) properly visible and explorable in the project delivery context itself, not just consumed invisibly by the agent's scoring formula.

## What — Scope

- A project-level risk flagging view surfacing staffing gaps (from S-303), milestone delays (from S-302), and client escalations, alongside HRMS-1107's already-computed health score — with a BU Head alert when the score goes red.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-303 and S-302 must be live and producing the signal data.

- HRMS-1107 (already built in EPIC-11) must be live and computing health scores that read from this data.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the project risk detail view

- Shows HRMS-1107's health_score and status prominently, with the underlying staffing-gap and milestone-delay signals right below it — not hidden behind a separate agent-only screen.

## Step 2: Wire the client escalation signal

- Logged escalations (from S-193's client messaging, topic=General with an escalation flag, or a dedicated escalation log) feed as the fourth visible risk signal alongside HRMS-1107's other three.

## Step 3: Confirm the BU Head alert wiring

- HRMS-1107 already alerts on CRITICAL status per its own business rules — this story confirms that alert links directly into this project's risk detail view, not just a bare notification.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0807-01 — This story displays HRMS-1107's score, it does not recompute it

- Avoids a second, potentially divergent health-scoring implementation — HRMS-1107 remains the single source of truth for the composite score, this is its project-delivery-context presentation.

- Field: health_score displayed here is read directly from HRMS-1107's project_health_scores table, not recalculated
