## Why — Business Rationale

- A vendor who submits a candidate and hears nothing back has no way to know whether to keep that candidate warm, submit them elsewhere, or move on. Real-time status visibility is what keeps a sub-vendor relationship productive — a vendor blind to outcomes stops sending BlitzenX their best candidates first.

## What — Scope

- A vendor-facing status view showing every submission's current state through its full lifecycle — Submitted, Under Review, Accepted, Rejected, or More Info Requested — with recruiter feedback always visible on a rejection.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-144 HRMS-P808 must be live — this story displays the state it produces, it doesn't originate any state changes itself.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the status list view

- Vendor sees all their historical submissions across all requests, sorted by most recent activity.

- Status badge: Submitted / Under Review / Accepted / Rejected / More Info Requested.

## Step 2: Wire rejection feedback display

- A Rejected submission always shows the recruiter's feedback note from S-144 — never a bare rejection with no explanation.

## Step 3: Wire real-time updates

- Status changes reflect immediately via the notification hooks already built in S-151/S-147's governance agent, not a manual refresh-only view.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0810-01 — Rejection is never shown without feedback

- Matches S-144's BR-0808-02 mandatory feedback rule — this view is where that feedback actually reaches the vendor.

- Field: UI renders the feedback_note field alongside every Rejected status, never a bare status alone
