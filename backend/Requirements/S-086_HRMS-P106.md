## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

The Messages tab in the candidate portal is where the candidate experiences their relationship with Thunder. It must show the complete conversation history across ALL channels — WhatsApp, email, and portal messages — in one chronological thread. The candidate sent a WhatsApp message on Monday and an email on Wednesday — they see both in one place, and Thunder's reply reflects both.

The candidate replies from the portal and that message appears in the same thread. This is the same unified timeline as the recruiter sees in HRMS-0405 — just the candidate-facing version.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build the candidate Messages page at /candidate/{token}/messages. Shows: (1) Full cross-channel conversation timeline (all channels). (2) Reply input at bottom. (3) Channel icons showing which channel each message came from. (4) Thunder's avatar (lightning bolt). Mobile-first.

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Show ALL Channels in One Thread — No Channel Filtering

The candidate portal shows the same unified conversation_messages that the recruiter sees — all channels in one timeline. A candidate who sent a WhatsApp and then received an email reply sees both in the same thread in the order they were sent. There are NO separate tabs for WhatsApp vs email.

↳ Triggered by field: All channels unified — no filtering

## BR-02: Candidate Reply Always Goes to Portal Channel — Thunder Routes Back on Preferred

When the candidate types in the portal reply box: the message is stored with channel=PORTAL. Thunder's response goes to the candidate's preferred channel (HRMS-0469 detected preference) — which may be WhatsApp or email. The portal shows all of Thunder's responses regardless of which channel Thunder used.

↳ Triggered by field: reply = PORTAL. Thunder response = preferred channel

## BR-03: Thunder's Response Reads FULL Cross-Channel History

When the candidate sends a portal reply and Thunder responds, Thunder's response is generated using the FULL conversation history (HRMS-P104 context awareness). Thunder does not just read the last portal message — it reads the complete conversation including prior WhatsApp and email exchanges. The candidate experiences continuity.

↳ Triggered by field: HRMS-P104 + HRMS-0432 full context always
