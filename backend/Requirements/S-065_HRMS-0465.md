## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

A recruiter starts their day and needs to know: what did Thunder do overnight? How many candidates responded? Who needs attention today? Who is at risk? Getting this as a structured WhatsApp + email briefing every morning means the recruiter hits the ground running with full situational awareness — without opening WROS and manually checking 30 candidate profiles.

This is Thunder reporting to the recruiter every morning, like a briefing from a diligent overnight team member.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build: (1) DigestGenerationJob runs at configured morning time (default 8 AM local recruiter time). (2) Generates a personalized digest per recruiter showing their overnight Thunder activity, candidates needing attention, interviews today, and top risks. (3) Sends via WhatsApp + Email. (4) Includes direct links back to WROS.

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Build DigestGenerationJob

- Runs at 8:00 AM in each recruiter's local timezone (from recruiter user profile)

- For each active recruiter in tenant: generate personalized digest (Step 2)

- Config in system_configuration: digest_send_time (default '08:00'), digest_enabled BOOLEAN

## Step 2: Build generateDailyDigest() function

- generateDailyDigest(recruiter_user_id, tenant_id) → { digest_sections }

- Section 1 — OVERNIGHT ACTIVITY: count of Thunder actions since 6 PM yesterday. 'Thunder sent 12 messages, qualified 2 candidates, scheduled 1 interview, and detected 1 ghosting.'

- Section 2 — CANDIDATES WHO RESPONDED: list candidates who replied to Thunder overnight. Name + what they said (brief). Links to their profiles.

- Section 3 — NEEDS YOUR ATTENTION TODAY: items from recruiter_intervention_queue with status=OPEN, ordered by priority. Max 5 items. 'John Smith: Escalated (requested human contact). Sarah Lee: Critical Drop Risk (82). Mike Chen: SLA Breach (31h no contact).'

- Section 4 — INTERVIEWS TODAY: all interviews with scheduled_at between today 00:00 and today 23:59 in recruiter's timezone. Shows candidate name, time in local TZ, interview level (L1/L2), interviewer name.

- Section 5 — TOP RISKS: top 3 candidates by drop_risk_score. Name + score + top signal.

## Step 3: Build WhatsApp digest message

- Format for WhatsApp (character limit, no HTML): plain text with emojis for section headers

- 📊 *THUNDER MORNING DIGEST — [Date]*

⚡ *Overnight:* 12 messages sent, 2 qualified, 1 interview booked.

📨 *Responded:* John Smith (resume sent), Sarah Lee (notice: 30 days)

🚨 *Needs You:* Mike Chen (escalated), ... [up to 5]

📅 *Interviews Today:* Jane Doe — L1 — 2PM CST with Tom K.

⚠️ *Top Risks:* Alex M (82), Chris P (74), Dana S (65)

View full dashboard: [WROS link]

- Send via WhatsApp to recruiter's registered phone number

## Step 4: Build Email digest

- HTML email with same 5 sections, better formatted with buttons and tables

- Subject: '⚡ Thunder Morning Digest — [Date] | [X] items need your attention'

- Each candidate name in sections is a hyperlink to their WROS profile

- 'View Full Dashboard' button at bottom

- Send from thunder@blitzenx.com

- Thunder email signature included

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Digest Sent in Recruiter's Local Timezone

The 8:00 AM send time is 8:00 AM in the recruiter's own timezone (from their user profile). A recruiter in India gets their digest at 8 AM IST. One in the US gets it at 8 AM EST. Use recruiter.timezone from the user profile.

↳ Triggered by field: recruiter.timezone

## BR-02: No Digest Sent If No Activity

If Thunder had zero activity overnight AND the intervention queue is empty AND no interviews today: do not send the digest. An empty digest adds noise. Minimum threshold: at least one of: N > 0 activity events, queue items, or interviews today.

↳ Triggered by field: minimum_content threshold

## BR-03: Digest Must Include Direct WROS Links

Every candidate name in the digest (WhatsApp or email) must be a direct link to their WROS profile MESSAGES tab. Recruiters must be able to take action in one tap from the digest.

↳ Triggered by field: WROS deep links
