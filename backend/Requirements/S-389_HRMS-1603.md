## Why — Business Rationale

- Same as how BlitzenX works in Tally today — payment confirmation is a human action, not an automated bank-feed detection, since WROS explicitly doesn't have a live ERP/bank integration. This keeps the trigger for partner incentives unambiguous: an invoice is paid the moment Accounting says it is, full stop, not inferred from anything else.

## What — Scope

- A simple, explicit Finance/Accounting action that transitions a Sent invoice to Paid, capturing the payment date — this is the sole trigger for S-226's Paid status and, downstream, for partner incentive calculation.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0907 (S-226) must be live — this story only adds the Paid-marking action to its existing status lifecycle.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the mark-as-paid action

- Available only on invoices currently in Sent status, per S-226's existing lifecycle — cannot mark a Draft or Approved (not-yet-sent) invoice as paid, since that would skip the client-visible Sent step entirely.

- Requires payment_date (the date payment was actually received, which may differ from the date someone got around to marking it in WROS) and an optional reference note.

## Step 2: Wire the incentive-calculation trigger

- The moment an invoice transitions to Paid, it becomes eligible for the next Partner Incentive Calculator run (HRMS-1611) — eligibility is based on payment_date falling within the relevant period, not on when the mark-as-paid action itself was clicked, so a late data-entry doesn't distort which month's incentive run it belongs to.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-1603-01 — Only Sent-status invoices can be marked paid

- Prevents skipping the client-visible Sent step entirely — an invoice must have actually been sent before it can be considered received and paid.

- Field: Mark-as-paid action is disabled/rejected for any invoice not currently in Sent status

BR: BR-1603-02 — Incentive eligibility uses payment_date, not the action's click timestamp

- A payment received on the 28th but entered into WROS on the 3rd of the next month should count toward the period it was actually received in, not the period it happened to be logged in — otherwise incentive timing becomes dependent on Accounting's data-entry promptness rather than actual payment timing.

- Field: Partner Incentive Calculator (HRMS-1611) filters on payment_date, never on the mark-as-paid row's created_at

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: An attempt to mark a Draft or Approved invoice as paid is rejected — only Sent-status invoices are eligible.

- AC-2: An invoice's incentive-period eligibility is determined by its payment_date, not by when the mark-as-paid action was actually performed, verified by entering a payment several days after the fact and confirming it's attributed to the correct earlier period.
