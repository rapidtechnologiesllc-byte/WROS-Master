## Why — Business Rationale

- A candidate sourced through a vendor shouldn't experience a worse or different journey than a candidate sourced directly — the moment a recruiter accepts them, they become a first-class WROS candidate, engaged by Thunder exactly the same way, on exactly the same timeline. Treating vendor-sourced candidates as second-tier would slow down BlitzenX's own placement speed for no reason.

## What — Scope

- Automatic Thunder engagement triggered the moment a recruiter accepts a sub-vendor submission — the candidate enters the standard qualification pipeline via WhatsApp and email, indistinguishable from any other candidate's experience.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-144 must have produced an ACCEPTED candidate record via createCandidateSafe().

- HRMS-0412 Thunder's standard engagement trigger must be callable for a newly-created candidate record, same as any direct-sourced candidate.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Wire the acceptance event to Thunder

- On candidate.created event with source_channel=SUBVENDOR, trigger the same Thunder first-touch flow used for any newly created candidate.

## Step 2: Confirm consent capture happens identically

- Consent is captured through the same flow as any candidate — sub-vendor sourcing does not create a shortcut around consent requirements.

## Step 3: Confirm the candidate has no visibility into the vendor relationship

- Nothing in Thunder's messaging references the sub-vendor — the candidate's experience is identical to being sourced directly.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-0809-01 — Sub-vendor sourced candidates receive identical Thunder treatment to any other candidate

- No differentiated qualification flow, timing, or messaging based on source_channel — the only difference anywhere in the system is the internal source tag, invisible to the candidate.

- Field: Thunder engagement trigger logic contains no source_channel branching

## BR: BR-0809-02 — Consent capture is not bypassed for vendor-sourced candidates

- Matches platform-wide consent policy — a vendor's own data handling with the candidate does not substitute for WROS's own consent capture.

- Field: candidate.consent_given follows the same capture flow regardless of source_channel

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: An accepted sub-vendor candidate receives a Thunder first-touch message within the same SLA window as any other newly created candidate.

- AC-2: No message content generated for a sub-vendor sourced candidate references the vendor or the sourcing channel.

- AC-3: Consent capture completes through the identical flow used for direct-sourced candidates.
