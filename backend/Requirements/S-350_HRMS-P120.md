## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

Thunder is doing most of the work. But when a human recruiter or HR needs to step in — for a salary negotiation, a critical closing call, an offer acceptance push — they need to walk into that conversation fully briefed. Without Thunder's intelligence, the human asks the same questions Thunder already knows the answers to, misses the candidate's real motivation, and fails to address their actual fears.

The HR Intelligence Briefing gives humans a complete, actionable briefing before every human touchpoint: exactly what this candidate wants, what they fear, what has been tried, what works, and the specific talking points that will move this specific candidate toward YES.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Build INTELLIGENCE tab on candidate profile (HR/Director role only)

- Add 'INTELLIGENCE' tab to candidate profile page — visible ONLY to HR and Director roles. Recruiters do not see this tab.

- Tab order for HR: PROFILE | MESSAGES | INTELLIGENCE | FEEDBACK | DOCUMENTS | TASKS | ACTIVITY

- This tab is the briefing room — everything HR needs before engaging the candidate directly.

## Step 2: Build Desire Profile visualization

- Horizontal bar chart showing each desire category and its score (0-1.0)

- Color coding: green=TOWARDS desires, red=AWAY_FROM (fears). Score shown as bar length.

- Example: CAREER_GROWTH ████████ 0.91 (green) | REMOTE_FLEXIBILITY █████ 0.62 (green) | INSTABILITY ████ 0.55 (red) | COMPENSATION ███ 0.41 (green)

- Engagement level badge: HOT 🔥 / WARM ☀️ / COOL 🌤️ / COLD ❄️

- Competing offer alert: red banner 'COMPETING OFFER DETECTED — Candidate is considering another opportunity. Urgency: CRITICAL'

## Step 3: Build Thunder Narrative Briefing section

- Show candidate_desire_profiles.narrative_summary (the LLM-generated 3-paragraph briefing for HR)

- Label: 'Thunder's Analysis — Updated [X hours ago]'

- Three labeled paragraphs: 'What They Want', 'What They Fear', 'Recommended Approach'

- Refresh button: re-generates narrative via generateDesireNarrative() — for cases where HR wants fresh analysis before a call

## Step 4: Build HR Talking Points generator

- Below narrative: 'Talking Points for Your Next Conversation' section

- Auto-generated from desire profile + content library: 3-5 specific bullet points HR should raise

- Format: each point is one sentence. Example: '✓ Lead with: BlitzenX promoted 3 Guidewire consultants to Technical Lead in 2024 — his top desire is career growth.' or '⚠ Address: His relocation concern — confirm the role is hybrid before the call.'

- Generated by LLM TALKING_POINTS prompt. Regenerated every time desire profile updates.

## Step 5: Build Thunder Motivation History section

- Show all proactive motivation messages Thunder sent (from motivation_outcomes table)

- Per message: date, trigger_type, desire_category_targeted, message preview (first 80 chars), outcome (Replied within 24h / No response / Offer accepted after).

- HR sees what Thunder tried and what worked — preventing them from repeating Thunder's attempts

- Last item: 'Thunder's last motivation message: [2 days ago]. Response: No reply. Recommended: Try a personal phone call addressing career growth.'

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: INTELLIGENCE Tab Visible to HR and Director Only

Recruiter role must not see the Desire Profile or Narrative Briefing. This information is strategic intelligence used for closing decisions and salary negotiations. Recruiters see the standard MESSAGES tab. HR and Director see the INTELLIGENCE tab.

↳ Triggered by field: HR and Director role only — HTTP 403 for recruiter accessing API

## BR-02: Competing Offer Alert Triggers Immediate HR Notification

When has_competing_offer=true is set on a candidate's desire profile: immediately notify the assigned HR manager and Director via WhatsApp + email: '[Candidate Name] has a competing offer. Their desire profile shows CAREER_GROWTH as top desire. Full briefing available in WROS INTELLIGENCE tab.'

↳ Triggered by field: has_competing_offer → immediate HR + Director notification

## BR-03: Talking Points Use Only Library Facts — Never Fabricated

The TALKING_POINTS prompt uses the same content library as HRMS-P119. HR talking points must be grounded in real BlitzenX facts. The system explicitly instructs the LLM not to invent statistics or make promises.

↳ Triggered by field: content_library facts only in talking points
