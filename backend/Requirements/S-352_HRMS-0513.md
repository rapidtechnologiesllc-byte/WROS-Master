## Why — Business Rationale

- The Core Eligibility Gate is the most important workflow in the talent engine. It is the moment an employee transitions from proving themselves in Speciality to being trusted to represent BlitzenX directly with carrier clients. This transition must never be casual, fast-tracked, or influenced by headcount pressure. It requires: AI evidence from the full performance store, RM recommendation, and BU Head final approval — all logged, all auditable, all mandatory.

## What — Scope

- Build the two-step Core Eligibility approval workflow. RM initiates review → AI Assessor generates scorecard → RM logs recommendation → BU Head gives final approval → engine changes to CORE. Every step logged. No shortcuts.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0512 engine model. HRMS-0515 performance store with minimum 90 days data. HRMS-0518 AI Assessor. HRMS-0522 90-day clock showing CERTIFIED.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Create core_eligibility_reviews table

- CREATE TABLE core_eligibility_reviews: id UUID PK, tenant_id UUID, employee_id UUID FK, initiated_by UUID (RM user_id), initiated_at TIMESTAMP, ai_recommendation ENUM('ELIGIBLE','NOT_YET','BORDERLINE'), ai_confidence_score INTEGER (0-100), ai_evidence_summary TEXT, ai_risk_flags JSONB, rm_recommendation ENUM('RECOMMEND','DO_NOT_RECOMMEND','DEFER'), rm_recommendation_notes TEXT, rm_recommended_at TIMESTAMP, bu_head_decision ENUM('APPROVED','REJECTED','DEFERRED'), bu_head_decision_notes TEXT, bu_head_user_id UUID, bu_head_decided_at TIMESTAMP, status ENUM('AI_PENDING','RM_REVIEW','BU_HEAD_REVIEW','APPROVED','REJECTED','DEFERRED')

## Step 2: Build eligibility initiation flow

- POST /api/core-eligibility/initiate: body { employee_id }. Auth: RM only.

- Validates: (a) employee.buddy_program_status=GRADUATED, (b) specialty_days_billable >= 90 (or 365 for HTD), (c) no active escalations of type RESOURCE_ERROR in last 30 days.

- Creates core_eligibility_reviews record with status=AI_PENDING.

- Triggers HRMS-0518 AI Assessor asynchronously.

## Step 3: Build RM review step

- On AI assessment complete: status=RM_REVIEW. Notify RM.

- RM reviews AI scorecard on /admin/employees/{id}/core-eligibility/{review_id}.

- PATCH /api/core-eligibility/{id}/rm-recommend: body { recommendation, notes (min 50 chars) }. Status → BU_HEAD_REVIEW. Notify BU Head.

## Step 4: Build BU Head final approval

- BU Head reviews full package: AI scorecard + RM recommendation on /admin/core-eligibility/{review_id}.

- PATCH /api/core-eligibility/{id}/bu-decide: body { decision, notes }. Auth: BU Head only.

- On APPROVED: set employees.delivery_engine=CORE, core_certified=TRUE, core_certified_date=NOW(), engine_entry_date=NOW(). Log in employee_engine_history.

- On REJECTED/DEFERRED: employee stays in SPECIALITY. Notes logged in performance store. RM notified.

## Business Rules

## BR: Cannot Initiate Review Unless 90-Day Clock Shows CERTIFIED

- HRMS-0522 90-Day Certification Clock must show status=CERTIFIED before core eligibility review can be initiated. System validates this and blocks initiation if not met. HTD track requires 365 days.

- Field: 90-day/365-day gate before eligibility review

## BR: Both RM and BU Head Decisions Are Mandatory — No Skip

- Status machine enforces sequential steps. BU Head cannot approve without RM recommendation logged. RM cannot recommend without AI scorecard complete. No step can be skipped.

- Field: Sequential mandatory steps — no bypass

## BR: Full Audit Trail Retained Permanently

- core_eligibility_reviews records are never deleted. Every approved, rejected, and deferred review is permanently retained for compliance. This is the legal record of why an employee was or was not certified.

- Field: Permanent immutable audit record

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: Review cannot initiate without 90-day clock CERTIFIED

- AC-2: AI scorecard generated within 5 minutes of initiation

- AC-3: RM recommendation requires 50+ char notes

- AC-4: BU Head approval sets delivery_engine=CORE atomically

- AC-5: Rejected/deferred: employee stays SPECIALITY, notes in performance store

- AC-6: Full review trail permanently retained
