## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

A candidate does not join the interview at the scheduled time. Thunder waits 15 minutes, sends a WhatsApp check-in, waits another 15 minutes for a response, and then formally logs the no-show and notifies the recruiter. This structured approach is far better than leaving the interviewer waiting indefinitely with no communication from the recruiting team.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build: (1) NoShowDetectionJob that fires 15 minutes after interview start time. (2) Thunder's check-in message. (3) No-show logging after 30 minutes of no response. (4) Recruiter notification. (5) Optional reschedule offer to candidate.

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Build NoShowDetectionJob (runs every 5 minutes)

- SELECT * FROM interviews WHERE status='CONFIRMED' AND scheduled_at_utc <= NOW() - INTERVAL '15 minutes' AND scheduled_at_utc >= NOW() - INTERVAL '90 minutes'

- For each interview in this window: check if candidate sent ANY inbound message after scheduled_at_utc. If yes (candidate checked in): skip.

- If no inbound message after scheduled_at_utc: this candidate may be a no-show. Send check-in message (Step 2).

- UPDATE interviews SET status='NO_SHOW_CHECK_IN_SENT', no_show_check_in_at=NOW()

## Step 2: Send Thunder's check-in message

- Thunder sends via WhatsApp (fastest channel): 'Hi [name]! Your interview with [Interviewer Name] was scheduled for [time]. Are you still joining? Please let us know and we can hold a few more minutes for you.'

- Send simultaneously via email as backup

- Store in conversation_messages with message_type='NO_SHOW_CHECK_IN'

## Step 3: Log confirmed no-show 30 minutes after interview start

- Second pass of NoShowDetectionJob: candidates with status='NO_SHOW_CHECK_IN_SENT' AND no_show_check_in_at <= NOW() - INTERVAL '15 minutes' with still no inbound reply

- UPDATE interviews SET status='NO_SHOW', no_show_confirmed_at=NOW()

- Log conversation_events: INTERVIEW_NO_SHOW { interview_id, candidate_id, scheduled_at_utc }

- Notify recruiter via HRMS-0113: '[Candidate Name] did not join their [L1/L2] interview at [time]. Please decide next steps.'

- Notify interviewer via email: 'The candidate did not join. We are following up with them now. We will reschedule if appropriate.'

## Step 4: Offer reschedule to candidate (optional path)

- After no-show logged: Thunder sends one more message 2 hours later: 'We missed you today! If something came up, we completely understand. Would you like to reschedule your interview? Please let us know and we can find another time.'

- If candidate replies: route to HRMS-0451 reschedule flow

- If no reply to this message either within 48 hours: update interview.status='NO_SHOW_NO_RESPONSE'. Recruiter decides to disqualify or give another chance.

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Wait Full 30 Minutes Before Logging No-Show

Check-in at 15 min, confirm no-show at 30 min. Some candidates join late. 30 minutes is the firm cutoff. Do not log no-show before 30 minutes have elapsed since scheduled_at_utc.

↳ Triggered by field: 30 minutes from scheduled_at_utc

## BR-02: Recruiter Makes Final Decision After No-Show

Thunder flags the no-show and offers a reschedule. The recruiter decides whether to give the candidate another interview opportunity or disqualify them. Thunder does not auto-disqualify on no-show.
