## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

The Resource Manager needs to see at a glance: who is on bench, how long they've been there, what the bench cost is accumulating to, who is allocated to what project, and where there are upcoming gaps. Without this dashboard, RM spends hours on phone calls and spreadsheets instead of making fast staffing decisions.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build RM Dashboard at /rm/dashboard. Five sections: (1) Bench Pool summary. (2) Utilization heatmap by employee. (3) NO MARKET SUBMISSION violation alerts. (4) Upcoming allocation gaps. (5) Demand vs bench supply.

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Build GET /api/rm/dashboard endpoint

- Auth: RM role. Returns: { bench_summary: { total_on_bench, bench_cost_month_usd, avg_days_on_bench, aging_alerts: count > 30 days }, utilization: [{ employee_name, utilization_pct, billable_hours, allocated_projects }], violation_alerts: [{ type, candidate_name, attempted_at, blocked_by }] (NO MARKET attempts), allocation_gaps: [{ project, role, gap_start_date, days_until_gap }], demand_vs_supply: { open_demands: N, bench_available: N, match_pct } }

## Step 2: Build RM Dashboard page

- Route: /rm/dashboard. Auth: RM role.

- Card 1 — Bench Summary: 4 metrics: Total on bench, Monthly cost (USD), Avg days on bench, [N] aging >30 days (amber alert).

- Card 2 — Utilization Heatmap: table per employee. Color: green=80%+, amber=50-79%, red<50%. Click employee → their allocation history.

- Card 3 — Policy Violations: list of attempted NO MARKET SUBMISSION violations. Shows who tried, when, which candidate, blocked by system. RM visibility only — not shown to recruiters.

- Card 4 — Upcoming Gaps: projects with resource gaps starting in next 30 days. Sorted by days_until_gap ASC (most urgent first).

- Card 5 — Demand vs Supply: bar chart showing open demands vs available bench candidates. Match % shown.

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

## BR-01: NO MARKET SUBMISSION Violations Visible to RM Only

Violation attempts are logged and shown to the RM but NOT to the recruiter who attempted them. The recruiter sees only the block message. The RM sees the full audit trail for compliance purposes.

↳ Triggered by field: Violation visibility: RM only
