## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

A candidate has an interview scheduled. They want to know: when exactly, who is interviewing them, in what format, and what to prepare. Without the Interview Tracker, they can only find this in a WhatsApp or email message from Thunder — which they have to scroll back to find. The Interview Tracker gives them a permanent, always-accessible reference.

It also provides a 'Add to Calendar' ICS download, a reschedule request button, and prep tips — all in one place.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build the Interview page at /candidate/{token}/interview. Shows: upcoming and past interviews with times in candidate local timezone, ICS download, reschedule request form, and prep tips.

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Build Interview Tracker page

- Route: /candidate/:token/interview

- If no interview exists: show 'No interview scheduled yet. Thunder will be in touch once your profile is reviewed.'

- If interview(s) exist: show upcoming first, then past

- Interview card shows: Level (L1 Technical / L2 Technical), Date + Time in CANDIDATE's local timezone (critical — show 'Tuesday January 21 at 2:00 PM CST'), Format ('Video Call' or 'Phone'), Interviewer name and title, Meeting link (if video)

## Step 2: Build ICS file generation

- For each upcoming interview: 'Add to Calendar' button generates .ics file on click

- ICS content: DTSTART + DTEND in UTC, SUMMARY: 'BlitzenX Interview — [Level]', DESCRIPTION: 'Your [L1/L2] interview with [Interviewer Name] at BlitzenX. [Meeting link]. For questions contact thunder@blitzenx.com', ORGANIZER: CN=Thunder MAILTO:thunder@blitzenx.com

- Download as interview_[date].ics. Works for iOS/Android/Google/Outlook calendar.

## Step 3: Build reschedule request form

- 'Request Reschedule' button below each upcoming interview card

- Click opens inline form: 'Please share 2-3 alternative time slots you are available' — textarea input

- On submit: POST to /api/portal/candidates/{id}/reschedule-request with interview_id + candidate_message

- Backend: store request, notify recruiter via HRMS-0113, Thunder sends acknowledgment to candidate via preferred channel

- Show confirmation: 'Your reschedule request has been submitted. Thunder will be in touch with new options shortly.'

## Step 4: Build preparation tips section

- Below interview card: expandable 'Preparation Tips' section

- Content from system_configuration INTERVIEW_PREP_TIPS (BA-approved static text)

- Generic tips: 'Review your resume', 'Be ready to discuss your Guidewire experience', 'Test your video link 10 minutes before', 'Have examples of past projects ready'

- L1-specific tips can differ from L2 — configurable per interview_type

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Interview Time Always in Candidate's Local Timezone

The interview time displayed to the candidate is ALWAYS in candidate_timezone from the interview record. Never show UTC. Never show the interviewer's timezone. If a candidate is in India (IST) and interviewer is in US (EST): show 'Tuesday January 21, 11:30 PM IST' to the candidate.

↳ Triggered by field: candidate_timezone (from interview record)

## BR-02: Reschedule Request Goes to Both Thunder and Recruiter

When candidate submits reschedule request via portal: (1) Store the request. (2) Thunder sends acknowledgment message to candidate via preferred channel. (3) Recruiter notified. (4) HRMS-0451 reschedule workflow is triggered by the recruiter, not automatically.

↳ Triggered by field: reschedule request flow
