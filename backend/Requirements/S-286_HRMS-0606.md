## Why — Business Rationale

- After adhoc submission, scheduling intro call takes 24-48h of email back-and-forth. Rapid mode auto-proposes 3 slots and sends client a one-click scheduling link — no email chain.

## What — Scope

- RapidScheduler: auto-generates 3 mutual available slots within 48h and sends scheduling link to client. Client one-click confirms. Calendar invites sent to all parties.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0604 submission. HRMS-1306 calendar. Client contact from HRMS-0102.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build RapidScheduler

- Finds 3 mutual 30-min slots in next 48h. Creates scheduling_requests: proposed_slots, token, expires_at=NOW()+24h. Sends email+WhatsApp to client.

## Step 2: One-click scheduling page

- Route: /schedule/{token} (public, token-protected). Client picks slot → interview created via HRMS-0449. Calendar invites sent. Expired link: new slots auto-generated.
