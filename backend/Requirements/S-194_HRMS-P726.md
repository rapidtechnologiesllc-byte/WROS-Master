## Why — Business Rationale

- A client shouldn't have to log into the portal daily to find out a new candidate was submitted or a milestone updated — the platform should tell them. This is the client-facing counterpart to the internal HRMS-0113 engine: the same discipline (multi-channel, configurable) applied outward.

## What — Scope

- Automatic client notifications on new submission, interview confirmed, timesheet submitted, RFP status change, and milestone update — portal and email always, WhatsApp if the Client Admin has configured it.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0113 Notification Engine Base must be live — this story is the client-facing configuration layer on top of it, not a separate delivery mechanism.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Wire the trigger events

- New submission, interview confirmed, timesheet submitted, RFP status change, milestone update — each calls HRMS-0113 with client-scoped recipients.

## Step 2: Build the Client Admin preference screen

- Per-event-type channel toggle: Portal (always on, cannot disable), Email (always on, cannot disable), WhatsApp (opt-in).

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0726-01 — Portal and Email notifications cannot be disabled

- These are the baseline guarantee that a client always has a record of platform activity even if they never configured WhatsApp — only the WhatsApp channel is optional.

- Field: Preference UI renders Portal and Email as locked-on, only WhatsApp is a real toggle

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: A new submission event triggers a client notification via Portal and Email regardless of configuration.

- AC-2: WhatsApp delivery only occurs for clients who have explicitly opted in.

- AC-3: Every one of the 5 event types (submission, interview, timesheet, RFP status, milestone) triggers correctly on its corresponding action.
