## Why — Business Rationale

- Sourcing against an ambiguous requirement wastes both AI and human recruiter effort — better to resolve the ambiguity with the client first than to submit candidates that miss the mark and have to start over. This story is what makes sure that pause actually happens rather than sourcing racing ahead of an unresolved question.

## What — Scope

- A structured clarification request flow — CS asks a question on an RFP, the client responds in the portal, and the RFP stays in Under Review with sourcing explicitly blocked until CS marks all clarifications resolved.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-187 must be live — clarifications are raised against an already-submitted RFP.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the CS clarification request UI

- CS raises a specific question against the RFP from their review screen.

## Step 2: Notify the client and build their response flow

- Client sees the question in-portal, responds; CS and the assigned recruiter see the response immediately.

## Step 3: Wire the sourcing gate

- RFP status remains Under Review, and S-192's AI Auto-Sourcing does not start, until CS explicitly marks Clarification Complete — this is a hard block, not a soft recommendation.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0723-01 — Sourcing is hard-blocked while any clarification remains open

- S-192's AI Auto-Sourcing checks this flag before starting — an open clarification is not a suggestion to wait, it's an enforced gate.

- Field: S-192 sourcing trigger checks rfp_submissions.clarification_complete == true before initiating any search

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: A raised clarification notifies the client and keeps the RFP in Under Review status.

- AC-2: S-192's AI sourcing does not start while clarification_complete is false, verified by attempting to trigger sourcing with an open clarification.

- AC-3: Marking Clarification Complete is a CS-only action, not automatic on client response.
