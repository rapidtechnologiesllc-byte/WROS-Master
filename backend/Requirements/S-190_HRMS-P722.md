## Why — Business Rationale

- A client who's submitted a requirement and hears nothing for a week starts calling their CS contact asking for an update — time that CS could spend actually sourcing. A clear, always-current status view answers 'where's my requirement' before the client has to ask.

## What — Scope

- A client-facing RFP detail view showing status progression (Received → Under Review → Sourcing → Candidates Being Prepared → Submitted → Closed) with full history, timestamps, candidates-submitted count, next action, and the assigned CS contact — with a notification on every status change.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-187 must be live and producing RFP records with a status field this view can display.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the status timeline UI

- Visual progression through the 6 status stages, with timestamps at each transition.

## Step 2: Build the detail panel

- Candidates submitted count, next action description, assigned CS contact with direct message link (into S-193).

## Step 3: Wire status-change notifications

- Every transition triggers an HRMS-0113 notification, both email and in-portal.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0722-01 — Status displayed always matches the authoritative backend state exactly

- No client-side caching or approximation of status — this view reads live state, since a stale status is worse than no status at all for managing client expectations.

- Field: Status view queries current rfp_submissions.status directly, no client-side derived state

## Integrations

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: The status timeline always reflects the current backend status with no lag beyond standard page load.

- AC-2: Every status transition produces both an email and in-portal notification to the client.

- AC-3: The candidates-submitted count matches the actual count of submissions linked to that RFP's resulting Demand.
