## Why — Business Rationale

- Bench-first is one of BlitzenX's 10 hard business rules. It must be enforced at the planning layer as well as the execution layer. This story builds the policy engine that validates bench check results before any external sourcing is authorized — preventing accidental bypasses where recruiter creates external hire demand without checking bench first.

## What — Scope

- Build the Bench-First Policy Engine: a hard gate that must be passed before any external sourcing is authorized. Logs every bench check. Blocks external sourcing unless bench check confirms insufficient supply.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0503 bench pool. HRMS-0506 staffing eligibility. HRMS-0306 gap analysis.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build BenchFirstPolicyEngine

- BenchFirstPolicyEngine.check(demand_id): queries bench pool for employees matching demand skills and delivery engine. Returns: { bench_candidates: [{ employee_id, name, skill_match_pct, bench_duration_days }], bench_sufficient: BOOLEAN, check_timestamp: TIMESTAMP, gap_remaining: INTEGER }

- Creates bench_first_check record: demand_id, bench_candidates_found, bench_sufficient, checked_at, override_requested, override_approved_by nullable, override_justification nullable.

- External sourcing can only proceed when: (a) bench_sufficient=false, OR (b) bench_sufficient=true but override approved by RM with justification logged.

## Step 2: Build override flow

- If bench has candidates but RM wants to proceed with external hire anyway (e.g. bench candidate is Core-eligible and needed for Core): RM submits override justification. POST /api/bench-first/override: body { demand_id, justification (min 100 chars) }. Creates override record.

- Override logged in bench_first_check.override_justification. Appears in compliance analytics (HRMS-1210) as a bench-first violation.

- Three overrides in 30 days for same BU: alert BU Head.

## Business Rules

## BR: External Sourcing Blocked Until Bench Check Complete

- The 'Create External Job Req' and 'Send to Sub-Vendor' actions are disabled until BenchFirstPolicyEngine.check() has been run for that demand. If check not run: show 'Run Bench Check First' prompt. This prevents accidental external sourcing without checking bench.

- Field: External sourcing gate — bench check required to unlock

## BR: Override Requires Minimum 100-Character Justification

- If bench candidates exist but RM proceeds to external sourcing: justification must be at least 100 characters. System counts characters in real time. Short justifications ('not available', 'client prefers new') are explicitly blocked. The 100-char minimum forces a meaningful explanation.

- Field: 100-character minimum override justification
