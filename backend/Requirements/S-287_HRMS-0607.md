## Why — Business Rationale

- System monitors open adhoc demands and predicts SLA breach before it happens. Escalates at configurable thresholds.

## What — Scope

- SLAMonitorJob: runs every 30 min, computes breach_risk_score from elapsed % + risk factors, triggers AMBER/RED/CRITICAL alerts at 50/75/90.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0601 demands with sla_hours. HRMS-0602 classification. HRMS-0603 match results.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build SLAMonitorJob

- Runs every 30 min (5 min for P1). For each open adhoc: elapsed_pct = elapsed_hours/sla_hours*100. Risk factors: no bench match (+30), no sourcing (+20), no client response (+15), P1 (+25). breach_risk_score = elapsed_pct + factors, cap 100. Threshold alerts: ≥50 AMBER to RM, ≥75 RED to RM+Director, ≥90 CRITICAL WhatsApp to RM+Director+BU Head.

## Step 2: SLA dashboard section

- Adhoc queue: SLA column with elapsed % and risk badge. Historical compliance % for Director.
