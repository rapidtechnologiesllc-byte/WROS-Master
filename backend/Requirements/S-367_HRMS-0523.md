## Why — Business Rationale

- Not all escalations are equal. A client escalating because the BlitzenX resource missed a deadline they owned is fundamentally different from a client escalating because of scope creep, unclear requirements, or internal client politics. Treating both the same — resetting the certification clock, penalising the employee — is unjust and destroys morale. The escalation classification engine distinguishes the two types and applies consequences only where the employee was genuinely at fault.

## What — Scope

- Build escalation intake, investigation workflow, and classification engine. RM logs escalation. Investigation determines: RESOURCE_ERROR (employee fault) or ENVIRONMENT (external factors). RESOURCE_ERROR: clock resets, performance event logged. ENVIRONMENT: employee protected, context documented, no clock impact.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0522 certification clock. HRMS-0515 performance store. HRMS-0253 projects.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Create escalations table and intake

- CREATE TABLE escalations: id UUID PK, tenant_id UUID, employee_id UUID FK, project_id UUID FK, reported_by UUID (RM), reported_at TIMESTAMP, escalation_description TEXT (min 100 chars), escalation_type ENUM('RESOURCE_ERROR','ENVIRONMENT','UNCLASSIFIED') DEFAULT 'UNCLASSIFIED', classification_notes TEXT nullable, classified_by UUID nullable, classified_at TIMESTAMP nullable, clock_reset_triggered BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE, performance_event_id UUID nullable.

- POST /api/escalations: RM logs escalation. Auth: RM, BU Head. Must include min 100 char description.

## Step 2: Build investigation and classification workflow

- On escalation logged: notify BU Head. BU Head has 24 hours to initiate investigation.

- Investigation questions presented to BU Head: (1) Was the requirement clearly defined before the employee started the task? (2) Did the employee raise concerns before the deadline? (3) Are there documented client-side changes that contributed? (4) What does the Tech Lead / Buddy feedback say about this employee's performance in this area?

- BU Head classifies: RESOURCE_ERROR or ENVIRONMENT. Classification requires min 100 char notes.

## Step 3: Apply classification consequences

- RESOURCE_ERROR: call ClockResetHandler.reset(employee_id). Write to HRMS-0515 (event_type=ESCALATION, negative signal). Include in Core eligibility AI scoring as -15 point penalty.

- ENVIRONMENT: write to HRMS-0515 (event_type=ESCALATION, neutral — employee protected). Add note: 'Environmental escalation — resource protected. Client-side factors documented.' No clock reset. No scoring penalty.

- Appreciation signals (positive client contact): event_type=APPRECIATION, positive performance event. No clock impact (already running).

## Business Rules

## BR: Every Escalation Must Be Classified Within 24 Hours

- An UNCLASSIFIED escalation after 24 hours triggers Director alert. Escalations cannot sit unclassified — the employee is in limbo (clock technically running but under investigation). 24-hour classification SLA is non-negotiable.

- Field: 24-hour classification SLA — Director alert if breached

## BR: ENVIRONMENT Classification Is Permanent Protection

- Once an escalation is classified ENVIRONMENT: it cannot be reclassified as RESOURCE_ERROR. The classification is permanent and immutable. This prevents retroactive punishment of employees who were protected at the time.

- Field: ENVIRONMENT classification is permanent and irrevocable
