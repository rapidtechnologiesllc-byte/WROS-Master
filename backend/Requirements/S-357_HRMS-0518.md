## Why — Business Rationale

- The Core Eligibility decision is the most consequential talent decision BlitzenX makes. Getting it wrong in either direction is costly: certifying someone too early risks a client-facing failure; delaying too long loses the employee's momentum and BlitzenX's Core capacity. The AI Assessor reads the full 90+ day performance record and produces an evidence-based recommendation that removes bias and inconsistency from the evaluation — while keeping the final decision with the humans who are accountable for it.

## What — Scope

- Build CoreEligibilityAssessor: reads all performance events from HRMS-0515 for the employee, weights them by category and recency, generates ELIGIBLE/NOT_YET/BORDERLINE recommendation with confidence score, evidence summary, and risk flags. Presented to RM and BU Head as advisory input to their decision.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0515 with minimum 90 days of events. HRMS-0516 manager inputs (at least 12 weekly cycles). HRMS-0520 Buddy Program graduation record.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build CoreEligibilityAssessor service

- CoreEligibilityAssessor.assess(employee_id): reads all employee_performance_events for this employee. Groups by category. Computes weighted scores: Buddy Program Overall (15%), Manager Weekly Average — Accountability (15%), Manager Weekly Average — Requirements Understanding (10%), Manager Weekly Average — Development (10%), Delivery Velocity — stories/week trend (10%), Peer Trust Score (15%), Client Feedback Signal (10%), Timesheet Discipline (5%), Milestone Completion Rate (5%), Escalation Record (5% — RESOURCE_ERROR escalations heavily penalise, ENVIRONMENT escalations neutral).

- Total weighted score 0-100. ELIGIBLE ≥ 80. BORDERLINE 65-79. NOT_YET < 65.

## Step 2: Build LLM evidence summary generation

- After computing score: build LLM prompt with structured event data. 'You are a talent intelligence AI for BlitzenX. Assess this employee for Core Certification — direct carrier client work. Score: [N]. Key signals: [structured event summary]. Generate: (1) 3-5 sentence evidence summary for BU Head review. (2) Top 3 strengths. (3) Top 3 risk flags if any. (4) Specific recommendation rationale.' Max 800 tokens.

- Store in core_eligibility_reviews.ai_evidence_summary, ai_risk_flags, ai_confidence_score.

## Step 3: Build recency weighting

- Events in the last 4 weeks are weighted 2x relative to events 8-12 weeks ago. Trajectory matters more than average. An employee who was struggling at Week 4 but has shown consistent improvement in Weeks 10-12 should score higher than one who started strong but is declining.

- Trajectory calculation: compare avg score first 4 weeks vs last 4 weeks. If last_4_avg > first_4_avg: IMPROVING (+5 confidence bonus). If declining: -10 confidence penalty.

## Business Rules

## BR: AI Assessment Is Advisory — BU Head Decision Is Final

- The AI score and recommendation are input to the BU Head's decision, not the decision itself. A BU Head can approve an employee the AI rates BORDERLINE if they have context the AI does not. A BU Head can reject an employee the AI rates ELIGIBLE if they have concerns not captured in the data. Both decisions are logged with rationale.

- Field: AI=advisory. BU Head=final. Both logged.

## BR: RESOURCE_ERROR Escalations Heavily Penalise Score

- A RESOURCE_ERROR escalation (as opposed to environmental) in the assessment period reduces the raw score by 15 points per occurrence. Two RESOURCE_ERROR escalations in the Specialty period make ELIGIBLE effectively unreachable. This enforces: Core Certification requires a clean Specialty record.

- Field: RESOURCE_ERROR: -15 points per occurrence. Two = ELIGIBLE unreachable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: Assessment completes within 5 minutes of initiation

- AC-2: Weighted score computed correctly across all 10 categories

- AC-3: Recency weighting applied — recent events weighted 2x

- AC-4: LLM evidence summary generated with strengths and risk flags

- AC-5: ELIGIBLE ≥ 80, BORDERLINE 65-79, NOT_YET < 65

- AC-6: RESOURCE_ERROR escalation applies -15 point penalty
