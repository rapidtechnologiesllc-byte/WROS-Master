## Why — Business Rationale

- An invoice generated and approved in WROS still needs to reach BlitzenX's actual accounting system to be tracked as a receivable, matched against payment, and rolled into real financial statements. Without this sync, Finance would be manually re-entering every invoice into the ERP — exactly the kind of manual reconciliation Section 1.2 says WROS is meant to eliminate.

## What — Scope

- A sync pipeline sending approved (Sent-status) invoices from S-226 to the ERP system, with payment status sync back into WROS and receivables tracking, multi-currency aware.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-226 HRMS-0907 must be live and producing invoices that have reached Sent status.

- HRMS-0908 base ERP integration/credentials must be provisioned.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the invoice export

- On an invoice reaching Sent status (per S-226's lifecycle), push the full invoice (line items, client, currency, total) to the ERP.

## Step 2: Build the payment status sync-back

- ERP-side payment confirmation flows back into WROS, updating the invoice's status to Paid per S-226's lifecycle — this is the mechanism that actually closes the loop S-226 defined but didn't itself implement the ERP side of.

## Step 3: Build receivables tracking

- Outstanding (Sent but not yet Paid) invoice totals tracked and surfaced for Finance visibility, cross-referenced against S-228's Client Revenue Realization Dashboard.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-1309-01 — Only Sent-status invoices export to ERP, never Draft or Approved-but-unsent

- Matches S-226's own lifecycle discipline — an invoice that hasn't been explicitly sent to the client per BR-0907-02 has no business appearing in the ERP as a receivable yet.

- Field: ERP export trigger fires only on the Draft->Approved->Sent transition reaching Sent, not earlier stages

BR: BR-1309-02 — Payment confirmation from ERP is the authoritative trigger for S-226's Paid status

- WROS doesn't independently guess when an invoice is paid — it trusts the ERP's payment confirmation as the source of truth, closing the loop S-226 defined.

- Field: invoices.status transitions to Paid only on an inbound ERP payment confirmation event, not on any WROS-side timer or guess

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: An invoice only exports to ERP once it reaches Sent status, never earlier in its lifecycle.

- AC-2: An invoice's status updates to Paid only upon an inbound ERP payment confirmation, never a WROS-side assumption.

- AC-3: A sync failure alerts Finance after the standard retry window rather than failing silently.
