## Why — Business Rationale

- The Reserve Fund is BlitzenX's financial buffer, and its health status — whether the balance is meeting the 12-month-expense target — is one of the five headline metrics on your dashboard. Getting the monthly flow logic right (contribute 25% on a profitable month, draw down to cover a loss month, never the reverse) is what keeps that buffer meaning what it's supposed to mean.

## What — Scope

- The monthly Reserve Fund calculation per entity — contribution or drawdown based on that month's NPBR, running balance, and a health status (GREEN/RED) against the 12-months-of-current-expense target — seeded with the actual starting balances you provided (BXUS $350K, BXIN ₹30L, both noted as placeholder figures pending final confirmation).

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-1607 must be live and producing each entity's monthly NPBR (= EBITDA) before Reserve Contribution.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the monthly flow calculation

- If NPBR > 0: Reserve Contribution = 0.25 × NPBR. Actual Net Profit = 0.75 × NPBR. Reserve Balance = Previous Balance + Contribution.

- If NPBR ≤ 0: Reserve Contribution = 0. Actual Net Profit = NPBR (the loss, unchanged). Reserve Balance = Previous Balance - ABS(NPBR) — a draw against the reserve to cover the loss.

## Step 2: Build the health status calculation

- Target Buffer = 12 × current month's Expense.

- Status = GREEN if Reserve Balance ≥ Target, RED if below.

- Dashboard-facing display: Current Balance | Target | +/- Delta, per entity and combined.

## Step 3: Seed the starting balances

- BXUS: $350,000. BXIN: ₹30,00,000 (converted to USD via the configured FX rate for combined display).

- Both explicitly flagged in the UI as provisional starting figures pending final confirmation, per your own note that these are not the original/final numbers — same placeholder-visibility discipline as the cost-engine constants.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-1612-01 — Contribution only on a profitable month; a loss month draws down, never both in the same cycle

- The monthly flow is strictly one direction per month — matching your locked logic exactly, no partial-contribution-and-partial-drawdown blending.

- Field: Reserve Contribution and drawdown are mutually exclusive per month, determined solely by the sign of that month's NPBR

## BR: BR-1612-02 — Starting balances are visibly flagged as provisional until confirmed

- You explicitly noted the seeded figures (BXUS $350K, BXIN ₹30L) are not the original numbers — the system must not let anyone downstream mistake a placeholder seed for a confirmed historical balance.

- Field: reserve_fund_ledger's initial seed row carries is_provisional=true until explicitly confirmed by Finance

BR: BR-1612-03 — Reserve Balance is tracked per entity, combined only for dashboard display

- Matches HRMS-1607's no-auto-consolidation principle — BXIN's reserve and BXUS's reserve are separate running balances, summed only at display time, never merged into one pool a loss in one entity could draw down against the other's buffer.

- Field: reserve_fund_ledger.entity is a required discriminator; no cross-entity balance transfer exists in this story (that's HRMS-1606's manual intercompany settlement, a distinct concept)
