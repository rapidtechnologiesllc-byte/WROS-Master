## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

When an offer is released, the candidate must be able to view the complete offer details, accept or decline formally, and ask questions — all within the portal. Getting a PDF attached to an email is not enough. The portal offer viewer gives them a structured, mobile-friendly offer experience with a formal Accept/Decline action that feeds directly into HRMS-0456 tracking.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build the Offer page at /candidate/{token}/offer. Shows: role title, salary (formatted in local currency), start date, key terms. Accept / Decline / Ask a Question buttons. Offer letter PDF download. Expiry countdown.

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Build Offer page

- Route: /candidate/:token/offer

- If offer.status=DRAFT or no offer: show 'No offer available yet. Thunder will notify you when your offer is ready.'

- If offer.status=RELEASED: show offer details

- If offer.status=ACCEPTED: show 'Offer accepted! Welcome to BlitzenX. Your joining date is [date].' (read-only, no more actions)

- If offer.status=DECLINED: show 'You declined this offer.' (read-only)

## Step 2: Build offer detail card

- Show prominently: role_title, salary (formatted in candidate's local currency — NOT in base units), start_date, offer_expiry_date

- Salary format: India → 'X LPA (per annum)'. US/Canada → '$X,000 per year'. UK → '£X,000 per year'. Europe → '€X,000 per year'.

- Expiry countdown: 'This offer expires in [X] days' — red when <3 days. Gray when expired.

- Offer letter download button: downloads offers.offer_letter_url (S3 file)

- 'Full offer details are in your offer letter — tap to download and review the complete terms.'

## Step 3: Build Accept / Decline / Ask actions

- Three buttons: 'Accept Offer' (green, primary), 'Decline Offer' (red, secondary), 'Ask a Question' (gray, tertiary)

- 'Accept Offer': confirmation modal 'By accepting, you confirm you accept all terms in the offer letter. Confirm | Cancel'. On confirm: POST /api/portal/offers/{id}/accept. On success: page updates to 'Accepted' state. Feeds HRMS-0456.

- 'Decline Offer': modal 'We understand. Could you share why? (optional)' — textarea. 'Decline' button. POST /api/portal/offers/{id}/decline { decline_reason }. On success: page shows 'Declined' state. Feeds HRMS-0456.

- 'Ask a Question': opens Thunder chat (navigates to Messages tab with pre-filled 'I have a question about my offer: '). Thunder's FAQ bot handles it via HRMS-0455.

## Step 4: Handle expired offers

- If NOW() > offer.offer_expiry_date AND status still RELEASED: show 'This offer has expired. Please contact your BlitzenX recruiter to discuss next steps.'

- Show recruiter contact info (recruiter name from assignment). Do not show Accept/Decline buttons on expired offer.

- Backend: OfferExpiryJob runs daily. If offer.status=RELEASED AND offer_expiry_date < NOW(): UPDATE status='EXPIRED'. Notify recruiter.

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Salary Displayed in Candidate's Local Currency — Never Base Units

The salary in the portal offer view is ALWAYS displayed in the candidate's local currency format. Never show 1800000 to a candidate — show '18 LPA'. Never show 12000000 cents — show '$120,000 per year'. The conversion and format follows the same rules as HRMS-0454 notification message.

↳ Triggered by field: base_salary → local currency display

## BR-02: Accept Requires Explicit Confirmation — Cannot Be Accidental

The 'Accept Offer' button must show a confirmation modal before accepting. The modal explicitly states that accepting confirms agreement with all offer letter terms. The candidate must click 'Confirm' in the modal — not just the Accept button alone.

↳ Triggered by field: Accept requires two-step confirmation

## BR-03: Decline Reason Is Optional — One Question Only

The decline modal asks for a reason but it is optional. The candidate does not have to explain. 'Decline' button works without any text in the reason field. Do not require a reason to decline — that creates friction and damages brand relationship.

↳ Triggered by field: decline_reason is optional
