## Why — Business Rationale

- Once bench matches are found, the AI allocation recommendation reads match results + client history and produces a specific recommendation with rationale. One-click to submit.

## What — Scope

- AllocationRecommender: LLM reads top 5 matches + client preference history → ranked recommendation with rationale. One-click submit to client.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0603 adhoc_bench_matches. HRMS-0602 classification. Client history from submissions.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build AllocationRecommender

- Reads top 5 matches + client rejection history. LLM prompt produces: primary_recommendation { employee_id, rationale, confidence_pct }, alternative { employee_id, rationale }. Stored in adhoc_allocation_recommendations.

## Step 2: One-click submit

- [Submit to Client] button creates submission. RM must confirm 'Have you reviewed the profile?' dialog before submission sent.
