## Why — Business Rationale

- BXIN and BXUS are deliberately separate P&Ls with no automatic consolidation — but when BXIN can't cover its own India costs from Specialty billing alone, BXUS has to cover the shortfall, and that movement of money between entities needs its own auditable record. Per your explicit direction, this is a manual entry, not an automatic calculation — the two entities' financial independence is the whole point, and auto-triggering a transfer would quietly undermine that separation.

## What — Scope

- A ledger where Finance manually records intercompany settlement entries — BXUS covering a BXIN deficit — referencing each entity's monthly NPBR (from the P&L Engine) as context, but never auto-generating or auto-posting the transfer itself.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-1607 BXIN/BXUS Separate P&L Engine must be live — this ledger references each entity's computed NPBR as read-only context for the entry.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the settlement entry form

- Finance manually enters: amount, from_entity, to_entity, month, and a reference note (e.g., "BXIN NPBR deficit for July").

- The relevant month's NPBR for both entities is displayed as read-only reference context on the same screen — informational only, never auto-populating the amount field.

## Step 2: Build the settlement history view

- Full chronological record of every intercompany entry, by month, by direction.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-1606-01 — This ledger is entirely manual entry, with no auto-calculation or auto-population from NPBR, ever

- The whole point of BXIN and BXUS being separate P&Ls is that consolidation is a deliberate human financial decision each month, not an automatic system behavior — even showing the NPBR figures alongside the entry form must never cross into pre-filling or suggesting the settlement amount.

- Field: Settlement amount field has no default value and no computed suggestion anywhere in its implementation
