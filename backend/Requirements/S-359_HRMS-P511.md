## Why — Business Rationale

- A 50% HTD conversion rate means half of every Java engineer intake cohort does not become a Guidewire consultant. If that rate drops further — to 40%, 30% — BlitzenX is burning salary cost and training investment on a pipeline that is not producing. The moment conversion drops below 50% for two consecutive months, the system must pause new intake automatically. Continuing to add headcount to a broken pipeline is the worst possible response.

## What — Scope

- Build monthly conversion rate monitor. Calculate: (HTD graduates achieving Core-ready in target timeframe) / (total HTD intake that month). If below 50% for two consecutive months: auto-pause new HTD intake, notify BU Head and Hemant, block HTD sourcing trigger until BU Head explicitly re-enables with audit log entry.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HTD employee records with htd_track=TRUE. HRMS-P506 phase gate completion records. HRMS-P509 dashboard.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build monthly conversion rate calculator

- Runs on 1st of each month. For previous month: cohort = employees WHERE htd_track=TRUE AND htd_start_date BETWEEN [month_start] AND [month_end]. converted = COUNT(cohort WHERE core_certified=TRUE AND (core_certified_date - htd_start_date) <= 400 days). conversion_rate = converted/cohort_size. Store in htd_monthly_metrics: month, cohort_size, converted, conversion_rate.

## Step 2: Build breach detection and auto-pause

- On each monthly calculation: check if last 2 consecutive months both < 50%. If yes: set system_config.htd_intake_paused=TRUE, htd_pause_triggered_at=NOW(), htd_pause_reason='Conversion rate below 50% for 2 consecutive months.'

- Notify BU Head + Hemant via WhatsApp + email immediately: 'HTD Intake PAUSED. Conversion rate: [month1]% and [month2]%. New intake blocked until audit complete and BU Head re-enables.'

- Block HRMS-0307 SourcingWorkflowTrigger for HTD strategy demands while paused.

## Step 3: Build re-enable flow with audit requirement

- POST /api/htd/resume-intake: Auth BU Head only. body { audit_findings (min 200 chars), corrective_actions (min 200 chars) }. Sets htd_intake_paused=FALSE. Logs in htd_pause_log.

- Cannot re-enable without both audit_findings and corrective_actions meeting minimum length — forces documented review before resuming.

## Business Rules

## BR: Auto-Pause Requires No Human Trigger

- The breach detection runs automatically on the 1st of each month. BU Head does not need to notice the trend and act — the system acts. This removes the human delay in recognising a pipeline problem.

- Field: Auto-pause on detection — no human trigger required

## BR: Re-Enable Requires Documented Audit — Not Just BU Head Click

- Re-enabling HTD intake requires minimum 200 characters each for audit findings and corrective actions. A BU Head cannot click a button to resume — they must document what went wrong and what has been fixed. This prevents resuming intake without genuine diagnosis.

- Field: 200-char minimum for both audit fields before re-enable
