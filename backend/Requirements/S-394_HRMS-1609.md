## Why — Business Rationale

- Resource Manager overhead is a real cost of delivering billable work, and your locked model spreads it evenly across every billable employee's hour rather than leaving it as an unallocated overhead line — this is what makes the Min Bill Rate formula actually reflect true delivery cost instead of understating it by ignoring RM cost entirely.

## What — Scope

- A calculation implementing your locked RM Burden formula exactly: total annual BXIN RM cost divided by billable employee count divided by billable hours per year, producing a flat per-hour burden applied uniformly to every billable employee's cost.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-1608 must be live — RM Burden combines with FLC in the downstream Min Bill Rate calculation.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the RM Burden formula

- RM Burden per hour = Total Annual BXIN RM Cost ÷ Billable Employee Count ÷ Billable Hours Per Year — using your worked example, ₹35,00,000 (converted via FX) ÷ 45 ÷ 1,680 = $0.541/hr.

- Recalculates whenever total RM cost, billable headcount, or the billable-hours-per-year constant changes — same recalculate-on-change discipline as HRMS-1608.

## Step 2: Apply uniformly to every billable employee

- This is a single flat rate applied to every billable employee's hourly cost — not weighted or varied by individual employee, per your "fair pool, Option A" decision already locked in Section 20 of your reference document.
