## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

Client Services manages the relationship between BlitzenX and its clients — tracking what positions are open, what candidates have been submitted, and what the client has come back with (shortlisted, rejected, offered). Without a dedicated dashboard, Client Services uses spreadsheets and misses submissions or follow-up deadlines, damaging client relationships.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build CS Dashboard at /cs/dashboard. Four sections: (1) Active Client Engagements. (2) Submission Pipeline per client. (3) Pending client feedback (submissions awaiting response). (4) Interview pipeline for active clients.

## Step 1: Build GET /api/cs/dashboard endpoint

- Auth: CS role. Filter by tenant_id.

- { client_engagements: [{ client_name, open_positions: N, active_submissions: N, interviews_this_week: N }], submission_pipeline: [{ client_name, job_title, candidate_name, submitted_at, client_status: 'PENDING'|'SHORTLISTED'|'REJECTED'|'INTERVIEW_SCHEDULED' }] (last 30 days), pending_feedback: [{ client_name, job_title, candidate_name, submitted_at, days_waiting }] (submitted but no client response, sorted by days_waiting DESC), interview_pipeline: [{ candidate_name, client_name, job_title, interview_date, interview_level }] (next 14 days) }

## Step 2: Build CS Dashboard page

- Route: /cs/dashboard

- Card 1 — Active Clients: table per client. Columns: Client Name, Open Positions, Submitted Candidates, Pending Feedback count, Interviews This Week.

- Card 2 — Submission Pipeline: Kanban-style columns: Submitted → Shortlisted → Interview → Offer. Count per column per client. Click column → filtered list.

- Card 3 — Awaiting Feedback: orange-highlighted list. Sorted by days_waiting DESC. 'URGENT' badge when > 5 days without client response.

- Card 4 — Interview Pipeline: calendar-style list for next 14 days. Client name + candidate + job + date/time.

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Awaiting Feedback > 5 Days = URGENT

Any submission where client has not responded in over 5 days gets URGENT badge in Awaiting Feedback card. This is a service level indicator — BlitzenX's commitment to clients includes proactive follow-up.

↳ Triggered by field: days_waiting > 5 = URGENT
