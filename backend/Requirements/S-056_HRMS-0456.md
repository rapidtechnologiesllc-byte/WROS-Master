## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

When a candidate accepts, declines, or counters an offer, the system must capture this immediately — the moment the candidate communicates their decision to Thunder. The acceptance triggers the preboarding pipeline. A decline triggers the candidate archiving and pipeline re-opening. A counter requires human involvement. Each outcome has a completely different downstream process.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build: (1) Detect acceptance/decline/counter intent from candidate message. (2) Update offers.status accordingly. (3) Trigger correct downstream flow per outcome. (4) Notify relevant parties (HR, recruiter, Thunder's final message).

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Extend intent detection for offer responses

- Add offer-specific intents to HRMS-0433 when status=OFFER_SENT:

- offer_accepted: 'I accept', 'I accept the offer', 'I am happy to join', 'I would love to join', 'yes, I accept'

- offer_declined: 'I decline', 'I am not able to accept', 'I am not joining', 'I decided to go elsewhere', 'withdrawing my application'

- offer_counter: 'can we discuss the salary', 'I was hoping for more', 'counter offer', 'negotiate'

- These intent classifications run within HRMS-0433's detectIntent() when offer_faq_active=true

## Step 2: Handle ACCEPTANCE outcome

- When intent=offer_accepted: UPDATE offers SET status='ACCEPTED', candidate_response_at=NOW()

- UPDATE candidates.status = 'ACCEPTED'

- Thunder sends confirmation: 'Congratulations and welcome to BlitzenX! We are thrilled to have you join the team. Our HR team will be in touch with the next steps for your joining process. In the meantime, I will start collecting some documents we need. 🎉'

- Call transitionState(conversation_id, 'PREBOARDING', 'Offer accepted', 'THUNDER')

- Publish event: offer.accepted { candidate_id, offer_id, tenant_id }

- Notify HR and recruiter: '[Candidate Name] has accepted the offer for [Role Title]. Starting date: [date].'

- Trigger HRMS-0457 Document Collection Agent

## Step 3: Handle DECLINE outcome

- When intent=offer_declined: Thunder responds: 'Thank you for letting us know. We completely understand, and we appreciate the time you spent with us. We wish you all the best in your next role. We will keep your profile for future opportunities that may be a better fit.'

- UPDATE offers SET status='DECLINED', candidate_response_at=NOW()

- Ask Thunder to capture decline reason: 'If you are comfortable sharing, could you let us know why you decided not to proceed? It helps us improve our process.' (one-time ask — do not persist if no answer)

- UPDATE candidates.status = 'DECLINED_OFFER'

- Call transitionState(conversation_id, 'COMPLETED', 'Offer declined', 'THUNDER')

- Publish event: offer.declined { candidate_id, offer_id, decline_reason nullable }

- Notify HR and recruiter: '[Name] declined the offer. Reason (if provided): [reason]. Consider re-opening the pipeline.'

## Step 4: Handle COUNTER outcome

- When intent=offer_counter: Thunder responds: 'Thank you for sharing your thoughts. I am passing your feedback to our recruiting team who will be in touch to discuss. Please hold tight!'

- UPDATE offers SET status='COUNTERED', candidate_response_at=NOW()

- Call transitionState(conversation_id, 'ESCALATED', 'Candidate countered offer', 'THUNDER')

- Notify recruiter (with urgency): '[Name] has countered the offer. Their message: [last candidate message]. Please respond within 24 hours — offer expires on [expiry_date].'

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Acceptance Triggers Preboarding — Not Joining

Offer ACCEPTED transitions conversation to PREBOARDING state. The candidate has not joined yet. Do not update employees table or create employee record. That happens in HRMS-0708 when the candidate actually joins.

↳ Triggered by field: conversation.status=PREBOARDING (not COMPLETED)

## BR-02: Decline Reason Is Optional — Ask Once

After decline: ask once for the reason. If candidate does not provide it within 24 hours: do not ask again. The reason field stays null. Never make a declining candidate feel interrogated.

↳ Triggered by field: decline_reason collection is one-time ask

## BR-03: Counter Requires Human Within 24 Hours

When offer is countered: notify recruiter with urgency flag. Include offer expiry date in notification. A counter that goes unanswered until the offer expires loses the candidate.

↳ Triggered by field: offer_counter → 24h recruiter response required
