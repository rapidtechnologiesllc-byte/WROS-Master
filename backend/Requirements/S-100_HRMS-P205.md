## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

Technical interviewers need to know: who they are interviewing today, the candidate's technical background, the role they are interviewing for, and what the L1/L2 decision criteria are — all before they join the call. Without this, interviewers go into interviews unprepared and give inconsistent assessments.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build the Technical Interview Queue at /technical/interview-queue. Shows today's and upcoming interviews for this interviewer. Each interview card links to candidate summary, resume, technical score, and the feedback form.

## 4. BEFORE YOU START

▶  Do NOT write a single line of code until every item below is confirmed complete.

- Interviews linked to interviewer_user_id. Resume parsing complete (HRMS-0428). Technical score calculated (HRMS-0437). HRMS-P206 feedback form built.

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Build GET /api/technical/interview-queue endpoint

- Auth: Technical Interviewer role. Filter: interviews WHERE interviewer_user_id = current user AND scheduled_at >= today AND status IN ('CONFIRMED','RESCHEDULING').

- Response per interview: { interview_id, candidate_name, job_title, level (L1/L2), scheduled_at_local (interviewer's TZ), duration_minutes, meeting_link, candidate_summary: { years_experience, current_employer, skills_top_5, technical_score, resume_url } }

## Step 2: Build Interview Queue page

- Route: /technical/interview-queue

- Tab 1 — Today: interviews scheduled for today in interviewer's timezone. Bold 'NOW' badge if within 30 minutes. Orange 'SOON' if within 2 hours.

- Tab 2 — Upcoming: next 14 days of interviews.

- Each interview card: candidate name, role, L1/L2 badge, time in interviewer's TZ, meeting link button, [View Candidate] button, [Give Feedback] button.

- [View Candidate] expands a panel below the card showing: years experience, top 5 skills, technical score bar, resume download link, AI-generated candidate summary (2 sentences from HRMS-0419).

- [Give Feedback] navigates to HRMS-P206 feedback form for this interview.

## Step 3: Build 'Start Interview' join link

- For interviews within 30 minutes: 'Start Interview' primary button replaces 'View Meeting Link'. One-click joins the video call.

- After interview time passes: 'Give Feedback' becomes the primary button (green, prominent). Reminder if feedback not submitted 1 hour after interview end.

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Interviewer Only Sees Their Own Interviews

The interview queue shows ONLY interviews where interviewer_user_id = logged-in user. Never shows other interviewers' queues.

↳ Triggered by field: interviewer_user_id filter

## BR-02: Technical Score from HRMS-0437 — Do Not Expose Raw Score Without Context

Technical score shown as bar (0-100) with label 'Strong Match'/'Good Match'/'Partial Match' based on thresholds. Raw number shown on hover only. Interviewers should not anchor to a number before the interview.

↳ Triggered by field: Score visualization with label
