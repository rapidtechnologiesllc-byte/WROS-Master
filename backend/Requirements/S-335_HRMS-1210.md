## Why — Business Rationale

- BlitzenX's 10 hard business rules (experience gate, no market profile, W2 only, bench-first, etc.) generate compliance events every time they fire. The Compliance Analytics Dashboard aggregates these: how many times was the experience gate triggered? How many override requests were made? How many sub-vendor C2C violations? Which rules fire most frequently — indicating training gaps or process issues.

## What — Scope

- Build: (1) Rule violation frequency dashboard. (2) Override approval rate and timeline. (3) Submission compliance scorecard. (4) Exportable compliance audit report for legal/HR.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-1201 analytics. conversation_audit_log. submission_violations. experience_override_approvals. subvendor_violations.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build GET /api/analytics/compliance

- Auth: Admin, Director. Params: date_from, date_to.

- Returns: { rule_violations: [{ rule_name, violation_count, unique_recruiters_involved: N, trend: 'INCREASING'|'STABLE'|'DECREASING' }], override_requests: { total, approved, rejected, avg_approval_days }, subvendor_violations_total: N, recruiters_flagged_for_review: N, recruiters_suspended: N }

## Step 2: Build Compliance Dashboard at /admin/analytics/compliance

- Rule violation table: rule name, count, trend badge. Sorted by count DESC.

- Override funnel: requested → Director approved → BU Head approved → Final approved.

- Recruiter compliance scorecard: violations per recruiter in period.

- [Export PDF Report] button — generates a printable compliance report for legal/HR.

## Step 3: Build exportable compliance PDF report

- POST /api/analytics/compliance/export-report: generates PDF with: period, rule violation counts, override decisions, recruiter violations, key audit log events. Uses PDF generation pattern from HRMS-0707.

- Suitable for legal and HR review — formal document format with BlitzenX letterhead.

## Business Rules

## BR: Compliance Report Is Immutable Once Exported

- Exported compliance PDF reports are stored in S3 with timestamp in filename. They are immutable records. Generating a new report does not overwrite previous exports.

- Field: Immutable exported reports

## BR: Admin and Director Only

- Compliance data is sensitive — recruiter violation counts, override decisions.

- Field: Admin/Director only
