## Why — Business Rationale

- A client submission is the moment an HTD graduate's training investment either pays off or doesn't — submitting someone who's missed a step in training damages both the client relationship and the credibility of the whole HTD program. This checklist is the single, explicit gate that confirms every prerequisite is genuinely done before that first submission happens.

## What — Scope

- A pre-submission readiness checklist — training complete, assessment passed, HTD internal interview clear (per S-261), documentation verified, BU Head sign-off — that auto-generates a readiness report and moves the graduate into the deployable Guidewire resource pool only once every item is checked.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-360 HRMS-P506-REV 4-phase gate structure must be live — this checklist is the final confirmation step after Phase 4 completes.

- S-261 must have logged a PROCEED_TO_TRAINING Director decision and subsequent training completion.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the checklist

- Five items: training_complete (from S-360 phase gates), assessment_passed, htd_interview_clear (from S-261), documentation_verified, bu_head_signoff.

## Step 2: Enforce all-or-nothing gating

- Every item must be individually checked true — no partial or majority-complete state moves a candidate forward.

## Step 3: Auto-generate the readiness report

- On full checklist completion, a structured report is generated summarizing the full HTD journey (training scores, interview outcomes, phase gate history) for audit and for the BU Head's own reference.

## Step 4: Move to the deployable resource pool

- On checklist completion, the employee's status updates to mark them eligible for client submission — this is the moment an HTD graduate becomes visible to standard demand-matching, same pool as any Specialty-ready employee.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-P508-01 — All five checklist items are mandatory, no partial-completion submission eligibility

- A candidate missing even one item (e.g., BU Head sign-off pending) is not yet deployable — this is a hard gate, not a soft recommendation.

- Field: pool_eligible flag only sets true when all 5 checklist booleans are true simultaneously

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: A candidate with 4 of 5 checklist items complete does not become pool_eligible.

- AC-2: Completing the 5th item auto-generates the readiness report and immediately updates pool eligibility.

- AC-3: A pool-eligible HTD graduate becomes visible to standard demand-matching identically to any other Specialty-ready employee.
