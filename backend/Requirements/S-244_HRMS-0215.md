## Why — Business Rationale

- A revenue target without enough pipeline behind it is a target that's going to be missed — the standard rule of thumb in staffing and services businesses is roughly 3x pipeline coverage of the target. This ratio is the single number that tells a BU Head whether they have enough deals in motion to plausibly hit their number, well before the quarter closes and it's too late to react.

## What — Scope

- Pipeline coverage ratio — total pipeline value divided by revenue target — calculated per BU and continent, with an alert when coverage drops below 3x.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-238 must be live for pipeline value.

- BU revenue targets must be configured (existing Workforce Planning target-setting).

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the coverage ratio calculation

- coverage_ratio = total_pipeline_value_usd / bu_revenue_target_usd, using S-238's shared pipeline figure.

## Step 2: Build the alert

- coverage_ratio < 3.0 triggers an HRMS-0113 notification to the BU Head.

## Step 3: Build the BU/continent filter view

- Same continent-aware filtering pattern as S-241's Executive Dashboard.
