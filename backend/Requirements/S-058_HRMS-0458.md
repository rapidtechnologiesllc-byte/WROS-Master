## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

An offer accepted is not a guarantee of joining. Candidates can still drop out during preboarding. The Joining Readiness Score gives HR a single number (0-100) that tells them how likely this candidate is to actually show up on day one — based on: documents submitted, background check status, offer signed, start date confirmed, and how many days until joining.

A score of 95 means the candidate is fully prepared and will almost certainly join. A score of 40 means there are several outstanding items and potential risk of drop-out.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

## Step 1: Build calculateJoiningReadiness() function

- calculateJoiningReadiness(candidate_id, offer_id, tenant_id) → integer 0-100

- Component 1 — Documents (40%): ROUND((received_docs / total_required_docs) * 40)

- Component 2 — Offer formality (20%): offer status=ACCEPTED AND start_date confirmed AND offer signed (if e-signature available) = 20. Only ACCEPTED = 15. Missing start date confirmation = 10.

- Component 3 — Days until joining (15%): >30 days away=15pts, 15-30 days=12pts, 7-14 days=10pts, 3-7 days=8pts, <3 days=15pts (imminent — committed), overdue=0pts.

- Component 4 — Responsiveness since acceptance (15%): replied to Thunder's document requests within 48h=15pts, replied within 48-96h=10pts, not replied within 96h=0pts.

- Component 5 — Background check (10%): CLEARED=10pts, IN_PROGRESS=5pts, NOT_STARTED=2pts, FAILED=0pts (blocking flag).

- Total = SUM of all components (max 100)

- UPSERT into candidate_joining_scores: { candidate_id, offer_id, readiness_score, score_breakdown JSONB, calculated_at }

## Step 2: Create candidate_joining_scores table

- id UUID PK, tenant_id UUID, candidate_id UUID, offer_id UUID, readiness_score INTEGER, score_breakdown JSONB, calculated_at TIMESTAMP

- UNIQUE on (tenant_id, candidate_id, offer_id)

## Step 3: Build JoiningReadinessJob (runs every 6 hours for PREBOARDING candidates)

- SELECT candidate_id, offer_id FROM candidate_conversations cc JOIN offers o ON o.candidate_id=cc.candidate_id WHERE cc.status='PREBOARDING' AND cc.tenant_id=X

- For each: call calculateJoiningReadiness(). Upsert score.

- Publish event: candidate.readiness_score_updated { candidate_id, readiness_score }

- If score drops below 50: notify recruiter: '[Name] joining readiness score has dropped to [X]. Review their preboarding status.'

- Label: 'Joining Readiness: [X]%'

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

## BR-01: Score Drops Below 50 = Immediate HR Alert

When readiness_score falls below 50: notify recruiter immediately (not on next scheduled job run). Sub-50 score indicates significant joining risk. HR should intervene proactively.

↳ Triggered by field: readiness_score < 50

## BR-02: Score Is Distinct From Profile Completeness Score

Profile completeness (HRMS-0407) measures qualification data. Joining readiness measures preboarding completion. They are different scores for different stages. Never confuse or combine them.
