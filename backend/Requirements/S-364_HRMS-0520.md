## Why — Business Rationale

- Every new BlitzenX hire goes through 30 days of structured evaluation before touching a client. Not as a formality — as a genuine vetting process that determines if this person can represent BlitzenX at a subcontractor client without creating risk. The 35 KPI framework ensures evaluation is consistent, data-driven, and multi-dimensional: technical ability is only 15 of the 35 parameters. Operational discipline, communication, ownership, and professional behaviour are equally weighted because a technically brilliant engineer who submits timesheets late and ignores HR emails will create client escalations.

## What — Scope

- Build the complete 35-KPI Buddy Program tracking system. Three scorecards: HR (12 KPIs), Buddy Engineer (15 KPIs), RM (8 KPIs). Weekly score collection. All scores write to HRMS-0515 performance store. Day 30 composite scorecard generated for BU Head graduation gate.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0515 performance store. HRMS-0101 employees with buddy_program_status field.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Create buddy_program_records and buddy_kpi_scores tables

- CREATE TABLE buddy_program_records: id UUID PK, tenant_id UUID, employee_id UUID FK, buddy_engineer_user_id UUID FK, program_start_date DATE, expected_end_date DATE, actual_end_date DATE nullable, status ENUM('IN_PROGRESS','GRADUATED','EXTENDED','EXITED'), extension_count INTEGER DEFAULT 0, extension_reason TEXT nullable, bu_head_decision_notes TEXT nullable, created_at TIMESTAMP.

- CREATE TABLE buddy_kpi_scores: id UUID PK, tenant_id UUID, buddy_record_id UUID FK, kpi_number INTEGER (1-35), kpi_category ENUM('HR','BUDDY','RM'), kpi_name VARCHAR(200), score INTEGER (1-5 or 0-100 depending on KPI), scored_by UUID, scored_date DATE, week_number INTEGER (1-4), notes TEXT nullable.

## Step 2: Seed the 35 KPI definitions

- INSERT INTO buddy_kpi_definitions: HR KPIs (1-12): timesheet_punctuality, hr_email_response_time, onboarding_task_completion, it_setup_speed, benefits_enrollment, policy_signoff_rate, background_check_responsiveness, contract_execution_speed, training_module_completion, meeting_attendance, calendar_responsiveness, written_communication_tone.

- BUDDY KPIs (13-27): technical_knowledge_depth, requirements_understanding, adhoc_problem_solving, learning_speed, work_quality, documentation_discipline, question_quality, mistake_ownership, initiative_level, team_integration_speed, communication_clarity, deadline_reliability, escalation_judgement, pressure_behaviour, peer_interaction_quality.

- RM KPIs (28-35): timesheet_accuracy, standup_quality, proactive_communication, sprint_velocity, rm_check_in_responsiveness, interview_participation, interview_quality_score, bench_responsibility.

## Step 3: Build weekly KPI collection workflow

- Each Friday: HRMS-0516 bot extension asks HR inputs for KPIs 1-12. Buddy Engineer submits scores for KPIs 13-27 via portal form /admin/buddy-program/{record_id}/weekly-score. RM submits KPIs 28-35.

- All scores write to buddy_kpi_scores AND via PerformanceStoreWriter to employee_performance_events with event_type=BUDDY_KPI.

- Week 4 composite calculation: average per category + weighted overall: HR 34%, Buddy 43%, RM 23%.

## Step 4: Build Day 30 scorecard generation

- On Day 30 (or program_end_date): auto-generate buddy_program_scorecard: per-KPI scores, category averages, weighted overall score, trajectory (Week 1 vs Week 4 averages), lowest-scoring KPIs flagged for BU Head attention.

- Scorecard sent to BU Head via HRMS-0113 with link to full detail and graduation gate (HRMS-0521).

## Business Rules

## BR: All 35 KPIs Must Be Scored Each Week — No Partial Submissions

- Weekly KPI scoring is rejected if any of the 35 KPIs has not been scored. HR, Buddy Engineer, and RM must each complete their full scorecard before Week N is marked complete. Partial submissions are saved as drafts but not counted in performance calculations until complete.

- Field: All 35 KPIs required for complete week — partial = draft only

## BR: Single KPI Below 2.0 Average for Two Consecutive Weeks Triggers RM Alert

- If any individual KPI averages below 2.0 for two consecutive weeks: RM is alerted with the specific KPI and context. This allows targeted intervention before Day 30, not reactive action after a failed graduation.

- Field: KPI < 2.0 for 2 weeks = RM alert — specific KPI identified

## BR: Buddy Engineer Cannot Score Their Own Employee — Conflict Prevention

- The buddy_engineer_user_id on the buddy_program_record cannot match the scored_by on any BUDDY KPI score row. System validates and rejects self-scoring. This prevents buddies gaming scores for friends.

- Field: Buddy cannot self-score or score a buddy they are personally close to (RM assigns buddies)
