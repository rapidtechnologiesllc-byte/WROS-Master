## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

Recruiters often want to quickly re-run a search they did earlier in the session or yesterday, without rebuilding it from scratch. A simple recent-searches list speeds this up.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build a 'Recent Searches' dropdown showing the last 10 searches run by this recruiter, with one-click re-execution.
