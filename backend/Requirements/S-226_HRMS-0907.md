## Why — Business Rationale

- R-10 exists specifically to stop an invoice going out for a period with unresolved timesheets — but R-10 is only meaningful if the invoice generation process actually checks it, tracks the invoice through its full lifecycle, and gives both BlitzenX and the client a clean document at the end. Generation, status tracking, and PDF export are one continuous flow a finance user experiences as a single action, not three separate screens.

## What — Scope

- Invoice generation from approved time entries (one line item per employee per project), an internal approval step before sending, full status lifecycle tracking (Draft → Approved → Sent → Paid), PDF export, client billing period configuration, and multi-currency support.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0903 Timesheet base and HRMS-0605 TimesheetRevenueProcessor must be live — R-10's approval check happens inside that processor, this story is the invoice document layer on top of it.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build invoice generation from approved time

- One line item per employee per project for the billing period, rate from the project's role billing rate.

- Generation itself calls HRMS-0605, which enforces R-10 — no invoice generates for a period with any unapproved timesheet.

## Step 2: Build the internal approval step

- Draft invoice requires internal Finance approval before it can transition to Approved/ready-to-send — a human checkpoint before anything reaches the client.

## Step 3: Build status lifecycle tracking

- Draft → Approved → Sent → Paid, each transition timestamped and logged via HRMS-0118 Activity Timeline.

## Step 4: Build PDF export

- Client-ready PDF with line items, totals, and BlitzenX letterhead, generated on the Sent transition.

## Step 5: Build billing period configuration and multi-currency support

- Client billing period (weekly/biweekly/monthly) configurable per client per HRMS-0115 system config.

- Invoice totals computed in the client's default_currency (per S-230) with underlying USD-cents storage per R-09.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-0907-01 — Invoice generation is blocked by R-10 via HRMS-0605, no bypass at this layer

- This story's generation step calls the existing TimesheetRevenueProcessor rather than reimplementing the approval check — R-10 stays enforced in exactly one place.

- Field: Invoice generation endpoint calls HRMS-0605 first; a REJECTED response from it blocks generation entirely

## BR: BR-0907-02 — Draft requires internal Finance approval before Sent

- No invoice reaches a client without a human Finance check, regardless of how clean the underlying timesheet data looks.

- Field: Status transition Draft->Approved requires an explicit Finance-role user action, never automatic

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: An invoice cannot be generated for a period with any unapproved timesheet, verified against R-10.

- AC-2: An invoice cannot transition from Draft to Approved without an explicit Finance-role action.

- AC-3: PDF export accurately reflects the invoice's line items and total.
