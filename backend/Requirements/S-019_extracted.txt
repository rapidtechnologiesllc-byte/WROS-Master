[Normal] 2. WHY THIS EXISTS
[Normal] As a candidate has dozens of message exchanges with Thunder, the full conversation becomes too long to include in every Thunder prompt. A 2-3 sentence summary of what has been collected and what was discussed allows Thunder to stay contextually aware without reading every message from scratch. Summaries also help recruiters instantly understand where a conversation is without reading the full thread.
[Normal] 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING
[Normal] After every set of Thunder exchanges (every 5 messages or after any state transition), generate a concise 2-3 sentence summary of the conversation state. Store in candidate_conversations.summary. Display in the recruiter MESSAGES tab Status Card.

[Normal] 6B. BUSINESS RULES
[Normal] ▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.
[Normal] BR-01: Summary Is Generated After Every 5 Messages OR State Transition
[Normal] Do not generate on every single message — LLM calls have cost and latency. Trigger: (message count for this conversation) % 5 == 0 OR a state transition just occurred.
[Normal] BR-02: Summary Must Be 50–400 Characters
[Normal] Validate LLM output length. Under 50 chars: too vague, regenerate once. Over 400 chars: truncate to 400. If regeneration also fails: keep previous summary and log SUMMARY_GENERATION_FAILED.
[Normal]   ↳ Triggered by field: summary
