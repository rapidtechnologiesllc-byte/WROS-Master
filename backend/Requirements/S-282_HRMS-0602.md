## Why — Business Rationale

- The AI classifier reads demand details and sets: (1) SLA in hours, (2) difficulty score 1-10, (3) recommended sourcing channel. This ensures right effort applied immediately without RM manually assessing each request.

## What — Scope

- Build AdhocClassifier: LLM-powered service producing SLA hours, difficulty score, sourcing recommendation, and plain-language summary.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0601 adhoc demand. Anthropic claude-sonnet-4-6 API access.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build AdhocClassifier service

- AdhocClassifier.classify(demand_id): LLM prompt with demand details. Output JSON: { sla_hours, difficulty_score: 1-10, sourcing_recommendation: BENCH|LINKEDIN|SUBVENDOR|HTD|COMBINED, difficulty_explanation (max 150 chars) }.

- Update demand: adhoc_sla_hours, ai_difficulty_score, ai_sourcing_recommendation, ai_classification_notes.

- Fallback if LLM unavailable: sla_hours=48, difficulty_score=5, sourcing_recommendation=COMBINED.

## Step 2: Expose classification in demand detail

- AI Classification card: SLA, Difficulty, Channel, Explanation. RM can override any field with logged reason.

## Business Rules

## BR: Classification Within 60 Seconds

- AdhocClassifier async call completes within 60 seconds. Fallback applied if exceeded.

- Field: 60-second SLA

## BR: RM Override Logged for Model Training

- Overrides stored with original AI values and 30+ char reason. Fed back for training.

- Field: Override stored for training
