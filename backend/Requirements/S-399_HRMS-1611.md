## Why — Business Rationale

- Partner compensation isn't flat salary — Troy and Curtis both earn revenue-share on top of base pay, Avinash earns 5% of total org revenue, and Troy additionally earns per-new-client and per-first-deployment bonuses. Every one of these depends on real, confirmed revenue — not invoiced, not forecast, but actually paid — which is exactly why this calculator's trigger has to be as precise as you specified: only invoices manually marked paid, nothing else, so an incentive never gets paid out against money that hasn't actually landed.

## What — Scope

- A monthly calculation, timed to complete by the 7th-8th for a 10th-of-month payment, computing each partner's incentive from only that period's manually-confirmed-paid invoices — revenue share percentages, Avinash's org-wide 5%, and Troy's new-client and first-deployment bonuses — as a rolling calculation that correctly catches a late payment whenever it actually clears, not a hard cutoff that could silently drop it.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-1603 must be live — this calculator's entire trigger is invoices marked paid through that story's manual action.

- HRMS-1607 must be live for the org-revenue figure Avinash's share depends on.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the eligible-revenue query

- Every invoice with payment_date (per HRMS-1603's BR-1603-02) falling within the target period, regardless of when the mark-as-paid action itself was performed — a payment received on the 28th but logged on the 3rd still counts toward the period it was actually received in.

## Step 2: Build the revenue-share calculations

- Troy: 1.5% of Axion Core Revenue (paid invoices only, per the eligible-revenue query above).

- Curtis: 1% of PRISM Core Revenue (same basis).

- Avinash: 5% of Total Org Revenue — reads HRMS-1607's org-level rollup for paid-invoice revenue specifically, not the full accrual-basis P&L revenue figure, since incentive eligibility and P&L revenue recognition are deliberately different bases per your instruction.

## Step 3: Build Troy's bonus calculations

- New-Client Bonus: $10K per new client — triggered by a client's first-ever paid invoice, not first invoice sent, matching the same paid-only discipline as everything else in this story.

- Deployment Bonus: $1K per first Core deployment — triggered by the first paid invoice associated with a Core-engine deployment for that client.

## Step 4: Build the rolling-calculation, no-hard-cutoff behavior

- Run this calculation on a schedule targeting completion by the 7th (allowing the 8th as a buffer before the 10th payment date), but any invoice whose payment_date falls in an earlier period and was only just marked paid still gets included in whichever run first sees it — never silently dropped because it missed its 'natural' month's calculation window.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-1611-01 — Only manually-marked-paid invoices are eligible revenue for any incentive calculation

- Matches your explicit instruction exactly — invoiced-but-unpaid revenue, however confidently expected, never contributes to a partner incentive figure.

- Field: Every revenue-share and bonus calculation filters strictly on invoices.status=Paid with a populated payment_date

BR: BR-1611-02 — A late payment is caught by the next run that sees it, never silently dropped

- The rolling-calculation design means an invoice marked paid after its natural period's run has already completed is not lost — it's picked up correctly whenever it does get marked, attributed to its actual payment period.

- Field: Each calculation run queries for any eligible-but-not-yet-calculated paid invoice, not just the current period's invoices

BR: BR-1611-03 — New-client and deployment bonuses trigger on first paid invoice, not first sent invoice

- A bonus for landing a new client should reflect that the client actually paid, not just that BlitzenX invoiced them — matches the platform-wide 'paid, not invoiced' discipline running through this entire epic.

- Field: new_client_bonus and deployment_bonus triggers check for the client/deployment's first Paid-status invoice specifically
