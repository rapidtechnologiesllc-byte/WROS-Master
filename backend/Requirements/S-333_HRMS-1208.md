## Why — Business Rationale

- HRMS-P113 tracks the attribution chain for every candidate — first touch through final placement. This analytics story aggregates that data to answer: where do BlitzenX's best hires actually come from? LinkedIn sourcing produces many candidates but how many become long-term employees vs wash out quickly? Referrals produce fewer candidates but what is their quality and retention?

## What — Scope

- Build source of hire analytics: placement count by source, 90-day retention by source, quality score by source, time-to-hire by source. Comprehensive view of which sourcing investments produce durable placements.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-1201 analytics. HRMS-P113 candidate attribution data in analytics_fact_sourcing.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build GET /api/analytics/source-of-hire

- Returns: { by_source: [{ source, total_sourced, qualified, placed, still_active_90d, quality_score_avg, time_to_hire_avg_days }], first_touch_attribution: [{ source, placements }], last_touch_attribution: [{ source, placements }] }

- still_active_90d: employees from this source still ACTIVE after 90 days (retention proxy)

- quality_score_avg: avg overall_score at time of submission for placed candidates from this source

## Step 2: Build Source of Hire Dashboard

- Route: /admin/analytics/source-of-hire

- Placement funnel by source. Quality score comparison bar chart. 90-day retention rate per source.

## Business Rules

## BR: 90-Day Retention as Quality Proxy

- true quality of hire takes years to measure. 90-day retention (employee still ACTIVE after 90 days from joining) is used as a reasonable short-term proxy for hire quality.

- Field: 90-day retention as quality proxy — labeled as estimate
