## Why — Business Rationale

- The Specialty pool is BlitzenX's proving ground and its bench supply buffer. If Core demand cannibalises Specialty too aggressively — pulling too many Core-Certified employees into direct client work — Specialty loses its buffer and BlitzenX cannot respond to new SI partner requests without bench. The 40-person minimum guard ensures Specialty always has sufficient Core-Certified capacity to absorb new demands and continue developing talent.

## What — Scope

- Build the Specialty pool minimum guard: monitors Core-Certified employees in Specialty at all times. If a Core move would drop the count below 40: alert BU Head with impact analysis. BU Head must log a replacement plan before the move can proceed.

## Before — Prerequisites

- employees table with delivery_engine and core_certified fields. HRMS-0514 Core-Pull detection. HRMS-0533 demand workflow.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build SpecialtyPoolGuard service

- SpecialtyPoolGuard.check(employee_id_being_moved): count = COUNT(employees WHERE delivery_engine=SPECIALITY AND core_certified=TRUE AND status NOT IN ('BUDDY_PROGRAM','EXITED')). If count - 1 < 40: return { pool_size_after_move: count-1, below_minimum: TRUE, gap: 40-(count-1) }.

- Called by: HRMS-0514 Core-Pull Engine before executing, HRMS-0533 Confirmed demand workflow before allocation.

## Step 2: Build BU Head alert and replacement plan gate

- If below_minimum=TRUE: block move. Notify BU Head: 'WARNING: Moving [name] to Core will reduce Core-Certified Specialty pool to [N] — below minimum of 40. You must log a replacement plan before this move can proceed.'

- BU Head must POST /api/specialty-pool/replacement-plan: body { employee_id_moving, replacement_strategy (text, min 100 chars), expected_replacement_date DATE }. Only after this: move proceeds.

- Replacement plan stored in specialty_pool_replacement_plans: employee_id, replacement_strategy, expected_date, logged_by, logged_at.

## Business Rules

## BR: 40 Is the Absolute Minimum — Not a Target

- The minimum of 40 Core-Certified employees in Specialty is a hard floor, not a soft target. No move that would breach this floor can proceed without a logged replacement plan. This applies to Core-Pulls (HRMS-0514), confirmed demand allocations (HRMS-0533), and any manual engine transfers.

- Field: 40 = hard floor. All three move types check this guard.

## BR: Replacement Plan Must Include Expected Date

- A replacement plan without an expected_replacement_date is rejected. 'We will hire someone' is not a plan. The expected date creates accountability and feeds the predictive demand model (HRMS-0532) as a future supply signal.

- Field: expected_replacement_date required in replacement plan
