## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

Every candidate interaction with Thunder — a question asked, a word chosen, an objection raised, a portal page visited — is a signal about what they care about. A candidate who asks 'what is the career growth path at BlitzenX?' is signaling they value growth over salary. A candidate who immediately asks 'what is the salary range?' values compensation first. A candidate who visits the 'About BlitzenX' page three times wants company validation.

The Desire Intelligence Engine continuously reads these behavioral signals, extracts what they reveal about the candidate's desires and fears, and builds a dynamic desire profile that Thunder and HR can use to craft perfectly targeted motivation. This is what separates a talent relationship system from a messaging bot.

The system learns from EVERY candidate interaction — conversation messages across ALL channels, portal navigation behavior, chat questions, objections raised, response speed changes, sentiment patterns, what they clicked, what they lingered on. All of this feeds a continuously updated picture of who this candidate is and what they need to say YES.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build: (1) Signal collection layer — captures behavioral events from all touchpoints. (2) Signal processing pipeline — LLM analyzes signals to extract desire indicators. (3) Desire Profile — a structured, continuously updated model of what this candidate wants, fears, and needs. (4) Learning feedback loop — outcomes (accepted offer, declined, ghosted) feed back to improve signal interpretation.

## Step 1: Define the candidate desire taxonomy (BA must approve this list)

- DESIRE categories that the system tracks:

- CAREER_GROWTH: Does the candidate prioritize learning, advancement, and title progression? Signals: asks about training, growth path, mentorship, career trajectory.

- COMPENSATION: Is salary the primary motivator? Signals: asks about salary early, requests salary range, counter-offers, uses salary-related words in objections.

- STABILITY: Does the candidate want job security and long-term commitment? Signals: asks about company tenure, employee retention, contract length, BlitzenX client stability.

- REMOTE_FLEXIBILITY: Is work location/flexibility critical? Signals: asks about remote work, hybrid, WFH, relocation concerns, mentions family in location objections.

- DOMAIN_INTEREST: Does the candidate have passion for Guidewire / insurance / specific domain? Signals: asks domain-specific questions, mentions domain projects proactively.

- COMPANY_REPUTATION: Does candidate care about BlitzenX brand, clients, culture? Signals: visits About page repeatedly, asks about clients, employee reviews.

- WORK_LIFE_BALANCE: Is balance a priority? Signals: asks about working hours, overtime, travel requirements, mentions burnout or overwork.

- SPEED_OF_DECISION: How urgently is the candidate looking? Signals: response speed, mentions competing offers, asks about start date.

## Step 2: Build candidate_desire_signals table

- CREATE TABLE candidate_desire_signals: id UUID PK, tenant_id UUID, candidate_id UUID FK→candidates.id, signal_source ENUM('CHAT_MESSAGE','WHATSAPP_MESSAGE','EMAIL_MESSAGE','PORTAL_PAGE_VIEW','OBJECTION','QUESTION_ASKED','RESPONSE_SPEED','SENTIMENT','OFFER_QUESTION') NOT NULL, signal_data JSONB NOT NULL (raw signal: message text, page url, response time, sentiment score, etc.), desire_category VARCHAR(100) nullable (filled by processing pipeline), desire_direction ENUM('TOWARDS','AWAY_FROM') nullable (towards=candidate wants this, away_from=candidate fears this), desire_strength DECIMAL(3,2) nullable (0.0-1.0), extracted_insight TEXT nullable (human-readable 1-sentence insight from this signal), processed BOOLEAN default false, created_at TIMESTAMP, processed_at TIMESTAMP nullable

- Index on (tenant_id, candidate_id, created_at DESC)

## Step 3: Build signal collection hooks

- FROM CHAT (HRMS-P116): on every chat.message_sent → INSERT signal: source=CHAT_MESSAGE, signal_data={ message_body, portal_page, hour_of_day }

- FROM CONVERSATIONS (all channels): on every message.received → INSERT signal: source= channel type, signal_data={ message_body, channel, response_time_minutes (time since last outbound) }

- FROM OBJECTIONS (HRMS-0472): on every objection classified → INSERT signal: source=OBJECTION, signal_data={ objection_type, key_concern }. Desire direction=AWAY_FROM for objections.

- FROM PORTAL NAVIGATION (new — see Step 4): on every portal page view → INSERT signal: source=PORTAL_PAGE_VIEW

- FROM SENTIMENT (HRMS-0436): on every sentiment score → INSERT signal: source=SENTIMENT, signal_data={ sentiment, confidence, message_id }

- FROM RESPONSE SPEED: after each inbound message: calculate response_time_minutes since last outbound. INSERT signal: source=RESPONSE_SPEED, signal_data={ minutes, time_of_day }. Fast response = high engagement signal.

## Step 4: Build portal page view tracking

- Add page view event to each portal page: GET /api/portal/track — body: { page: 'home'|'messages'|'profile'|'interview'|'offer'|'documents'|'about' (if About page added), time_on_page_seconds, scroll_depth_pct }

- Called by frontend on page leave (beforeunload) or after 30 seconds on page

- INSERT candidate_desire_signals: source=PORTAL_PAGE_VIEW, signal_data={ page, time_on_page_seconds, scroll_depth_pct }

- Key insight: candidate spending 45 seconds on offer salary section vs 5 seconds = signals compensation desire

## Step 5: Build SignalProcessingJob — LLM extracts desire indicators

- Runs every 30 minutes. Processes all unprocessed signals (processed=false) for all active candidates.

- For each signal: build extraction prompt: 'Analyze this candidate behavior signal from a recruiting context. Signal type: {source}. Signal data: {signal_data}. Candidate context: {summary from memory}. Identify: (1) Which desire category does this signal indicate? Pick ONE from: [CAREER_GROWTH, COMPENSATION, STABILITY, REMOTE_FLEXIBILITY, DOMAIN_INTEREST, COMPANY_REPUTATION, WORK_LIFE_BALANCE, SPEED_OF_DECISION]. (2) Is the candidate moving TOWARDS or AWAY_FROM this desire? (3) Strength 0.0-1.0. (4) One-sentence insight. Return JSON: { desire_category, desire_direction, desire_strength, extracted_insight }'

- UPDATE candidate_desire_signals: desire_category, desire_direction, desire_strength, extracted_insight, processed=true, processed_at=NOW()

- After batch: call updateDesireProfile() for each candidate with new signals (HRMS-P118)

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Signal Collection Is Silent and Non-Intrusive

The candidate never knows their behavioral signals are being analyzed. No notifications, no banners, no consent dialogs beyond the standard privacy policy. The desire intelligence is built silently from natural behavior — exactly how Netflix recommends movies without asking what genre you like.

↳ Triggered by field: Signals collected passively — no candidate-visible indicators

## BR-02: Every Channel Signal Is Collected — No Channel Hierarchy

WhatsApp messages, email messages, portal chat, portal page views, response times — all channels are equal sources of desire signals. A candidate who never messages but visits the portal offer page 4 times is signaling strong compensation interest just as clearly as one who asks about salary in chat.

↳ Triggered by field: All channels: WHATSAPP, EMAIL, PORTAL, CHAT — all feed signals

## BR-03: Objection = AWAY_FROM Signal — Always

Every classified objection (from HRMS-0472) is stored as desire_direction=AWAY_FROM for its category. 'Not relocating' = AWAY_FROM REMOTE_FLEXIBILITY. 'Salary too low' = TOWARDS COMPENSATION (candidate wants more). The objection taxonomy maps directly to desire categories.

↳ Triggered by field: objection_type → desire_category mapping

## BR-04: Fast Response = High Engagement Signal

When a candidate replies to Thunder within 30 minutes, that is a signal of high engagement and likely interest. response_time_minutes < 30 = desire_strength=0.8 towards whatever the conversation was about at that moment. response_time_minutes > 48 hours = low engagement signal.

↳ Triggered by field: response_time_minutes as desire signal
