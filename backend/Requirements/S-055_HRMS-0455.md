## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

After receiving an offer, candidates have questions: 'What does the salary breakdown look like?', 'What is the start date?', 'What are the benefits?', 'Can I negotiate?'. Without an FAQ bot, every question goes to a recruiter — often outside business hours — and delays the candidate's decision. Thunder answers common offer questions 24/7, keeping candidates engaged and informed during the critical decision period.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build the Offer FAQ Bot — Thunder responds to candidate questions about the offer during the OFFER_SENT state. Uses offer details, BlitzenX FAQ knowledge base, and the LLM to generate accurate answers. Escalates to recruiter for questions it cannot answer (salary negotiation, bespoke queries).

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Build BlitzenX FAQ knowledge base

- Store as system_configuration records per tenant: key=FAQ_{topic}, value=answer text

- Topics Lead BA must provide and approve: BENEFITS, JOINING_PROCESS, FIRST_DAY, BACKGROUND_CHECK, PROBATION_PERIOD, LEAVE_POLICY, REMOTE_WORK_POLICY, EQUIPMENT_PROVIDED

- Example: FAQ_BENEFITS = 'BlitzenX offers health insurance, 18 days of paid leave, performance bonus, and learning budget of $500 annually.'

- These are static facts — Thunder reads them, does not generate them. Lead BA controls all FAQ content.

## Step 2: Build answerOfferQuestion() function

- answerOfferQuestion(candidate_id, tenant_id, question_text) → { answer, needs_escalation: bool }

- Load: offer record (role, salary, start date, expiry), all FAQ_ config values for this tenant

- Classify question type: salary-related (salary, pay, compensation, bonus), logistics-related (start date, role, location), policy-related (benefits, leave, equipment), negotiation-related (can I negotiate, lower, higher, counter)

- For negotiation questions: needs_escalation=true. Thunder says: 'I have flagged your question about salary negotiation to our team. A recruiter will be in touch to discuss.'

- For all other questions: build OFFER_FAQ prompt with question + offer details + FAQ knowledge base. Call callLLM(). Generate answer.

- Validate answer: must reference specific offer details (name, role, date) — not generic. Must not make promises beyond what is in the offer or FAQ.

- Return { answer, needs_escalation: false }

## Step 3: Wire FAQ bot to conversation flow

- When HRMS-0433 detects intent=asking_question AND conversation.status=OFFER_SENT AND offer_faq_active=true:

- Call answerOfferQuestion(candidate_id, tenant_id, message_body)

- If needs_escalation=true: call HRMS-0435 escalation flow. Notify recruiter: '[Name] is asking about salary negotiation on their offer.'

- If answer generated: send Thunder's answer via candidate's preferred channel. Store in conversation_messages.

## Step 4: Handle offer acceptance and decline signals during FAQ

- If candidate says 'I accept' or 'I would like to accept' or 'I am happy to join': route to HRMS-0456 Offer Acceptance Tracking even if this comes during FAQ conversation.

- If candidate says 'I decline' or 'I am not interested in joining': route to HRMS-0456 decline flow.

- Intent detection catches these signals — FAQ bot only handles questions, not decisions.

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Negotiation Questions Always Escalate to Recruiter

Thunder never negotiates salary. Any question containing negotiation intent (negotiate, counter, higher, lower salary) immediately escalates to a recruiter. Thunder's response: 'I've flagged this to our recruiting team and someone will be in touch.' Do not attempt to answer negotiation questions.

↳ Triggered by field: question classification = negotiation → escalate

## BR-02: FAQ Answers Must Reference Actual Offer Data

Thunder's answers must use the actual offer details for this specific candidate — not generic statements. 'Your start date is February 1, 2025' not 'your start date will be communicated soon'. If the answer requires specific data not available: escalate to recruiter.

↳ Triggered by field: answer must include specific offer data

## BR-03: FAQ Bot Only Active During OFFER_SENT State

The FAQ bot only responds to questions when offer_faq_active=true AND conversation.status=OFFER_SENT. After offer is accepted or declined: offer_faq_active=false. During qualification or interview stages: questions are handled by HRMS-0425 qualification flow, not the FAQ bot.

↳ Triggered by field: offer_faq_active AND status=OFFER_SENT
