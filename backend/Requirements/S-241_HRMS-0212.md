## Why — Business Rationale

- A Director needs one screen that answers 'how's revenue looking' without stitching together three separate reports — pipeline, won, lost, forecast, and BU breakdown, all in one place, filterable by continent for a business that spans multiple regions.

## What — Scope

- The executive-level revenue dashboard: pipeline value, won, lost, weighted forecast, BU breakdown, and trend charts — multi-currency aware, filterable by continent.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-238 must be live — this dashboard is a consumer of that shared forecast function, per BR-0209-01.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the top-line summary cards

- Total pipeline value, won this period, lost this period, weighted forecast — all calling S-238's shared calculation.

## Step 2: Build the BU breakdown table

- Same figures sliced by BU, respecting HRMS-0107 BU context.

## Step 3: Build the trend charts

- Monthly trend lines for pipeline growth and forecast accuracy over the trailing 12 months.

## Step 4: Build the continent filter

- Filters the entire dashboard view to a specific continent, per HRMS-0121's continent-aware locale model.
