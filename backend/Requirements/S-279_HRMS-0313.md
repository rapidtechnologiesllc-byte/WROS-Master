## Why — Business Rationale

- Approved plans are commitments. Variance tracking holds the recruiting function accountable: did we hire as many people as planned? For which roles? How long did it take? Where did we miss and why? This closes the planning loop — variance data feeds the next plan cycle and the AI model (HRMS-0311).

## What — Scope

- Build plan vs actuals variance tracking: compare approved plan headcount by role vs actual hires YTD. Weekly variance report. Alert on >15% negative deviation.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0303 approved demand plans. HRMS-0708 employee conversions (actual hires). analytics_fact_hiring_funnel.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build PlanVarianceEngine

- PlanVarianceEngine.calculate(bu_id, as_of_date): for each approved plan item: planned_heads = heads_external. actual_hires = COUNT(employees WHERE source=DEMAND_PLAN AND demand_plan_item_id=X AND joining_date <= as_of_date). variance_pct = (actual - planned) / planned * 100. time_to_fill_avg = AVG(joining_date - demand.created_at) for filled demands.

## Step 2: Build variance report and alerts

- GET /api/workforce-planning/variance?bu_id=X&as_of=DATE: returns variance per role.

- Weekly alert: if any role variance < -15%: notify BU Head and Director via portal notification + WhatsApp.

- Variance report UI: table per role + variance % bar chart. Export PDF for weekly staffing review.

## Business Rules

## BR: >15% Negative Variance Triggers Alert Within 24 Hours

- Any role with planned_heads - actual_hires > 15% of planned (i.e. more than 15% behind plan) triggers automatic alert to BU Head and Director. Alert fires once per week — not every calculation cycle. BU Head must log a response (on track / adjusted plan / escalating) within 48 hours of alert.

- Field: >15% variance = BU Head 48h response required
