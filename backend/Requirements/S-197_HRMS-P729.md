## Why — Business Rationale

- A client who thinks BlitzenX's response time is slow, or whose expectations about acceptance rates are unrealistic, is easier to align with actual data than with a phone call. This gives the Client Admin the numbers to see the engagement's real performance for themselves.

## What — Scope

- A Client-Admin-only analytics view: total submissions, acceptance rate, average time from submission to interview, roles filled vs. open, and a rejection-reason breakdown.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-196 must be live — this extends the dashboard's data access pattern.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the metrics calculations

- Total submissions, acceptance_rate = accepted / total, avg_time_to_interview computed from submission and interview timestamps.

## Step 2: Build the rejection breakdown

- Aggregated by S-178's structured rejection reasons (Skills/Experience/Location/Communication/Salary/Role filled/Other), giving the client visibility into their own pattern.

## Step 3: Restrict to Client Admin

- Regular client users see the S-196 overview; this deeper analytics view is Client Admin scoped.
