## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

Beyond the 10 required qualification fields, candidates have preferences that shape which opportunities are right for them: remote vs onsite, domain (e.g. P&C insurance vs healthcare), company size, role type (technical vs management track). Capturing these preferences proactively makes Thunder's matching dramatically better and makes every future communication more personalized.

This is the difference between a transactional recruiter ('tell me your CTC') and a relationship recruiter ('what kind of work environment do you thrive in?').

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build: (1) A set of optional preference questions Thunder asks after the 10 required fields are collected. (2) Preference storage in candidate_memory_facts with category=PREFERENCE. (3) Preference questions asked conversationally, not as a form. (4) Preferences used in candidate-job matching and personalized messaging.

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

▶  Follow these steps in exact order. If a step is unclear, ask the lead BA — never assume.

## Step 1: Define preference question catalog (BA-approved)

- Store as code constants — Lead BA must approve:

- WORK_ENVIRONMENT: 'Once everything is set up, are you looking for fully remote work, a hybrid arrangement, or would you prefer to be in an office environment?'

- DOMAIN_PREFERENCE: 'Do you have a preference for the type of industry or domain you work in? For example, insurance, healthcare, banking, or are you open to any domain?'

- ROLE_TYPE: 'Are you more interested in a hands-on technical role, or are you looking to move into a more leadership or management track?'

- COMPANY_SIZE: 'Do you have a preference for the size of company you join? Larger enterprises or smaller, faster-moving teams?'

- CAREER_GOAL: 'What is your primary career goal for your next role — technical growth, leadership opportunities, stability, or something else?'

## Step 2: Build askPreferenceQuestion() function

- askPreferenceQuestion(candidate_id, tenant_id) → { question, preference_type } or null

- Only called when: conversation.status=QUALIFIED AND all 10 required fields received AND candidate has not been asked all preference questions yet

- Track asked preferences: check candidate_memory_facts for category=PREFERENCE to know which have been asked

- Return the next un-asked preference question in catalog order

- Return null when all 5 preference questions have been asked (or after 2 consecutive non-answers)

## Step 3: Wire preference capture after qualification completes

- In HRMS-0425: when is_complete=true and candidate transitions to QUALIFIED: after the qualification completion message, call askPreferenceQuestion()

- If a question is returned: append preference question to the completion message. 'That's everything I needed! Just one more thing — [preference question]'

- Parse candidate's answer via HRMS-0422 extractFacts() — preference answers will be captured automatically as PREFERENCE category facts

- After each preference answered: call askPreferenceQuestion() again for the next one

- Preferences are optional — if candidate doesn't answer or says 'not sure': call skipField pattern and move to next preference or stop

## Step 4: Use preferences in matching and messaging

- HRMS-0432 Context Builder: include PREFERENCE category facts in candidate context for all Thunder prompts

- HRMS-0509 Bench matching: filter matches by preference when candidates.skills match job.required_skills — secondary filter by remote_preference if job has remote_work field

- HRMS-P302 Personalized value messaging: use preferences to craft targeted BlitzenX pitch

## 6B. BUSINESS RULES

▶  Every rule is non-negotiable. If in doubt, escalate to lead BA — never silently skip a rule.

## BR-01: Preferences Only After All Required Fields Collected

Never ask preference questions until completeness = 100% (all 10 required fields received). Asking 'do you prefer remote work?' before asking for the notice period wastes conversational capital on the wrong questions.

↳ Triggered by field: completeness=100% before preference questions

## BR-02: Preferences Are Optional — Never Block Progression

If candidate doesn't answer a preference question or says 'not sure': record fact_value='Not specified', move on. Do not repeat preference questions. They are nice-to-have context, not required data.

↳ Triggered by field: Preferences are optional capture
