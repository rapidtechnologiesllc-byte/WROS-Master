## Why — Business Rationale

- Once a submission clears compliance and dedup, a human still needs to make the actual judgment call on candidate quality — that's not something to automate away. This screen is where a recruiter spends their time on the part of the job that actually needs their judgment, instead of manually checking employment type or searching for duplicates by hand.

## What — Scope

- A dedicated review queue showing every sub-vendor submission that has cleared the FT-only and dedup gates, with Accept to Pipeline, Reject with Feedback, and Request More Info actions, supporting bulk operations for high-volume vendors.

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-142 and S-143 gates must be live and running before this queue receives any submissions.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the review queue

- List view: vendor name, candidate name, resume link, submission note, skills, experience — filterable by vendor and by request.

## Step 2: Build the three review actions

- Accept to Pipeline: creates the real candidate record via createCandidateSafe(), source-tagged per S-152, moves to S-145.

- Reject with Feedback: requires a feedback note (min 20 chars), visible to the vendor via S-146.

- Request More Info: sends a structured question back to the vendor, submission stays PENDING.

## Step 3: Build bulk actions

- Multi-select submissions from the same vendor/request, apply Accept or Reject to the batch — each individual submission still gets its own createCandidateSafe() call and audit trail, bulk action is a UI convenience, not a shortcut around per-candidate processing.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0808-01 — Accept always goes through createCandidateSafe(), never a direct insert

- Per R-07, this is the only creation path, even for a bulk-accepted batch — each candidate in the batch gets its own createCandidateSafe() call and dedup re-check at creation time.

- Field: Bulk Accept iterates and calls createCandidateSafe() once per candidate, not a batch INSERT

## BR: BR-0808-02 — Rejection always requires feedback, no silent rejects

- A vendor with no visibility into why they're being rejected can't improve their submission quality — feedback is mandatory, not optional.

- Field: Reject action UI blocks submission without a feedback note of at least 20 characters

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: Only submissions that passed S-142 and S-143 gates appear in this queue.

- AC-2: An Accept action creates exactly one candidate record via createCandidateSafe(), verified by audit trail.

- AC-3: A Reject action without a feedback note is blocked by the UI.

- AC-4: A bulk Accept of 5 submissions results in 5 separate createCandidateSafe() calls, not one batch insert.

## Not In Scope

- No AI-assisted candidate quality scoring in this story — recruiter judgment only; if AI ranking is added later it's a separate AI Intelligence Layer story.

- No auto-accept threshold based on vendor scorecard — every submission gets individual human review regardless of vendor history.
