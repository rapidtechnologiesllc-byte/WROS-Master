## Why — Business Rationale

- Manager ratings and KPI scores tell you what the system can measure. Peer trust tells you what the system cannot: would your colleagues trust you to represent BlitzenX with a client? Would they want you on their next engagement? Peer trust is the single most predictive signal for Core readiness because Core work requires someone who can operate independently, handle client pressure, and earn client confidence — and peers see this before any manager scorecard does.

## What — Scope

- Build automated peer pulse survey at Week 6 and Week 12 of Specialty deployment. 5-question survey sent to Tech Lead + 2 random peers (not selected by employee). Results written to HRMS-0515 performance store. Peer trust score is 15% of Core eligibility weighted score.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0515 performance store. HRMS-0522 clock at Week 6 (42 days) and Week 12 (84 days). Employee peers identified from project allocation records.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build survey trigger at Week 6 and Week 12

- Specialty certification clock daily job: when billable_days_count crosses 42 (Week 6) and 84 (Week 12): trigger PeerSurveyDispatcher.

- PeerSurveyDispatcher: identify respondents — (1) Tech Lead on current project. (2) Two randomly selected peers from same project team (NOT selected by the employee, NOT the Buddy Engineer from training). Send survey via WhatsApp + portal.

## Step 2: Build survey questions and response collection

- 5 questions, 1-5 scale: (1) Would you want this person on your next engagement? (2) Do they take ownership without being asked? (3) Do they communicate proactively when there is a problem? (4) Would you trust them to represent BlitzenX directly with a carrier client? (5) [Free text — NLP tagged]: What is their single biggest growth area?

- Anonymous to the employee — respondent names not shown on employee profile. Visible to BU Head and RM only with respondent context.

## Step 3: Build peer trust score calculation

- peer_trust_score = average of questions 1-4 scores across all 3 respondents (Tech Lead + 2 peers). Free text Q5 NLP-tagged and stored as text in performance store.

- Write to HRMS-0515: event_type=PEER_SURVEY, score=peer_trust_score, score_category='peer_trust', notes=NLP_tags.

- Week 6 and Week 12 scores both contribute to AI Assessor's peer_trust input (Week 12 weighted 2x as more recent).

## Business Rules

## BR: Respondents Cannot Be Selected by the Employee

- Peer survey respondents are selected by the system from the project team roster — not by the employee being evaluated. The Tech Lead is always included. The two peer slots are randomly selected from employees on the same project. This prevents employees from selecting only friends who will give them high scores.

- Field: System-selected respondents — employee has no input into selection

## BR: Survey Responses Anonymous to Employee — Visible to Management

- The employee's profile shows aggregate scores and NLP-tagged themes. Respondent names and individual scores are visible to BU Head and RM only. Employee never sees who said what. This protects respondent candour.

- Field: Anonymous to employee. Full detail to BU Head/RM.
