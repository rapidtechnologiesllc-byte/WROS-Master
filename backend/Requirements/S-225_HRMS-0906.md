## Why — Business Rationale

- Planned project revenue and what actually gets billed diverge for mundane reasons — an approved timesheet that never made it onto an invoice, hours logged against a fixed-bid project past its planned budget, partial billing nobody flagged. This story is the time-tracking-specific half of revenue leakage detection — S-243 in Revenue Visibility Engine handles the broader pipeline/demand leakage, this one is scoped specifically to the gap between tracked time and billed time.

## What — Scope

- A comparison engine between planned project revenue and time-tracked actuals, flagging unbilled time and partial billing gaps at the project level.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0905 Timesheet Approval base must be live and producing approved hours.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the planned-vs-actual comparison

- Per project, compare planned_revenue (from the project's role billing rates × planned duration) against actual approved billable hours × rate.

## Step 2: Build the unbilled-time flag

- Approved hours with no corresponding invoice line item past a configurable grace period — this is the same check S-243's unbilled-time leakage type uses; this story owns the underlying detection logic, S-243 surfaces it in the broader leakage list.

## Step 3: Build the partial-billing flag

- A project invoiced for less than its full approved hours in a given period, without an explicit partial-billing reason logged.

## Business Rules

BR: BR-0906-01 — This story's detection logic is the shared source S-243's leakage list reads from

- Avoids two independently-implemented unbilled-time checks drifting apart between the Time Tracking epic and Revenue Visibility Engine.

- Field: S-243's unbilled-time leakage type queries this story's detection output, does not reimplement it

## BR: BR-0906-02 — Partial billing requires a logged reason to avoid a false leakage flag

- Sometimes partial billing is intentional (a client-negotiated cap) — logging the reason distinguishes a real leakage problem from an accepted business decision.

- Field: A partial_billing_reason field, if populated, suppresses the leakage flag for that period
