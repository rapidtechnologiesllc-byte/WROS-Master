## Why — Business Rationale

- The annual demand plan tells you what you need for the year. The 30/60/90 day forecast tells you what you need NOW and SOON. Recruiters and RMs need this short-term view to prioritize: which roles must be filled in the next 30 days? Where is the bench gap most urgent? This drives weekly staffing reviews.

## What — Scope

- Build short-to-medium term hiring forecast: aggregate demand from approved plans + open demands + opportunity pipeline weighted by probability. Produce a role-breakdown forecast for the next 30, 60, and 90 days with gap indicators.

## Before — Prerequisites

- HRMS-0303 approved demand plans. HRMS-0305 opportunity-to-demand conversion. HRMS-0503 bench pool.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build ForecastEngine service

- ForecastEngine.generate(bu_id, as_of_date): combines three demand sources: (1) workforce_demand_plan_items with target_start_month within 90 days. (2) Open demands from demands table (status=OPEN/IN_PROGRESS) with required_by_date within 90 days. (3) Opportunity pipeline demands weighted by close_probability (from HRMS-0305).

- Output: { forecast_30d: [{ role_type, delivery_engine, demand: N, bench_available: N, gap: N }], forecast_60d: [...], forecast_90d: [...] }

- Gap = demand - bench_available. Positive gap = must hire. Zero or negative = bench sufficient.

## Step 2: Build GET /api/workforce-planning/forecast

- Auth: Admin, Director, RM, BU Head. Params: bu_id, as_of_date (default today).

- Returns ForecastEngine output. Cached 4 hours (demand doesn't change minute-to-minute).

- urgency_flag per line item: CRITICAL (gap >0 in 30d), HIGH (gap >0 in 60d), WATCH (gap >0 in 90d).

## Business Rules

## BR: Forecast Includes Weighted Pipeline Demand

- Opportunities with close_probability ≥ 60% are included in the 60d forecast. Opportunities with close_probability ≥ 80% are included in the 30d forecast. Below 60%: included in 90d only. This prevents over-hiring on speculative deals while ensuring high-probability deals are staffed in advance.

- Field: Probability-weighted pipeline inclusion thresholds

## BR: Forecast Refreshes Every 4 Hours — Not Real-Time

- Forecast is computationally expensive (multiple joins across plans, demands, bench, and pipeline). Cached for 4 hours. Every analytics page shows 'Forecast as of [timestamp]'. If RM needs immediate refresh: manual refresh button available.

- Field: 4-hour cache with manual refresh option
