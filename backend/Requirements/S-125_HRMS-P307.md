## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

How well is the nurture program working? What percentage of nurtured candidates eventually become active candidates again? Which nurture touchpoint gets the most responses? What is the opt-out rate? Without these metrics, the program runs blind.

## 3. WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

Build Nurture Analytics dashboard at /recruiter/nurture-analytics. Metrics: enrollment count, response rate per touchpoint, opt-out rate, re-engagement rate (pool → active pipeline), top performing messages.

## BR-01: Re-Engagement Rate Definition

Re-engagement rate = COUNT(pool candidates who started an active hiring conversation) / COUNT(enrolled in nurture) * 100. A pool candidate is 'reengaged' when their conversation.status moves from COMPLETED/POOL to QUALIFYING.

↳ Triggered by field: re-engagement rate definition
