## Why — Business Rationale

- BlitzenX works with multiple sub-vendors, and not all of them are equal — some send strong candidates fast, others send volume with low acceptance rates. Without a scorecard, the choice of which vendor to send the next request to is guesswork. Per-vendor and portfolio-wide analytics were originally two separate stories, but they're the same underlying data sliced two ways — one vendor at a time, or across all vendors — not two different capabilities.

## What — Scope

- A unified analytics view with two modes: a per-vendor scorecard (submission-to-acceptance rate, candidate quality, FT compliance rate, response rate, time-to-first-submission) for prioritizing future requests, and an Admin portfolio view (active vendors, monthly submissions, overall acceptance rate, sub-vendor contribution to filled positions, cost per sub-vendor hire vs. direct).

## Before — Prerequisites

- S-144 and S-147 must be live and accumulating data — this story has nothing to display without their history.

## Implementation Steps

## Step 1: Build the per-vendor scorecard calculations

- submission_to_acceptance_rate = accepted / total submissions.

- ft_compliance_rate = 1 - (violations / total submissions) per S-142/S-147 data.

- response_rate and time_to_first_submission computed from S-147's deadline-tracking data.

## Step 2: Build the Admin portfolio view

- Total active vendors, this month's submissions by vendor, portfolio-wide acceptance rate.

- Sub-vendor contribution to filled positions: % of total placements sourced via SOURCE=SUBVENDOR tag (S-152).

- Cost per sub-vendor hire vs. direct — pulled from finance/revenue data where vendor fee structures are recorded.

## Step 3: Build the vendor comparison view

- Sortable table across all vendors on any scorecard metric, used when deciding which vendors to send a new request to.

## Business Rules

## BR: BR-0812-01 — Scorecard metrics are computed, not manually entered

- Every figure on the scorecard derives from underlying submission/violation/placement records — nothing here is a free-text or manually-maintained field, keeping the scorecard trustworthy.

- Field: All scorecard fields are read-only, calculated from source tables on each view load or scheduled refresh

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1: A vendor's scorecard accurately reflects their actual submission-to-acceptance rate, verifiable by manual recalculation against source data.

- AC-2: The Admin portfolio view's sub-vendor contribution percentage matches an independent count of SOURCE=SUBVENDOR placements.

- AC-3: A stale data source does not break the dashboard — last known values display with a staleness indicator.
