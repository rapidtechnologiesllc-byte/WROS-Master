## Full P&L Components to Track

### 1. REVENUE (Sales Pipeline)
- New deals closed this week
- Deal values by size (small, medium, large)
- Deal velocity (deals/week)
- Revenue recognition per contract terms
- YTD revenue
- Annual target vs pace

### 2. COST OF REVENUE (Delivery Costs)
- Full-time delivery team (salaried)
- Part-time contractors
- Subcontractors / freelancers
- Infrastructure costs (servers, tools, data)
- Direct project delivery costs
- COGS as % of revenue (target: 30%)

### 3. GROSS PROFIT
- Revenue - COGS
- Gross margin % (target: 70%)

### 4. OPERATING EXPENSES
- **Salaries & Benefits**
  - Account Managers
  - Sales Development Reps (SDRs)
  - Partner Operations Manager
  - Customer Success Manager
  - HR/Admin support (allocated)
  
- **Sales & Marketing**
  - Advertising (LinkedIn, Google, etc)
  - Trade shows & events
  - Sales collateral
  - Brand & website
  
- **General & Administrative**
  - Office space & facilities
  - Software licenses & tools
  - Insurance & legal
  - Utilities & internet
  - Travel & meals
  
- **Technology Infrastructure**
  - CRM system
  - Analytics & reporting
  - Collaboration tools
  - Security & compliance

- OpEx as % of revenue (target: 20-25%)

### 5. OPERATING PROFIT
- Gross Profit - Operating Expenses
- Operating margin % (target: 20%)

### 6. OTHER INCOME/EXPENSES
- Interest income
- Other service income
- Interest expense
- Miscellaneous expenses

### 7. NET PROFIT (Bottom Line)
- Operating Profit + Other Income/Expenses
- Net margin % (target: 15-20%)

## Tracking Schedule

**WEEKLY (Every Friday in Partner Report)**
- Top line revenue (deals closed, YTD total, pace vs target)
- Major expense categories (delivery, sales, admin)
- Gross profit and margin %
- Operating profit and margin %
- Net profit and margin %
- Variance to target margins
- Action items if margins are off

**MONTHLY**
- Detailed P&L statement (all line items)
- Expense audit (identify savings opportunities)
- Trend analysis (margins improving or declining?)
- Headcount efficiency (revenue per employee)
- COGS per delivery unit (normalized cost per project)

**QUARTERLY**
- Full P&L review with Partner
- Forecast for rest of year
- Margin improvement plans
- Hiring/staffing needs vs profitability
- Strategic adjustments

**ANNUALLY**
- Full year P&L
- Actual vs Target performance
- Bonus calculations (tied to profit, not just revenue)
- Strategic planning for next year

## Accountability Structure

**Partner is accountable for:**
- Revenue target (top line)
- Profit target (bottom line)
- Gross margin (≥70%)
- Operating expense ratio (≤25% of revenue)
- Net profit margin (≥15%)
- YTD pace to annual goal (≥90% of needed pace)

**If Partner misses targets:**
- Revenue miss: Increase sales activity + close rate coaching
- Gross margin miss: Delivery efficiency review + cost reduction
- Operating expense miss: Headcount efficiency + tool consolidation
- Profit miss: Multi-factor investigation + escalation to CEO

**Partner gets support from:**
- Finance team (P&L tracking, variance analysis)
- CEO (strategic guidance, resource allocation)
- BU Heads (delivery efficiency, cost control)
- HR (headcount planning, compensation optimization)

## Financial Health Scoring

Partner gets a weekly **P&L Health Score** (0-100):

```
Score = (Revenue_Pace_Pct * 0.3) +
        (Gross_Margin_Achievement * 0.2) +
        (OpEx_Achievement * 0.2) +
        (Profit_Achievement * 0.3)

Example:
  Revenue pace: 85% = 25.5 points (0.85 × 30)
  Gross margin: 64% vs 70% = 16 points (91% × 20)
  OpEx ratio: 32% vs 25% = 15 points (78% × 20)
  Profit: $38K on $40K target = 29.25 points (97% × 30)
  ─────────────────────────────
  Total = 85.75/100 🟡 GOOD (target: 85+)
```

**Scoring Guide:**
- 90-100: 🟢 EXCELLENT (all metrics hit)
- 80-89: 🟡 GOOD (most metrics hit, minor variances)
- 70-79: 🟡 NEEDS IMPROVEMENT (multiple variances)
- <70: 🔴 CRITICAL (significant misses, action plan needed)

## No Excuses Principle

Every Friday at 7PM when Partner reports to CEO:

**If P&L Health Score < 80:**
- Partner must present: What went wrong, Why, What's the fix
- Timeline: Fix must be in place by next week
- Escalation: CEO may reassign resources or reduce territory

**If Partner stays below 80 for 2 consecutive weeks:**
- Immediate escalation meeting
- CEO + CFO + Partner accountability review
- Possible remediation: headcount reduction, territory reduction, or role change

**If Partner returns to 85+ for 2 consecutive weeks:**
- Celebration, bonus opportunity, expanded territory if applicable
