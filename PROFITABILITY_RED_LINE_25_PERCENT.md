# The Non-Negotiable: 25% Net Profit Floor

**APPLICATION-WIDE RULE (No Exceptions)**

> If net profit falls below 25%, it is an IMMEDIATE CRITICAL escalation.
> There is no debate. No "we'll fix it next quarter."
> EVERY agent at EVERY level must protect this number.
> It's in their blood. It's how they breathe. Every decision considers profitability.

---

## The 25% Floor Explained

**Why 25%?**
- 25% net profit = $1M profit on $4M revenue
- Allows for: salaries (60%), COGS (20%), OpEx (20%), plus 25% pure profit
- Anything below that means the business model is broken
- Below 15% = company fails within 12 months

**What It Means for Each Level:**

| Level | Target | Red Line | Escalation Trigger |
|-------|--------|----------|-------------------|
| **Tech Lead** | Velocity on target | Low productivity | Manager coaching |
| **Manager** | Team health >75 | Team health <50 | Architect intervention |
| **Architect** | Technical efficiency | Rework/waste | BU Head review |
| **BU Head** | Delivery, utilization, COGS <30% | Any falls <75% | Partner + CEO review |
| **Partner** | Net profit ≥25% | Net profit <25% | **IMMEDIATE CEO + CFO MEETING** |

## How Every Agent Protects the 25% Floor

### Tech Leads Protect Profitability By:
- **Velocity:** Completing more work = higher output per salary
- **Quality:** Writing good code = less rework = lower COGS
- **Efficiency:** Avoiding rabbit holes = predictable velocity
- **Ownership:** "This fix will pay for itself" mentality

### Managers Protect Profitability By:
- **Team Productivity:** High velocity = lower cost per delivery
- **Skill Development:** Senior team = higher rates = higher margins
- **Retention:** Keep experienced people = avoid onboarding cost
- **Coaching:** Fix blockers = maximize billable time

### Architects Protect Profitability By:
- **Technical Debt:** Low debt = fast delivery = high margin
- **Architecture:** Good design = reusable components = lower COGS
- **Efficiency:** Well-built systems = less maintenance = higher profit
- **Scalability:** Leverage past work = high-margin delivery

### BU Heads Protect Profitability By:
- **Delivery Efficiency:** On-time delivery = happy customers = renewal revenue
- **Utilization:** Keep team 75%+ allocated = minimize bench time
- **COGS Control:** Delivery team efficiency = lower unit cost
- **Revenue:** Higher revenue base = spreads OpEx across more dollars

### Partners Protect Profitability By:
- **Deal Selection:** Only take deals with ≥30% gross margin
- **Pricing:** Never discount below 25% net margin floor
- **Delivery:** Execute efficiently = achieve target margins
- **OpEx Control:** Sales team productivity = low cost per deal

### CEO Protects Profitability By:
- **Budget Allocation:** Invest only in high-ROI initiatives
- **Pricing Strategy:** Set floor prices that protect 25%+ margin
- **Market Selection:** Exit/avoid low-margin markets
- **Scale:** Invest in infrastructure that improves margins across organization

## Escalation Protocol

**If Net Profit Margin Drops Below 25%:**

### Hour 0-4 (Friday Evening)
- Finance alerts Partner and CEO
- Partner reviews actual vs budget with finance team
- Root cause identified (COGS too high? OpEx too high? Revenue less than expected?)

### Hour 4-8 (Friday Evening)
- Emergency Partner + CFO + CEO call
- Decision made on immediate actions
- Freeze authorization started

### Hour 8-24 (Friday Midnight - Saturday Morning)
- Communication sent to all BU Heads
- "Profitability emergency" declared
- All discretionary spending frozen
- Partner submits recovery plan

### Hour 24-72 (Saturday-Monday)
- Recovery plan implementation begins
- Daily status checks with CEO
- Priority: Get back to ≥25% immediately

### Day 4+ (Tuesday Onwards)
- If still <25%: Possible role changes or exits
- If recovered to ≥25%: Post-mortem to prevent recurrence

## Examples of 25% Floor Protection in Action

### Example 1: COGS Rising (Delivery Costs Too High)

**Week 1:** Net profit 26% (COGS 31%, should be 30%)

Action: 
- Tech Leads asked to improve velocity 5% (30 min/day time savings)
- Architect reviews for optimization opportunities
- Manager assesses if team is well-matched to project types

**Week 2:** Net profit 25.2% (COGS 30.8%)

Action:
- Continue optimization, watch trend
- If continues rising → replace subcontractor with FTE

**Week 3:** Net profit 26.1% (COGS 30.0%) ✅ STABLE

---

### Example 2: OpEx Too High (Sales Team Waste)

**Week 1:** Net profit 24.5% (OpEx 28%, should be 25%)

Emergency Escalation Triggered:

Actions:
- Review all SDR activity (are they making calls? closing deals?)
- SDR #1: 5 deals/month → keep (revenue generating)
- SDR #2: 0 deals/month → remove immediately (cost with no revenue)
- Hire 1 more high-quality SDR to replace #2

**Week 2:** Net profit 25.2% (OpEx 25.5%)

Continue monitoring, verify SDR quality.

**Week 3:** Net profit 26.0% (OpEx 25%) ✅ STABLE

---

### Example 3: Revenue Collapse (Can't Afford To Keep Operating)

**Week 1:** Net profit 18% (Revenue down 40% due to lost customer)

Emergency Level: SEVERE 🔴🔴

Decision:
- Cut 2 people immediately (saves $10K/month OpEx)
- Partner focuses on sales 100% (no strategic work)
- Contractor spend cut to zero (internal only)
- Target: Get to 25%+ within 2 weeks

**Week 2:** Net profit 21% (improving, but not there yet)

Continue layoff impact, accelerate sales efforts

**Week 3:** Net profit 24.8% (approaching target)

One more week, close 2 more deals

**Week 4:** Net profit 26.2% ✅ RECOVERED

Post-mortem: Diversify customer base, never let one customer = 30% of revenue
