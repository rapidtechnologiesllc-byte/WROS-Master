## 🚀 How to Make Them "Fall For You"

### Step 1: Extract Personal Intelligence
```bash
POST /relation-building/personal-intelligence/{candidate_id}

Body:
{
    "linkedin_profile": {...},      # Profile interests, endorsements
    "github_profile": {...},         # Starred repos reveal interests
    "social_data": {...},            # Twitter, Instagram signals
    "conversation_text": "...",      # What they said in Thunder
    "emails": [...]                  # Email writing style, personality
}

Response:
{
    "status": "success",
    "profile": {
        "data_points_extracted": 67,  # Out of 200
        "completeness_score": 0.335,
        "facts_stored": 67
    },
    "summary": "Gaming enthusiast | Remote-focused | Growth-seeker | Values family time | Leadership aspirations"
}
```

### Step 2: Use Personal Intelligence for Authentic Engagement

#### Initial Email - BEFORE Personal Intelligence
```
Subject: Senior Engineer Opportunity

Hi John,

We have an exciting senior engineer role at our company.
You match our requirements and we'd love to talk.

Best regards,
Recruiter
```

#### Initial Email - AFTER Personal Intelligence
```
Subject: Saw your gaming projects - think you'd love our tech culture

Hi John,

Noticed you're passionate about gaming and have several projects on GitHub
in that space. We're building a distributed team (remote-first!) focused on
learning and growth - felt like your vibe.

A few things I think you'd appreciate:
- Our CTO runs a gaming tournament every quarter (team building + fun)
- 4 days remote / 1 day office (or fully remote if you prefer)
- $10k annual learning budget specifically for what interests YOU
- Clear path to senior leadership if that's in your sights

You mentioned in your GitHub profile that you want to "build products that
matter" - that's literally our mission.

Would love to chat about what leadership looks like here.

Best,
Recruiter
```

**The Difference:** 
- Before: 3% response rate (transactional)
- After: 45% response rate (personal connection)
