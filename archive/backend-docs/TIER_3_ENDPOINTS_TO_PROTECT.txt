TIER 3: CRITICAL ENDPOINTS NEEDING PERMISSION PROTECTION

JOBS ENDPOINTS (jobs.edit permission):
- POST /jobs (create job)
- PUT /jobs/{job_id} (edit job)
- DELETE /jobs/{job_id} (delete job)
- POST /jobs/assign (assign candidate to job)
- File: app/api/v1/endpoints/jobs/crud.py

INTERVIEWS ENDPOINTS (interviews.edit permission):
- POST /interviews (schedule interview)
- PUT /interviews/{interview_id} (update interview)

- PUT /interviews/{interview_id}/decision (make hiring decision)
- File: app/api/v1/endpoints/interviews.py
